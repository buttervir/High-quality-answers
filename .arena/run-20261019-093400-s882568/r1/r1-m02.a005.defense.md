ATTACK 1: CONCEDE. GST depends on gross turnover, not margin, and my own Phase 3 revenue (₹2.1 lakh a month, about ₹25 lakh a year) is over the ₹20 lakh limit. Private coaching is taxable at 18%. Fixed: Phase 3 is now modelled with GST in two cases. In the safe case the client pays a flat ₹2,000 and the business absorbs the GST, which needs about 145 tutor-hours (10 tutors). In the upside case GST is added on top of the price, which needs 100 hours. I also added a rule to watch turnover from Month 18, when it is about ₹17 lakh a year.
ATTACK 2: CONCEDE. The s.64(1A) proviso clearly covers my own tutoring. It may not cover a margin earned on other tutors' hours while I am under 18. Fixed: I now flag this as an open risk, and it is the first question for the CA. Until the CA confirms otherwise, 39% of all Phase 2 margin earned before 18 goes into a tax reserve. That is the worst case of the parent's top rate: 30% + 25% surcharge + 4% cess. The "likely nil" claim now applies only to my own tutoring income and to income earned after 18.
ATTACK 3: CONCEDE. Recruiting DP2 students, who are minors, breaks my own F1 reasoning. Fixed: tutors are recruited only if they are 18 or over, and I removed DP2 students from the recruiting pool.
ATTACK 4: CONCEDE. Writing "the child-safety rules" without saying what they are was not exact enough. Fixed: there is now a written safeguarding section with these rules:
- Agency tutors teach online only, with sessions recorded and parent consent given.
- Home visits happen only with an adult at home, for students of every age.
- Every tutor gives ID, 2 references and a police clearance/character certificate before starting.
- There is a no-private-contact rule.
- There is an incident procedure that includes the POCSO s.19 duty to report.
ATTACK 5: CONCEDE. Earning ₹40,000 would break my own 20-hour cap, and 8 students sharing 20 hours means less than one session a week each. Fixed: the headline is now ₹25,000–30,000. The Phase 1 goal is 5 weekly students (about 20 hours a month) plus a waitlist of 3, and the stop rules have been rescaled to match.
ATTACK 6: CONCEDE. 110 hours × ₹900 = ₹99,000, not ₹1 lakh or more, and 4 hours a week was too little time to run the agency. Fixed: the hour targets are recalculated with GST so that they reach at least ₹1,00,000. Quality checks now mean skimming 15 minutes of one recording per tutor per month, plus a monthly parent feedback form. Billing is per prepaid package, not per session. The DP2 time budget goes up to 5 hours a week, and that time is listed by task.
