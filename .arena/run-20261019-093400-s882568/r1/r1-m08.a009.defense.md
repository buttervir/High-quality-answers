ATTACK 1: CONCEDE. Three full tests do not fit in 48 hours. Fixed: Stage 1 now has an hour budget per test that adds up to exactly 48 h (A 24 h, B 18 h, C 6 h or 0), Test B is cut to a 5-hour "Starter" package, sessions are online or walking-distance only (no cross-suburb travel), and there is a week-5 cut rule that moves hours from a test with no interest to the leading one.

ATTACK 2: CONCEDE. The ₹1 lakh figure had no maths and two deadlines. Fixed: Stage 3 now shows a line-by-line P&L for both routes (tutoring: 120 one-to-one hours + 25 group hours, 6 tutors, manager, costs = ₹1,00,000 net; digital: 10 retainers less delivery and manager = ₹1,00,000). It is labelled a stretch goal with a stated base case (₹50,000–60,000), and one deadline (December of DP2) is used everywhere.

ATTACK 3: CONCEDE. Part 5 missed the Ministry of Education's January 2024 coaching-centre guidelines (no under-16 enrolment, registration, applies to centres with more than 50 students). Fixed: a compliance section with hard rules (under 50 enrolled students, no premises for under-16 coaching, any Stage 4 centre serves 16+ only and registers), the Stage 3 P&L shown to need about 39 students, and a check on Maharashtra's current adoption added to the CA list.

ATTACK 4: CONCEDE. The DP1–DP2 summer is EE, IA and university-application season. Fixed: summer business hours cut to 12–15 a week, scheduled around a fixed daily EE block, school calendar collected in Step 0, the manager hire moved to the end of Stage 2, and a written fallback naming the two must-do summer tasks if hours fall short.

ATTACK 5: CONCEDE. IB predicted grades are not monthly. Fixed: the grade rule now uses a monthly measure the student can check (marks on returned tests and assessments, converted to the 1–7 scale, against a baseline from the first term report), plus the end-of-DP1 predicted grades as a second, hard check.

ATTACK 6: CONCEDE. Profit was pre-tax and the 18th-birthday handover was one phrase. Fixed: an after-tax line (roughly 35–39% in a high-earning parent's hands before 18, versus close to zero on the first ₹12 lakh a year in the student's own name after 18 under the current new regime, CA to confirm), a note that the 50/30/20 split is a choice, not a cut to job-replacing income, and step-by-step handover for both the company route and the proprietorship route.

ATTACK 7: CONCEDE. Under the Maharashtra Shops and Establishments Act, 2017, an establishment with fewer than 10 workers still files an online intimation (Form F) after it starts. Fixed: Part 5 now tells the parent-owner to file it, free and online, once tutors or a manager are engaged, and to have the CA confirm it applies.
