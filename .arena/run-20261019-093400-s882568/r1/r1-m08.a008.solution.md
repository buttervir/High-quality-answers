# A Business Plan for a 16-Year-Old DP1 Student in Mumbai

## 1. The answer in one paragraph

Start an **IB-specialist tutoring and exam-bootcamp business for Mumbai's international-school students** (IGCSE / MYP / early DP). Begin by tutoring yourself. Then grow it into a small agency of vetted tutors (IB alumni aged 18 and over), with paid group bootcamps timed to exam season. Until you are 18, run it legally through a **sole proprietorship in one parent's name**, because a minor cannot sign enforceable contracts in India. Your own tutoring income goes into **your own PAN and bank account**. Cap family capital at **exactly ₹5 lakh (about US$5,700, under 0.1% of net worth) until you turn 18**, and release it in tranches only when the business hits written targets.

There are two honest targets, kept separate:
- **Business profit ≥ ₹1 lakh/month (Level 1).** The base case reaches this by about **Q3 2027**, after 18% GST and a paid coordinator. The conservative case does **not** reach it while you are at school; it becomes a post-exam target from June 2028.
- **Your own personal income before 18** is about **₹40–45k/month** in the base case (your own tutoring plus a fixed founder fee). The rest of the profit legally belongs to your parent and is held for transfer to **your company**, which is incorporated before you leave for university, with you holding 75%.

Your IB grades act as a hard brake on all of this.

## 2. Assumptions (where the request is ambiguous)

1. **"Job replacing money"** means recurring **net profit** (after GST, costs and staff, before income tax) of:
   - **Level 1: ₹1,00,000/month** (₹12 lakh/year). That is above typical entry-level graduate pay in India.
   - **Level 2: ₹3,00,000/month** (₹36 lakh/year). That is closer to a well-paid Mumbai professional.

   Level 1 is measured on the **business** (everything you founded and run). Your **personal** cash is reported separately (Section 8.3), because before age 18 most of it is legally your parent's.
2. You started DP1 in mid-2026. IB final exams are **May 2028**, results come in early July 2028, and university applications run from mid-2027 to early 2028.
3. You turn 18 sometime **between late 2027 and late 2028**. The plan works for **any** birthday in that range (Phase 5).
4. Your family supports the plan, with a parent acting as legal operator. You are **not** being asked to manage family wealth.
5. Exchange rate: **₹88 ≈ US$1**. All Mumbai prices are **estimates that you verify in Phase 1** against at least 10 real parent quotes.
6. This is not legal or tax advice. Every legal or tax step is marked for confirmation with a Chartered Accountant (CA) or a Company Secretary (CS). Budget about ₹30–40k/year once GST registration is in place.

## 3. The hard constraints (they drive everything)

| # | Constraint | Exact implication |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Time.** DP1 involves 6 subjects, plus the Extended Essay (4,000 words), TOK essay and exhibition, an Internal Assessment in each subject, and CAS. | Real spare capacity is about **6–8 hrs/week in term**, **≤2 hrs/week in school exam weeks and IA-deadline weeks**, and less again in DP2. **This is the tightest constraint.** Every schedule below is budgeted hour by hour to fit it. When any instruction conflicts with the cap, **the cap wins**. |
| C2 | **Legal capacity.** A minor's contract is void (Indian Contract Act 1872, s.11; *Mohori Bibee v Dharmodas Ghose*, 1903). A minor cannot be a company director (a DIN requires majority). | An adult must hold the contracts until you are 18. A minor **can**, however, hold fully paid-up shares through a guardian (confirm with a CS). The plan uses that in Phase 5. |

| C4 | **Income-tax clubbing.** A minor's income is added to the parent's income, **except** income from manual work or from "skill, talent, specialised knowledge and experience". This was s.64(1A) of the 1961 Act, and the Income-tax Act 2025 (in force from 1 April 2026) carries the rule over; confirm the section number with your CA. | Fees you personally earn by tutoring stay yours and are taxed on your own return. Under the new regime, the rebate makes income up to ₹12 lakh effectively nil-tax. **Profit from other tutors' work is the parent's business income**, taxed at the parent's slab rate. |
| C5 | **GST.** Registration is mandatory once **aggregate turnover in a financial year (1 April–31 March)** exceeds **₹20 lakh** per PAN for services in Maharashtra. You must apply within 30 days of becoming liable. Private coaching is taxed at **18%**. | The proprietorship must sit under a parent's PAN **with no other business turnover**. Monitor **FY-to-date** turnover, not a trailing 12 months. **Set prices GST-inclusive from day one**, so registration never forces a price change on customers (Section 8.2). |
| C6 | **Capital is not a constraint.** The family has ~US$8M (~₹70 crore). | The risk is **overspending**. Money cannot buy time, experience or customer evidence, so capital is released only against evidence. |
| C7 | **IB academic integrity.** | Never write, substantially edit or "polish" anyone's IA, EE or TOK work. Never sell IB past papers or markschemes, which are IBO copyright. Breaking either rule could cost **your own diploma**. |
| C8 | **Child safety and data.** Your customers are minors. The DPDP Act 2023 and its 2025 Rules (phasing in through 2027) require verifiable parental consent to process children's data. POCSO applies. | The parent is always the paying, consenting customer. You need a written safeguarding code, sessions recorded or held in a common area, and minimal data collection. |
| C9 | **University abroad.** On a US F-1 or UK Student visa you generally **cannot actively work** for your business. Passive ownership is usually fine. Going abroad to study also typically makes you a **non-resident under FEMA**. | Before departure (about Aug–Sept 2028), the business must run **without you**: a GM, documented systems, and a company already incorporated. Confirm with an immigration adviser and a CA. |
| C10 | **Location: Mumbai.** | There is a dense cluster of IB and Cambridge schools (Bandra–Juhu–Andheri, Powai, Lower Parel–Worli, BKC, Malabar Hill) and a large paid-tuition culture. Commute times make **online-first** delivery the only way to fit C1. |

**Design rule from C1 and C2:** choose a business that (a) produces cash with under 8 hrs/week of your time, (b) needs no contract signed by you, (c) uses an advantage only a current IB student has, and (d) can be handed to a manager by mid-2028.

## 5. The business, exactly

**Working name:** something neutral, such as "[YourName] Academic Studio" or "Bridge to IB". **Do not** use your school's name or the IB logo: "International Baccalaureate" and "IB" are IBO trademarks. Describe the service in words ("tutoring for IB Diploma students") without implying endorsement.

**Customers:** parents of students at Mumbai IB, IGCSE and international schools.
1. **Grade 9–10 (IGCSE/MYP):** subject tutoring and "DP readiness".
2. **DP1 students a year behind you**, and DP2 students only in subjects where you are clearly ahead. Otherwise use an alumni tutor.
3. **Exam seasons:** IB mocks (Jan–Feb), IGCSE (May–June), MYP eAssessment (May), IB finals (May).

**Products and prices.** All proprietorship prices are **GST-inclusive** from launch. Before registration the proprietorship keeps the whole price. After registration, 18/118 of it goes to GST and customers see no change.

| Product | Delivered by | Price to parent (incl. GST) | Net of 18% GST | Paid to tutor | Margin after registration |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1:1 tutoring, you personally (subjects where your school grade is 6–7) | You, on **your own PAN** (no GST at your turnover) | ₹1,400/hr | ₹1,400 | n/a | ~100% (your skill income) |
| 1:1 tutoring, alumni tutor (IB graduate, 18+, scored 40+ or a 7 in the subject) | Tutor via proprietorship | ₹2,100/hr | ₹1,780 | ₹1,200/hr | **₹580/hr** |
| Exam bootcamp (6–8 students, 8 sessions × 2 hrs = 16 hrs) | Tutor alone | ₹16,000/student | ₹13,559 | ₹2,000/hr × 16 = ₹32,000 | 8 × 13,559 − 32,000 = **₹76,475/cohort** |
| "DP Readiness" summer course (Grade 10 → DP1: subject choice, IA/EE/TOK orientation, study systems), 10 students | Tutor, with you teaching 2 sessions in holidays | ₹21,000/student | ₹17,797 | ₹40,000/cohort | 10 × 17,797 − 40,000 = **₹1,37,966/cohort** |

**Price rule:** collect at least 10 real parent quotes in Phase 1, and keep alumni-tutor prices **15–30% below established adult IB tutors**. If adult tutors are at ₹2,500–3,000/hr, then ₹2,100 is 16–30% below. If the quotes show adult tutors at or below ₹2,400, drop to ₹1,800 inclusive and tutor pay to ₹1,100, which gives a net margin of ₹425/hr.

**Sensitivity:** every ₹100 of net margin per tutor-hour changes base-case monthly profit by ₹18,200 (182 hrs).

**Never sell:** IA, EE or TOK writing or editing; grade promises; IB past papers. Your own notes, and explanations of the criteria in your own words, are fine.

**Money flow (keep it exactly like this):**
- **Your own tutees** pay **you** by UPI into your account. This is skill income on your PAN, and you file your own ITR (a guardian signs while you are a minor). Keep your own tutoring turnover far below ₹20 lakh (it will be about ₹2 lakh/year).
- **Alumni-tutor and bootcamp customers** pay the **proprietorship**. The proprietorship pays tutors against their invoices and keeps the margin, which is your parent's taxable business income.
- **Founder fee.** The proprietorship pays you a fixed, documented fee for your management work: **₹10,000/month from Gate 1, ₹25,000/month from Gate 2.** For about 6 hrs/week that works out to roughly ₹960/hr, below your own tutoring rate, so it is defensible as an arm's-length related-party payment. Your CA confirms whether it is your skill income or is clubbed with the parent's income; either way, the tax is small at this level.
- **Retained profit** stays in the proprietorship's account, is recorded monthly in the board sheet, and moves to your company under the Memorandum (Phase 5).

## 7. The plan, week by week

**Hours rule (precedence over everything below):** at most **8 hrs/week in term**, **2 hrs/week in school exam and IA-deadline weeks**, and **15 hrs/week in holidays**. Log your hours every Sunday. In a 2-hour week, an alumni tutor covers your own students (you are paid nothing for those sessions; the proprietorship bills them), and you spend your 2 hours only on the Monday coordination call and urgent parent messages. If you go over the cap two weeks in a row, cut scope, not sleep.

### Phase 0: Set up (26 Oct – 8 Nov 2026, Diwali break, ~6 hrs/week)
1. Complete Section 6 steps 1–7.
2. Build a **Carrd** page (about US$19/year) or a Google Site with: who you are, subjects, prices, a booking and parental-consent Google Form, and your safeguarding rules.
3. Set up **WhatsApp Business**. Every student chat is a group that includes the parent.
4. Write the 1-page **Safeguarding & Integrity Code**: sessions online only, in a common area or recorded with consent; no private chats; no assessed-work writing; incidents reported to the parent operator.
5. Pick **your 2 strongest subjects** (e.g. Maths, Physics or Economics).
6. **Academic baseline:** record your school-reported 1–7 grades from your first DP1 tests. Replace them with the **first DP1 term report** (Nov–Dec 2026) as soon as it arrives. If your school issues official predicted grades, use those instead.
7. **Tranche 0 released: ₹25,000.**

### Phase 1: Validate (9 Nov 2026 – 31 Jan 2027, ~7.5 hrs/week)

| Weekly block | Hours |
|---|---|
| Own tutoring: Tue and Thu evenings, 1 × 1.5 hrs each, plus Saturday 2 hrs | 5.0 |
| Sales and admin: parent calls, tutor screening (Saturday) | 2.0 |
| Sunday review and hours log | 0.5 |
| **Total** | **7.5** |

1. **Weeks 1–2: 20 parent conversations** from your family's network, using 5 questions:
   1. Does your child have a tutor now, for which subject?
   2. How much do you pay per hour, and how often?
   3. What frustrates you about the current tutor?
   4. Would you try a session with me at ₹[price], first one free if it isn't useful?
   5. Who else should I talk to?

   **Output:** at least 10 price quotes and at least 5 trial bookings. These 20 calls happen in the 2-hour sales block across 2–3 weeks; you tutor fewer hours until trials convert.
2. **Weeks 2–6: tutor 3–4 students** within your 5 hours. Send a 3-line WhatsApp report after every session.
3. **Weeks 4–8: recruit 3 alumni tutors** (school alumni groups, seniors from 1–2 years ahead, LinkedIn). Screen each one:
   - IB transcript (40+, or a 7 in the subject)
   - a 20-minute demo lesson
   - 2 references and an ID check
   - signing the Code and a **tutor agreement with the proprietorship**
4. **Weeks 6–12:** send overflow demand to tutors through the proprietorship at ₹2,100 (tutor paid ₹1,200).
5. Sell the first **mock-season bootcamp** in January, delivered by a tutor in Feb–March.

**Gate 1 (31 Jan 2027).** All must be true to release Tranche 1 (₹1,00,000) and start the ₹10,000 founder fee:
- At least 8 paying students across both money flows
- At least 70% of Nov–Dec starters still active in January
- At least ₹60,000 cumulative revenue
- At least 2 alumni tutors with paying students
- **School grades held:** no subject down 1+ grade and total not down 2+ against the baseline (non-negotiable)
- **A GST projection:** your CA estimates FY 2027-28 proprietorship turnover. If it exceeds ₹20 lakh, prepare the GST application now.

If Gate 1 fails, run **one** 6-week fix cycle on the failing metric. If it fails again, see Section 10.

### Phase 2: Exam-season scale (1 Feb – 31 May 2027, ~6.75 hrs/week in normal weeks)

| Weekly block | Hours |
|---|---|
| Own tutoring | 3.0 |
| Quality: review 30 minutes of one recorded session per tutor per month (6 tutors), which is ~0.75 hr/week | 0.75 |
| Sales calls and tutor coordination | 2.0 |
| Finance and board sheet | 1.0 |
| **Total** | **6.75** |

You **do not** assist bootcamps; a tutor delivers them alone. During DP1 school exam weeks, the 2-hour rule applies.

1. Grow to **6 alumni tutors**: Maths AA/AI, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Economics, English.
2. Run **3–4 bootcamps** online on weekend mornings (e.g. "IB Maths Mock Rescue", "IGCSE Physics Final Sprint").
3. Marketing, ₹10,000/month at most:
   - Meta ads aimed at parents aged 35–55 near Bandra, Juhu, Powai, Worli and Lower Parel
   - a referral offer (1 free hour per referred family that books 4+ hours)
   - parent WhatsApp groups, with the admin's permission only
4. **CAS:** a linked outreach arm (classmates writing free study guides for a low-income school) can be your CAS project. Confirm with your CAS coordinator.
5. **GST:** check FY-to-date turnover (from 1 April 2027) each month. **Apply when it reaches ₹17 lakh**, which leaves room inside the 30-day window. In the base case that is about July 2027. Prices do not change.

**Gate 2 (31 May 2027)** releases Tranche 2 (₹2,00,000) and raises the founder fee to ₹25,000. All must be true:
- Revenue run-rate at least ₹1.5 lakh/month (Mar–May average)
- Business profit at least ₹40,000/month before coordinator and founder fee
- No upheld safeguarding or integrity complaint
- School grades held against the baseline

### Phase 3: Summer machine (June – July 2027, ≤15 hrs/week; EE before business)
1. Run **2–3 DP Readiness cohorts** (₹1,37,966 margin each). You teach 2 sessions per cohort, and tutors teach the rest.
2. **Hire a part-time operations coordinator:** 18+, 15 hrs/week, ₹20–25k/month, contracted by the proprietorship. They take over scheduling, payment follow-ups, parent reports and tutor timesheets, all written into the "playbook" Google Doc.
3. Mornings go to the EE draft and university shortlist; afternoons go to the business.

### Phase 4: DP2, hold not grow (Aug 2027 – May 2028, ≤4 hrs/week; 0–1 hr/week from 15 Feb 2028 to your last paper)
1. **No new products, and no growth that costs you time.** The one growth lever allowed is that the coordinator may onboard up to 3 more tutors to fill a waitlist. Your part is a 20-minute final interview per tutor, inside your 4 hours.
2. Your week: Monday coordination call (30 min), one recording review (30 min), one sales call (30 min), numbers (60 min). That is 2.5 hrs, leaving buffer.
3. **From 15 Feb 2028:** monthly review only. Pre-sell the summer 2028 DP Readiness cohorts in January.
4. **University applications:** report real numbers (students served, tutors managed, revenue band), supported by a CA certificate.

### Phase 5: Company, handover and age 18 (start by **March 2028**, so that it works for every birthday)
1. **Incorporate a private limited company before you leave India**, whatever your age.
   - Directors are both parents. At least one must be resident in India for 182+ days in the year (Companies Act s.149(3)).
   - Shareholders: **you, at 75%, holding fully paid-up shares through your guardian**, and a parent at 25%.
   - Fund the share capital with a gift from a parent. Gifts from a "relative" are tax-exempt, and your CA settles any clubbing of income on the gifted capital.
   - SPICe+ on the MCA portal costs about ₹10–25k all-in. Annual compliance, including the mandatory audit, is about ₹40–80k.
   - A CS confirms that minor shareholding is permitted and how it is recorded.
2. **Move the business into the company** (April–June 2028): assign the brand, domain, content, customer relationships and tutor agreements, with fresh tutor contracts signed by the company. **Note:** a tax-neutral proprietorship-to-company conversion (s.47(xiv) of the 1961 Act; check its successor in the 2025 Act) requires the proprietor to keep at least 50% of the votes for 5 years. That conflicts with your 75%. Expect your CA to recommend a valued asset transfer or a fresh start in the company instead.
3. **Hire a General Manager** by June 2028, contracted **by the company**, which signs through your parent-directors. Pay ₹40–60k/month plus 10% of operating profit. This works even if you are still 17.
4. **On your 18th birthday** (in India or abroad):
   - get a DIN, and the board appoints you **director** (DIR-12)
   - your shares are re-recorded in your own name
   - if you are abroad, you are likely a **FEMA non-resident**: your shares continue to be held on a non-repatriation basis, and you act only as a non-executive board member to stay within C9
   - an immigration adviser and your CA confirm both points for your country
5. **If you stay in India** (Ashoka, FLAME, an IIT or a gap year), work towards **Level 2 (₹3 lakh/month)** by:
   - a second city online (Bengaluru, Delhi NCR, Pune)
   - recorded courses at ₹3–8k each
   - an admissions arm staffed by experienced adult counsellors only

## 10. Kill, pivot and pause criteria (pre-committed)

- **Pause for 4 weeks** (a tutor covers your students, and you do no business work) if any subject's school-reported grade falls by 1+ from the baseline, or your total falls by 2+. Restart only once it has recovered.
- **Pivot** if Gate 1 fails twice, in this order:
  1. a different segment (Grade 9–10 DP Readiness only)
  2. a different channel (IB specialist for an existing tutoring centre, paid per head)
  3. a different product (written guides and recorded courses)
- **Stop permanently** on any substantiated safeguarding incident or any tutor writing assessed IB work. Refund the affected families in writing.
- **Do not chase Level 2 before June 2028.**
