# From IB DP1 to Job-Replacing Income: An Exact Plan for a 16-Year-Old in Mumbai

## Assumptions

- **Today is 19 October 2026.** The student started DP1 in mid-2026, so IB final exams fall in **May 2028**. The student turns 18 around mid-2028 and 21 around mid-2031. If the birthday is earlier or later, move the steps marked "at 18" to match.
- **"Job-replacing money"** means money the student can legally call their own and live on instead of a salary, **after tax**. There is one milestone and two targets:
  - **Milestone M (31 May 2028, end of IB):** the business makes **₹80,000+ a month pre-tax** for March–May 2028. Before 18, most of this is legally a family adult's income (Section 6.1), so it is a business milestone, not yet "your money".
  - **Target 1:** **₹1,00,000 a month after tax, in the student's own name**, for 3 months in a row. **Due Oct–Dec 2028** (Path I below).
  - **Target 2:** **₹2,50,000 a month after tax** (₹30 lakh a year, roughly a top-tier fresher package), with the student working 15 hours a week or less. **Due FY2030-31, by 30 June 2031** (Path I).
  - **Path I** = university in India, so the student can keep working. **Path A** = university abroad on a UK/US student visa, which in practice means the student cannot work in the business (Section 6.6). Path A has its own, lower numbers. **The university choice is the biggest single driver of this plan's income, so it's shown openly, not hidden.**
- **Currency:** ₹88 = US$1, so the family's US$8M is about ₹70 crore.
- **School comes first.** The IB Diploma decides university options, and it can't be redone cheaply. Business-time caps (all work included, travel too): **8 hours a week in DP1 term time, 15 in summer 2027 after the EE, 6 from Sept–Dec 2027, 4 from Jan–May 2028.**
- **Indian law and tax** are stated as I understand them for late 2026: new-regime slabs as set by Budget 2025 (0–4L nil, 4–8L 5%, 8–12L 10%, 12–16L 15%, 16–20L 20%, 20–24L 25%, above 24L 30%, plus 4% cess, with a rebate making resident income up to ₹12 lakh tax-free), domestic company rate 25.17% all-in under the 22% regime, and a GST threshold of ₹20 lakh for services in Maharashtra. Items marked **[CA]** or **[Lawyer]** must be confirmed by a professional before you act. The exact questions are in Section 7.

## PASS 1: The answer in one paragraph

Don't spend family money to "buy" a business. At 16 your real assets are what you know, the network you live in, and a safety net that lets you take risks, not the ₹70 crore. Start a **skill-based academic coaching business**: online tutoring and exam coaching for MYP and IGCSE students. It needs under ₹15,000, fits inside 8 hours a week, and its income legally counts as your own. Then turn it into a **tutor-matching agency** using IB graduates as tutors. Parents pay the tutor ₹1,800–3,000 an hour **directly**. The tutor pays the agency a one-third coordination fee. Because only that fee counts as the agency's turnover, GST registration is not needed until about 2029. Until you turn 18, a family adult holds the agency layer, because a minor can't contract. That adult pays you an arm's-length hourly fee for your work. Validate it in November–December 2026. Fill 5 weekly slots by March 2027. Hire tutors from April 2027. Hand operations to a coordinator by September 2027. Freeze growth for IB exams from January to May 2028. That gets you to **Milestone M**: about ₹81,000 a month pre-tax. At 18 the adult gifts you the business, and with a full-time summer it reaches **Target 1: ₹1.06 lakh a month after tax** by late 2028. If you study in India, it reaches **Target 2: ₹2.5 lakh a month after tax** in FY2030-31, on about ₹1.85 crore of bookings with a GM. If you study abroad, you can only be a passive owner. It then pays roughly ₹35–45k a month, and the targets move to your post-graduation years. Every stage has a numerical **kill/pivot test**, and there is a costed **fallback business** (Track B) if the first test fails.

The rest of this document works backwards from the end state, then gives the forward steps, then goes deep on the parts most likely to break.

## PASS 3 (part 1): The forward plan, week by week and month by month

### Phase 0: Setup, 19 October – 15 November 2026 (about 6 hours a week)

**Week of 19 October**
1. **Venture Charter (1 hour, written).** Write and sign one page with your parents covering:
   - (a) **The IB-first rule.** If the term predicted total drops 3+ points below your DP1 baseline, business hours halve until it recovers.
   - (b) **The capital rule.** Parents fund up to ₹50,000 as a documented interest-free loan, released in tranches only when gates are passed. The lifetime family risk budget to age 18 is **₹5 lakh (about 0.07% of net worth)**.
   - (c) **The DAH.** Name the Designated Adult Holder provisionally (final after the CA meeting). The DAH is the legal owner and contracting party of the agency layer until you turn 18. They pay you ₹1,000 for each logged hour of agency work. They commit in writing to gift the business to you in the month you turn 18 (the gift deed itself is signed at that point).
2. **Documents.**
   - **PAN.** A minor can hold one. Apply via NSDL/UTIITSL with a parent as representative if you don't have one.
   - **Aadhaar.**
   - **Savings account in your name.** Under RBI's April 2025 directions, banks may let minors over 10 operate savings accounts independently, within limits the bank sets. Ask your bank for the exact UPI and net-banking limits.
3. **School policy check.** Email your IB coordinator. Ask whether paid tutoring is allowed, and whether you may tutor students from your own school. If it's not allowed, serve other schools and online clients only.
4. **Pick two teachable subjects** where you can prove mastery: an IGCSE/MYP grade of A*/7 or a DP1 predicted 7 (e.g. Math, Physics, Chemistry, Economics, English). **Don't teach DP level yet.** Your credible market is MYP 3–5 and IGCSE (grades 8–10).

**Week of 26 October**
5. **Price check (2 hours).** Collect 10 real quotes for MYP/IGCSE Math and Science tutoring from listing platforms, parent WhatsApp groups and parents you know, and log each with its source. Expect about ₹800–1,500 an hour for student or college tutors and ₹2,000–5,000+ for specialists. **Set your price at the 40th percentile of the student-tutor band**, likely ₹1,000–1,200.
6. **Offer, exactly:** "IGCSE/MYP Math and Physics coaching by a current IB DP student. 55-minute live online sessions (in-person only at our home, with a parent present). Free 30-minute diagnostic. 8-session pack ₹8,800 prepaid (₹1,100 per session). Single session ₹1,250. Small group of 3: ₹700 per student per session."
   - **Why online by default:** a Mumbai home visit costs 30–60 minutes each way, and two visits in one evening would break the 8-hour cap. In-person happens only when the client comes to your home or lives in your own building or complex.
7. **Minimum tools (under ₹3,000):**
   - Domain: about ₹800–1,200 a year.
   - Free Gmail now; Google Workspace later.
   - One-page site on Carrd or Google Sites.
   - A Google Form for bookings.
   - WhatsApp Business.
   - Google Meet, with the parent invited.
   - UPI or bank transfer to your savings account. Skip payment gateways, whose KYC minors usually can't complete.

**Week of 2 November** (Diwali is on 8 November 2026. Plan outreach around it.)
8. **Safeguarding and consent pack (2 hours):**
   - Online sessions use your own account, with the parent on the invite.
   - In-person sessions only with an adult present.
   - No private messaging with the child. All messages go to a group that includes the parent.
   - Minimal personal data, kept in one Sheet.
   - A signed parent consent form covering data use and any recording. India's DPDP Act 2023 requires verifiable parental consent for children's data. Its rules were notified in November 2025 with phased compliance, so act as if it applies in full.
9. **Academic-integrity rule, on the site:** "We teach. We never write, edit for content, or complete any assessed work (IA, EE, TOK, coursework) for a student." Breaking this could get you investigated for IB academic misconduct, which could cost you your own Diploma.

**Week of 9–15 November**
10. **Outreach list of exactly 30 families**, each with a child in grades 7–10 at an IB, IGCSE or international school. Sources: parents' friends, relatives, your building or society, and siblings' or cousins' classmates' parents. Log each in the Sheet.
11. **Send one by one (not a broadcast), with a parent's intro:**
    > "Hi [Name] Aunty/Uncle, I'm [Student], [Parent]'s son/daughter. I'm in IB DP1 at [School] and scored [grade] in IGCSE/MYP [subject]. I'm starting online 1:1 and small-group coaching for grades 7–10 in [subjects]. This month I'm offering a free 30-minute diagnostic: I'll find the 3 topics costing [child] the most marks and send you a written plan. Would [child] like a slot this weekend?"

  - 10 minutes: a 6-question mini-test.
  - 10 minutes: walk through the mistakes.

- **Delivery standard:**
  - A 3-line summary to the parent group after every session (covered / homework / next).
  - A mini-test every 4 sessions, with the score trend sent to the parent.

**GATE 1: 31 December 2026**

**GATE 2: 31 March 2027**
- **Pass:** all 5 weekly slots full (4 × 1:1 plus 1 group of 3 = 7 students), **and** 3+ families waitlisted, **and** no IB slippage. → Phase 2.
- **Partial:** slots full but no waitlist. → Raise the price 15% for new clients and re-test on 31 May.

**Phase 1 income:** (4 × ₹1,100 + 3 × ₹700) = ₹6,500 a week × 4.33 = **₹28,145 a month** by March 2027. This is your own skill income, under your PAN, and tax-free at this level (Section 6.2).

### Phase 2: First tutors, April – August 2027 (8 hours a week in term, up to 15 in summer after the EE)

1. **Set up the agency layer under the DAH** (after the CA meeting, Section 7). The DAH signs the tutor coordination agreements. You run it day to day and log your hours. The DAH pays you ₹1,000 an hour.
2. **Commission price ladder** (parents pay the tutor; the tutor pays the agency):

   | Level | Parent pays tutor/hr | Tutor keeps | Agency fee | Fee % |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | MYP / IGCSE 1:1 | ₹1,800 | ₹1,200 | ₹600 | 33% |
   | IB DP 1:1 (tutor has a 7 in the subject) | ₹3,000 | ₹2,000 | ₹1,000 | 33% |
   | Small group (3), MYP/IGCSE | 3 × ₹900 = ₹2,700 | ₹1,500 | ₹1,200 | 44% |

   **Rules:**
   - The agency invoices each tutor on the 1st for last month's logged hours. Payment is due by the 7th.
   - A tutor who hasn't paid gets no new matches, and their existing clients are offered another tutor.
   - Parents book through the agency form. All reports go through the agency's parent group, so the agency owns the relationship.
   - Your own legacy clients stay with you at your own price under your own PAN.
3. **Recruit 4–5 tutors:**
   - **Sources:** IB alumni now at Mumbai colleges (St. Xavier's, Jai Hind, NMIMS, IIT Bombay, ICT), gap-year IB graduates, alumni WhatsApp and LinkedIn groups.
   - **Hard filters:**
     - Age 18+.
     - IB score 40+ or a 7 in the subject, verified live through the candidate portal login or a school letter.
     - 2 references.
   - **Screening:** a 20-minute call, then a recorded 30-minute demo lesson scored on a 10-point rubric (clarity, pacing, checks for understanding, warmth, punctuality). Hire at 8 or above.
   - **Coordination agreement** (one page, DAH and tutor) covering:
     - The fee and its payment terms.
     - The academic-integrity rule and safeguarding rules.
     - Confidentiality.
     - A no-solicitation clause *during* the engagement. Under Section 27 of the Indian Contract Act, a restraint *after* the engagement ends is generally void, so don't rely on one.
4. **Quality control:**
   - A 5-question parent rating after a new tutor's first 3 sessions.
   - A tutor averaging below 4.0 is coached once, then dropped.
   - The student or coordinator sits in on 1 session per tutor per month.
5. **Your role:** teach at most 2 hours a week. Spend 5–6 hours on diagnostics, matching and quality.
6. **Summer 2027:** write the Extended Essay first (protect 15+ hours a week for it). Then run a 4-week online "IGCSE Boards Bootcamp" for small groups, prepaid.

**GATE 3: 31 August 2027**
- **Pass:** 4+ tutors delivering 15+ hours a week, average parent rating 4.3+, at least 1 renewal per tutor, a waitlist of 6+, and every tutor fee paid on time. → Phase 3.
- **Fail:** churn, or ratings below 4.0. → Stay "solo plus 1–2 tutors" through DP2 (the downside case, about ₹38k a month) and rescale after exams.

### Phase 3: Systematise and hand over, September – December 2027 (6 hours a week)

1. **Hire the coordinator by 30 September 2027.** A college student, about 15 hours a week, ₹18,000 a month. Write a 2-page operations manual first, covering booking → diagnostic → match → first session → feedback → renewal → monthly tutor invoicing.
2. **Grow from 4 to 8 tutors by 31 December 2027.** Hire **1 tutor for every 3 waitlisted families, at most 2 a month**. Expected adds: Sep 1, Oct 1, Nov 1, Dec 1, so 4 + 4 = 8. Up to 2 a month is allowed if the waitlist supports it. At 35 hours a week, 8 tutors average 4.4 hours each, well inside a college student's spare time.
3. **Prepaid packs only.** Parents prepay the tutor for 8 or 12 sessions. Tutors pay the agency monthly.
4. **Add IB DP 1:1** through tutors who are IB graduates. It earns a ₹1,000 fee per hour.

### Phase 4: Growth freeze, 1 January – 31 May 2028 (4 hours a week maximum)

- No new tutors, products or campaigns. The coordinator runs day-to-day.
- Your duties: a 1-hour Sunday review plus at most 2 escalations a week (about 3.5 hours logged, which is ₹15,000 a month from the DAH).

- **Milestone M check, 31 May 2028:** 35 hours a week and ₹80k+ a month pre-tax enterprise profit for March–May.

## PASS 3 (part 2): Why this business and not the others

Each option is scored out of 5 on seven criteria that are hard constraints for you.

| Option | Minor-legal | IB-hours fit | Low capital | Your edge | Own income (tax) | Portable | Skill | **Total /35** |

| **Academic coaching → agency (recommended)** | 4 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 4 | **29** |
| AI/web services for SMEs (Track B) | 3 | 3 | 5 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 5 | **27** |

| Stock/F&O trading | 1 | 3 | 3 | 1 | 1 | 5 | 2 | **16** |

**How the scores were set:**
- **Coaching → agency:**
  - Minor-legal is 4, not 5, because the agency layer needs an adult holder.
  - Own income is 4 because the agency's profit is the DAH's until 18.
  - Portable is 3 because Path A forces passive ownership.
- **Content creator ties on score but loses on the goal.** Typical time to a ₹1 lakh-a-month income is 18–36 months, and very few creators get there at all. Coaching pays from week 4. Run a small study-tips channel **as marketing** for the coaching business (1 short video a week in the summer of 2027), not as the business itself.
- **Trading:**
  - SEBI's September 2024 study found about 93% of individual F&O traders lost money over FY22–FY24.
  - Minors' demat accounts are limited to delivery equity through a guardian, with no F&O or intraday trading.
- **D2C brands and franchises:** these need capital, inventory and contracts a minor can't sign. The business and its learning would really belong to the parents.
- **The capital-only route, stated honestly:**
  - ₹1 lakh a month of passive income at a 4% withdrawal rate needs ₹1L × 12 ÷ 0.04 = **₹3 crore (about 4.3% of net worth)**.
  - That is a gift, not a business.
  - Before 18, income on money gifted to you is clubbed with your parent's income.
  - It stays as the family's safety net, and it covers Path A years if needed.

## Section 5: Track B, the costed fallback (if Gate 1 fails)

**Business:** a "digital storefront in a week" service for Mumbai small businesses (clinics, salons, CA firms, caterers, boutiques).

**Product:**
- Google Business Profile set up and optimised.
- WhatsApp Business catalogue and auto-replies.
- A one-page website.
- A no-code booking flow: form → Google Sheet → WhatsApp or email confirmation.
- **Price:** ₹20,000 one-off (prepaid), plus ₹4,000 a month for care (updates, review replies, monthly report).

**Legal split (same logic as Section 6.1):**
- **One-off builds:** you do the work yourself, the client prepays 100% to you, and you deliver within 7 days. No enforceable contract is needed, and the income is your skill income.

## PASS 3 (part 3): Deep dives on the parts most likely to break

### 6.1 Legal structure while you're under 18

- **Contracts.** Under Section 11 of the Indian Contract Act 1872 a minor can't contract. In *Mohori Bibee v Dharmodas Ghose* (1903) the Privy Council held a minor's agreement void. You can't be sued on your agreements, but you can't enforce them either.
- **What you can do in your own name:** deliver your own prepaid services and receive payment into your own account. Prepayment means you never need to enforce anything.
- **What needs an adult:** engaging tutors and freelancers, issuing invoices with enforceable terms, and holding retainers. This is the **Designated Adult Holder (DAH)**.
- **Choose the DAH by three tests, in this order:**
  1. **GST headroom.** GST registration and the ₹20 lakh threshold work on **aggregate turnover per PAN across India, including exempt supplies.** Examples: a doctor's healthcare fees, rent from a residential let, any existing consultancy or proprietorship receipts. A parent who already has, say, ₹15 lakh of such turnover leaves only ₹5 lakh of headroom, and the agency's ₹7.6 lakh of FY2027-28 fees would push them over. Salary is not a supply and doesn't count. Ask the CA to compute each candidate's aggregate turnover. **[CA]**
  2. **Tax slab.** Agency profit is taxed in the DAH's hands. A salaried or non-earning parent or grandparent with total income under ₹12 lakh may pay little or no tax on it. A parent in the 30% bracket with a surcharge pays up to about 39%.
  3. **Reliability.** The DAH signs agreements, holds the bank account, and must actually gift the business at 18.
- **If no candidate has ₹10 lakh+ of headroom:** the DAH registers for GST and the agency absorbs 18% on its fee. The base case then falls to ₹65,000 a month (Section 6.3).
- **Why the commission model:** if the agency billed parents as principal, its turnover would be the full ₹2,160 average hourly price. That is about ₹22.8 lakh in FY2027-28, over the threshold, and GST would apply to every hour. In the commission model the agency's supply is a coordination service to tutors, so its turnover is only the fee: about ₹7.6 lakh in FY2027-28 and ₹16.8 lakh a year at 45 hours a week. Each tutor's own receipts (about ₹6–10 lakh a year) stay under the threshold too.
  - **Two conditions keep this clean:**
    - (i) The agency never collects the parent's money on the tutor's behalf. Collecting it could make it look like a principal or an e-commerce operator.
    - (ii) Agreements and invoices describe it plainly as a coordination fee.
  - **[CA: confirm both conditions and that s.52/s.24 e-commerce-operator registration doesn't apply.]**
- **Paying you:** the DAH pays you ₹1,000 per logged hour for sales, matching and quality work. That rate is set against what a senior tutor or part-time manager earns. As skill income it's yours, not clubbed. Keep a timesheet. A related-party payment must be at market value, so don't inflate it. **[CA]**
- **Company, partnership, LLP:**
  - A minor can't practically be a director.
  - Under Section 30 of the Indian Partnership Act 1932, a minor can only be admitted to the benefits of a partnership.
  - An LLP partner must be able to contract.
  - **So no company before 18.**
  - A Pvt Ltd needs at least 2 shareholders and 2 directors, with at least one director resident in India, so a parent holds 1% and is the second director.
- **At 18:** the business is gifted to you (Phase 5). In Path I it stays a proprietorship until FY2029-30, because at ₹13.5 lakh profit, personal slab tax (about 6.4%) beats the company route (25.17% + dividend tax). Incorporate when you hire the GM, need limited liability, or go abroad.
- **Udyam and Maharashtra Shops & Establishments:** check with the CA once you have staff. **[CA]**

### 6.2 Tax (exact mechanics)

- **Clubbing.** A minor's income is normally added to the parent who earns more. This was Section 64(1A) of the 1961 Act, and the rule carries into the Income-tax Act 2025 (in force from 1 April 2026). **The exception:** income from manual work or from an activity involving the minor's skill, talent or specialised knowledge is the minor's own. **Your own tutoring, your build work in Track B, and your hourly fee from the DAH are all skill income.** **[CA: confirm the section in the 2025 Act.]**
- **Your own income:** filed under your PAN, with the guardian verifying while you're a minor **[CA]**. Phase 1 income of about ₹3.4 lakh a year, plus the DAH fee, sits well under ₹12 lakh, so with the rebate it is tax-free.
- **Worked tax figures used in this plan (new regime, resident, 4% cess):**

  | Income | Slab tax | With cess | Net |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | ₹13.54 L (Target 1, proprietorship, no standard deduction) | 20,000 + 40,000 + 23,025 = ₹83,025 | ₹86,346 | ₹12.67 L |

  Marginal relief near ₹12 lakh: the tax can't exceed the income above ₹12 lakh. It doesn't bind in either row. No surcharge applies below ₹50 lakh. **[CA: confirm the current year's rates.]**
- **Path A residency:** you may still count as resident in FY2028-29 (an Indian citizen leaving for studies doesn't get the employment relaxation). Later years may be non-resident, with dividends taxed by TDS and the treaty rate. **[CA]**
- **GST summary:**
  - Private tutoring is taxable at 18% (only education leading to recognised qualifications is exempt).
