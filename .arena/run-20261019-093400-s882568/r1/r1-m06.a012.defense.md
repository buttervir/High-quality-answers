ATTACK 1: CONCEDE. Five tutors at my prices is about ₹44 lakh a year, so GST at 18% applies, and my profit math left it out. I rebuilt the math with GST-inclusive prices, phase-specific overheads (the ₹1.5L overhead the attacker used was my full-scale figure, not the 5-tutor one) and ~25% company tax. Result: about ₹1L/month after tax needs 5 fully loaded tutors in Phase 3 and 6 in DP2 with a full-time manager. The full-scale figure is now ~₹2.45L pre-tax, not ₹2.7–3.7L. Gates and dates were updated to match.
ATTACK 2: CONCEDE. My own trigger (first non-family tutor) fired in Week 9, but incorporation sat in Month 5. Incorporation now starts the day the Week-8 gate passes (Week 9). Tutors are recruited in Weeks 9–12 and start teaching only once the company and its bank account exist (~Week 13), under company contracts signed by the two directors. Adults are never paid from the minor's account.
ATTACK 3: CONCEDE. Margin earned on other tutors' hours is very likely not "the minor's own skill" income, so in Stage A it would be clubbed at the parent's rate. The fix fits with Attack 2: from Week 13 all revenue goes to the company, which is a separate taxpayer at ~25%, so nothing is clubbed. The student is paid a documented fee for real work they do (teaching, curriculum, management), which is the skill-income question for the CA. Profits are retained and no dividends are paid until the student turns 18. Stage A personal income is now limited to the founder's own teaching.
ATTACK 4: CONCEDE. Phase 2–3 had no hour budget. Each phase now has a per-task hour table that adds up to 8 hrs, and there is a fixed drop-order rule. Instagram is cut until the ops manager exists, the curriculum is built one course at a time and one week ahead, tutors are paid to write session plans, the CA does the incorporation, and the ops manager arrives at the start of Phase 3. Phase 3 now aims for 5 tutors, not 5–8.
ATTACK 5: CONCEDE. The fallback is now a full plan with its own steps: niche choice, the exact offer, the tool stack (WhatsApp Business app, a Meta-approved BSP on the client's own account, Make/Zapier, Google Sites), how to land 3 "founding clients" from warm intros, the payment rule (50% upfront by UPI, 50% before go-live), hour costs per setup, gates at fallback weeks 8 and 16, and when it triggers the company.
ATTACK 6: CONCEDE. Companies Act 2013 s.149(1) requires 2 directors for a private company, and an OPC can't have a minor as member. The plan now requires two adult directors (a parent plus another parent or a trusted adult relative, with at least one resident in India). While checking this I also found that the minor should not be a subscriber to the memorandum. Instead, the parents subscribe and the student is allotted or transferred the majority of fully paid shares through a guardian right after incorporation.
ATTACK 7: CONCEDE. "DP2 students" and "18+" mostly cancel out, and a 38+ score can't screen current students. The tutor pool is now defined as 18+ recent IB/IGCSE/A-level graduates and strong college students in the subject (in Mumbai, or online from anywhere in India), screened by subject grade, a demo lesson and references. There is an explicit pool check: fewer than 10 qualified applicants in 3 weeks means widening the criteria and raising pay to ₹900–₹1,000/hr.
