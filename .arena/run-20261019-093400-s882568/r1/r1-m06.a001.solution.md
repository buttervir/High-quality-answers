# From IB DP1 to Job-Replacing Income: The Exact Plan (Mumbai, Age 16)

## The answer in one paragraph

Start a **tutoring business for younger students (Grades 6–10: MYP, IGCSE, ICSE), taught by you, this week**, online and at home. First 1:1, then small groups, with the money paid into your own bank account. From Month 5, add a **small tutoring agency** where IB alumni at university (aged 18 and over) teach **small batches** under your brand. You keep about 45% of every batch-hour after GST and the tutor fee. By **Month 15–18** the two together come to about **₹1,00,000 a month before tax**, which matches a good fresher salary in Mumbai. Your own time stays capped at **6 hours a week**. Total cash needed: **₹25,000 or less**. The agency is legally run by a parent until you turn 18, for tax and contract reasons (Section 3), and passes to you at 18. If teaching isn't for you, Plan B (Section 7) uses the same steps for a local-business services agency.

---

## Assumptions

- **"Job-replacing money" = ₹1,00,000/month profit before tax**, about the same as a ₹12 LPA fresher salary package in Mumbai.
- **Milestones (one timeline used throughout):** Month 3 ₹25k → Month 6 ₹55k → Month 9 ₹60k → Month 12 ₹80k → Month 15–18 ₹1L. Month 1 = November 2026.
- **IB comes first.** DP grades decide university, and university is worth more than anything this earns before 18. You get hard caps, a kill switch and an exam calendar (Section 9).
- **Your parents agree.** One parent becomes the legal owner of the agency stage until you turn 18.
- **The family's ~$8M is a safety net and a network, not starting capital** (Section 8).
- You are at least average in one subject (Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Economics, English, Biology). If not, use Plan B.
- **Prices:** check them in Week 1 by asking 5 parents and checking 2 local tutor listings. All numbers below can be re-run from the formulas given.

## 1. How this plan was chosen (three experts, one decision)

| Expert | Their best idea | Where they pushed back |
|---|---|---|
| **Mumbai startup operator** | "Sell a service to people you can reach this week. No inventory, no app." | Wanted a digital agency for local businesses because it bills more per client. |
| **Chartered Accountant / lawyer** | "Income from a minor's **own skill** stays the minor's income. Income from **other people's work** gets added to a parent's income. Plan the structure in two layers from day one, and never let a minor sign contracts with adults." | Required GST planning based on **gross turnover**, not profit. |
| **IB coordinator / admissions counsellor** | "Tutoring strengthens your own subject knowledge, and 'built a tutoring company' is a strong application line." | Firm limits: 6 hrs/week, exam blackouts, never write anyone's IA or EE, and a paid business **does not count as CAS**. |

**How the disagreements were settled:**
- *Tutoring or digital agency?* **Tutoring.** The buyers are already in your school and family network, payment comes the same day, and it fits around IB. The digital agency is **Plan B**.
- *1:1 or groups?* **Groups as early as possible.** One batch-hour of 5 students earns 2–3 times a 1:1 hour, and parents pay less per student. That's what makes ₹1L possible inside the market's price ranges.
- *Who owns what?* **Two layers.** Your own teaching is yours (your PAN, your account). The agency belongs to a parent's sole proprietorship until you turn 18 (Section 3).

## 2. The numbers

**Mumbai market rates, 2026 (verify in Week 1):**
- Professional IB/IGCSE tutors: ₹2,500–₹5,000+/hr (1:1)
- University-student tutors: ₹1,000–₹2,000/hr (1:1)
- Small-batch coaching (4–6 students): about ₹600–1,000 per student per hour
- **You (strong DP1 student teaching Grades 6–10):** ₹1,200/hr 1:1, ₹700/student/hr in a batch

**Unit economics of the agency batch** (the formula behind the ₹1L):

| Item | Per batch-hour |
|---|---|
| 5 students × ₹750 (GST-inclusive price) | ₹3,750 |
| Less 18% GST included in the price (₹3,750 × 18/118) | −₹572 |
| Net revenue | ₹3,178 |
| Tutor fee (18+ IB alumnus) | −₹1,500 |
| **Your margin** | **₹1,678** |

Before GST registration, the ₹572 is extra margin. Treat it as a buffer, not income.
1:1 agency sessions at ₹1,800 (GST-inclusive, within the market range) → ₹1,525 net → ₹1,200 to the tutor → ₹325 margin. These are a convenience product, not the engine.

**Path to ₹1L/month:**

| Stage | Months | What runs | Monthly profit (pre-tax) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Solo 1:1 | 1–3 (Nov–Jan) | You teach 5 hrs/week 1:1 at ₹1,200 | about ₹25k |
| 2. Your own batches | 3–6 (Jan–Apr) | You teach 2 batches (4–5 students, ₹700/student/hr, 2 hrs/week each) + 2 hrs/week 1:1 = 6 hrs/week | about ₹55–60k |
| 3. Agency | 5–18 (Mar onwards) | You keep **1 batch** (2 hrs/week, about ₹25k) and manage tutors (4 hrs/week). Tutor batches grow: 4 batches by Month 9, 5–6 by Month 12, 7 by Month 15–18 | Month 9: ₹60k · Month 12: ₹80k · Month 15–18: **₹1.0L** |

**Month 15–18 check:** 7 tutor batches × 2 hrs/week × 4 weeks = 56 batch-hours × ₹1,678 = ₹94k. Subtract ₹15k/month fixed costs (CA and GST filing ₹3k, ads ₹8k, tools ₹1k, Head Tutor stipend averaged ₹3k), giving **₹79k agency**. Add **₹25k from your own batch**, for **about ₹1.04L/month**. That needs about **35 students in tutor batches and 4–5 tutors**. Not 100+ hours, and not professional-tutor prices.

## 3. Setup: legal, money and tax (exact structure)

### Layer 1: your own teaching (from Day 1)
1. **PAN for you** (as a minor, through a parent) on the Protean (NSDL) or UTIITSL site: about ₹107, arrives in 1–3 weeks.
2. **Your own self-operated minor savings account** (SBI, HDFC, ICICI and Kotak allow ages 10+), with UPI and a debit card. Only **your own teaching fees** go in here, about ₹25–60k a month. That's modest and easy to explain.
3. **UPI QR code** in your WhatsApp Business profile. Put a note on every payment: "Tuition – [student] – [month]".
4. **Tax:** income from a minor's own "skill, talent or specialised knowledge" is **not** added to the parents' income (Section 64(1A) of the Income-tax Act 1961, carried into the Income-tax Act 2025 that applies from 1 April 2026). Under the new regime, total income up to **₹12 lakh** is tax-free because of the rebate, so your own teaching (₹3–7L a year) is effectively tax-free. **File an income tax return every year anyway** (a CA charges ₹1,500–3,000).
5. **GST:** GST is counted per PAN on gross turnover. Your own teaching tops out at about ₹7L a year, well below ₹20L. No GST.
6. **Contracts:** a minor's contract is void in India. So **a parent signs the one-page parent agreement as your guardian**, and everything is **paid in advance**, so there's never anything to chase.

### Layer 2: the agency (set up in Month 4, before the first tutor)
7. **Legal owner:** a **sole proprietorship in the name of the parent who (a) has the lowest other taxable income and (b) has no existing GST registration.** Reason (b) matters: GST counts all businesses under one PAN together. A parent who already has a GST-registered business would have to charge GST on tutoring from day one.
8. **Register:** Udyam (free, online) in the proprietor's name, with a trade name such as "[Brand] Learning". Open a **current account** in that trade name (most banks accept Udyam plus the proprietor's PAN and Aadhaar). **All agency fees come in here and all tutor fees go out from here.** Tutor payouts never pass through your minor account.
9. **Who signs:** the proprietor parent signs the parent agreements for agency students and the **tutor agreements**. You're the operator: you sell, match, check quality and keep the Sheet. Your parent does the month-end bank transfers using your Sheet (about 15 minutes).
10. **GST:** price every agency product **GST-inclusive from the start**, so prices never change. When agency turnover passes **₹15L in a financial year** (around Month 12–13 at 5–6 batches), the CA registers for GST. Registration is legally required within 30 days of crossing ₹20L. After that, file monthly or quarterly through the CA (budgeted at ₹3k/month).
11. **Income tax on the agency:** it is the proprietor's income. Agency profit at full run rate is about ₹9.5L a year. If that parent's other taxable income is small, the total can stay near or under the ₹12L rebate line and the tax is close to zero. If not, budget **about 30% of agency profit** for tax, which brings ₹1.04L pre-tax down to about ₹80k a month after tax. A ₹12 LPA job is taxed too, so compare like with like.
12. **Family agreement (one page, signed by both parents and you):** agency profit is held in the proprietorship's account (or a parent's investment account) for you, and you decide how it's reinvested. At 18 the business, brand, Sheet, contracts and accumulated profit move to you. Gifts from a parent to an adult child are tax-free, and the minor-clubbing rule stops at 18.

**Cost of setup: about ₹500 for Layer 1, about ₹3–5k for Layer 2 (CA consultation and the current account's minimum balance aside).**

## 4. Days 1–30: launch, step by step

### Week 1: offer and first leads
- **Day 1:** pick **one subject and one grade band**, e.g. "MYP 4–5 / IGCSE Maths".
- **Day 2:** write 3 packages:
  - **Trial:** ₹500 for 60 minutes ("if it doesn't help, it's free")
  - **Monthly 1:1:** 8 sessions for ₹9,600, paid in advance
  - **Exam sprint:** 6 sessions in the 3 weeks before school exams, ₹7,500. Sell it only in **open windows** (Section 9.1).
- **Day 3:** a 5-question Google Form: grade, school, subject, weak topics, parent phone number.
- **Day 4:** a **40-name list**: younger siblings of friends, parents' friends with children aged 11–15, cousins, building-society families, juniors at your school.
- **Days 5–7:** send this to all 40, and ask your parents to forward it in their groups:

> *Hi Aunty/Uncle, I'm [Name], in IB DP1 at [School]. I'm starting small-batch tutoring for Grade 7–10 Maths (MYP/IGCSE/ICSE), online or at home in [area]. First session is ₹500, and if it doesn't help, it's free. I've got 6 slots this month. Could [child] try one this week? Here's a 1-minute form: [link]*

**Week 1 target:** 5 trials booked.

### Week 2: trials that convert
- Trial format: **10 min** diagnostic → **35 min** fix the weakest topic → **10 min** homework → **5 min** call with the parent ("here's what I found, here's the 4-week plan").
- Close: *"I can keep Tuesday and Friday 6 pm for [child]. The monthly plan is ₹9,600, paid in advance on UPI. Shall I lock the slot?"*
- Send the one-page agreement (signed by your parent as guardian): advance payment, 24-hour cancellation, no refunds for sessions already held, online or with an adult at home, and **"I don't write coursework, IAs or homework for students."**

**Week 2 target:** 3 paying students.

### Weeks 3–4: systemise
- A **2-line WhatsApp progress note** to the parent after every session.
- Referral ask: *"If you know one family who'd benefit, I'll give you one free session."*
- A Sheet with student, hours, paid (Y/N), referral source and exam dates.
- A **question bank** in Drive (topic → worksheets → answers). It becomes the tutors' training kit later.

**End of Month 1:** 3–5 students, ₹20–30k collected in advance, 5–6 hrs/week.

## 5. Months 2–18: from your hours to a business

### Months 3–6 (Jan–Apr 2027): your own batches
- From the 5th inquiry onwards, put students into **batches of 4–5 at the same level**, at ₹700/student/hr. You get ₹2,800–3,500 per hour, and each parent pays less than for 1:1.
- Run 2 batches × 2 hrs/week plus 2 hrs of 1:1 = **6 hrs/week cap**. **Month 6 target: about ₹55k.**
- Brand it: a name, a Canva logo, an Instagram page with results (first names only, with parental permission), and a **Google Business Profile** ("IGCSE Maths tuition [your area]").

### Months 4–5 (Feb–Mar 2027): set up the agency and hire before your DP1 exam blackout
- Set up Layer 2 (Section 3, steps 7–12) in Month 4.
- **Hire 2 tutors in Month 5**, before your DP1 end-of-year exam blackout. They're IB alumni from your school now at university, **aged 18+ only**, with a 6–7 in the subject. Post in the alumni WhatsApp group and on LinkedIn: *"Paid tutoring, ₹1,500 per batch-hour, flexible, online, MYP/IGCSE students."*
- **Tutor agreement** (signed by the proprietor parent): ₹1,500 per batch-hour, paid within 7 days of month-end; uses the brand's question bank and session format; progress note after every session; safeguarding rules; no writing of coursework; **no soliciting the brand's students while engaged** (post-engagement restraints are void under Section 27 of the Indian Contract Act, so none is included).
- **Real anti-poaching protection:** parents pay the brand, message the brand's number and get the brand's progress notes, so the relationship stays with the business. Tutors get a ₹500 bonus per student who renews for a term, which makes staying worth more than leaving.
- Quality: you sit in on each new tutor's first 2 batch sessions.

### Months 6–18: grow the agency to 7 batches
- **You:** keep 1 batch (2 hrs/week, about ₹25k on your own PAN) plus 4 hrs/week running the agency: parent calls, matching, reading 2 progress notes per tutor, and the month-end Sheet for payroll.
- **Tutor batches:** 4 by Month 9 (**₹60k total**), 5–6 by Month 12 (**₹80k**), 7 by Month 15–18 (**₹1.0L**).
- **The lead funnel for about 35 agency students** (about 4 net new a month from Month 6, allowing for about 20% churn):

| Source | Expected new students/month |
|---|---|
| Referrals (asked of every family each term, with 1 free session as reward) | 1–2 |
| Each tutor's own network (alumni families, siblings), 1 per tutor per term | 1 |
| Google Business Profile + 1 tutor-listing site (verify which ones Mumbai parents use) | 1 |
| One free 60-minute "exam strategy" workshop per term at a building society, club or MYP parent group (30 attendees → 3–5 sign-ups) | 1–2 (averaged) |

- **Weekly numbers:** billed batch-hours, students per batch (keep 4–6; merge any batch that drops below 4), and parent renewal rate (target 80%+).
- Add IB DP subjects only when they're taught by alumni with a 6–7. **Never tutor your own classmates.**

### Month 12 (Oct 2027, start of DP2): appoint a Head Tutor
- Promote your best tutor to **Head Tutor** for **+₹8,000/month**, paid only in the months when you're in blackout. They handle intake calls, trial bookings and matching. Your proprietor parent keeps doing payroll from the Sheet. This is how the agency keeps earning while you're paused for DP2 mocks and finals.

## 7. Plan B (if tutoring isn't your thing): local business services agency

Offer: **"I set up and run WhatsApp Business catalogues, Google Business Profiles and simple websites for local shops, clinics and family businesses."**
- **Price:** setup ₹15,000–40,000 (average ₹25,000), plus a monthly retainer of ₹7,000 (Google Business Profile updates, 8 posts, review replies, WhatsApp catalogue upkeep).
- **Leads:** your parents' business contacts (suppliers, clinics, CAs, restaurants, showrooms). Week 1: one free sample for a family friend, with before/after screenshots. Weeks 2–3: pitch 20 contacts. Target: 3 clients by Month 2.
- **The ₹1L maths:** 14 retainers × ₹7,000 = ₹98,000, plus 1 setup a month × ₹25,000, gives ₹1,23,000 revenue. Less a part-time assistant (a design or BMM student, ₹15,000/month) who does the posting, and ₹5,000 in tools: **about ₹1,03,000/month**.
- **Hours:** you do sales, setups and client calls in 6 hrs/week. The assistant does about 1.5 hrs per client per month of posting from templates. Without the assistant, Plan B tops out at about ₹50–60k within the IB cap.
- **Structure:** same two layers. Solo work goes to your PAN. Once the assistant is hired, it becomes a parent's proprietorship (turnover about ₹14.8L/yr, under the GST threshold). Advance payment, the parent signs, IB comes first.

## 8. Why not use the family's money?

- **Money doesn't fix the hard part, which is finding paying customers.** A café, D2C brand or franchise at 16 means rent and stock before you have proof, while you're in school 8 hours a day.
- **Rule (write it into the family agreement):** you can ask for capital only after **₹50k/month profit for 3 months in a row**, and only for something that clearly grows the business (e.g. ₹1–2L for tutor recruitment and ads).
- **What the family's wealth is really good for:** the **network** (your first 40 names and the workshop venues) and the **safety net**. Plus one adult who can legally own the agency and sign contracts until you're 18.

## 9. Stress-tested: what breaks and how it's fixed

**1. "My DP grades are falling" or "exam season clashes with sales season."**
*Hard limits:* a maximum of 6 hrs/week of your own time. **Kill switch:** if any predicted grade drops by 1 point, take on no new students until it recovers.
*Exam calendar* (confirm against your school's and clients' schools' dates):

| Period | Your status | Client demand | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nov–Jan (DP1) | Open | School mid-terms are over. Steady weekly tutoring | Build Stage 1 |
| Feb–Mar (DP1) | Open | School finals / ICSE boards | **Sell exam sprints yourself** |
| ~mid-Apr–Jun (DP1) | **Blackout**: 4 weeks before and during DP1 end-of-year exams | IGCSE May–June series | You teach existing students only. **Tutors (hired in Month 5) teach the IGCSE sprints.** Pre-sell sprints in March |
| Aug–Oct (DP2) | Open, with IA/EE deadlines first | School mid-terms, IGCSE Oct–Nov series | Sprints run by you and tutors. Head Tutor appointed |
| ~Dec–Feb (DP2) | **Blackout** (mocks) | School finals / boards | Head Tutor handles intake, tutors teach, parent does payroll |
| Apr–May (DP2) | **Blackout** (finals) | IGCSE | Same as above. You do 0 hours |

The blackout limits **your** teaching and intake. It never stops the business.

**2. "A parent offers ₹20,000 for an IA, a project or homework."**
*What breaks:* academic misconduct, which risks the student's grades and your own diploma.
*Fix:* the no-coursework clause is in every parent and tutor agreement. Your script: *"I teach the method, the student does the work."*

**3. "No students. The market's full of tutoring apps."**
*Fix:* (a) Make the first 3 trials free in exchange for written testimonials. (b) Narrow the offer to an exam sprint, **sold in the next open window in the calendar above**. If that window is a blackout, pre-sell it and have it taught after the blackout ends, or by a tutor once Stage 3 is running. (c) Offer a free revision session through your school's MYP coordinator. (d) Nothing after 4 weeks → switch to Plan B. Total loss: about ₹500 and 4 weekends.

**4. "Safety: strangers' homes, or a parent complains."**
*Fix:* **online by default.** In-person only at homes of families you know, in a shared room, with an adult present. Tutors are 18+, follow the same rule, and are never alone behind closed doors with a student. Complaints are answered within 24 hours with a free make-up session, and the proprietor parent reviews anything serious.

**5. "GST, tax, bank or contract problems at scale."**
*What breaks:* gross turnover crosses ₹20L, agency income gets added to a parent's income, a minor's savings account handles adult payroll, and contracts signed by a minor are void.
*Fix:* this is built into Section 3. The agency is a parent's proprietorship with its own current account and is GST-ready (GST-inclusive prices, registration at ₹15L). Tax is budgeted at up to 30%, the adult parent signs every contract, and your minor account only takes in your own teaching fees. The CA files the ITRs (yours and the proprietor's) and the GST returns.

**Extra: "Does this count for CAS?"** No, paid work doesn't count. Run a **free weekly session for a Mumbai education NGO or a municipal school**. That counts as CAS *Service*, and it makes you a better teacher.
