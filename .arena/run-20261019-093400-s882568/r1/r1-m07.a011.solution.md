# From DP1 to a job's take-home pay: one business, worked out in rupees

## The answer in one paragraph

Start a **small-group online tutoring business** in the one subject where you score a 7. It has two products:
- **Grade 9–10 (MYP 4–5 / IGCSE)** groups, which you teach yourself during DP1.
- **DP1 groups**, for students one year below you, which paid alumni tutors teach from DP2 onward.

A summer "Bridge to DP" course connects the two. Every May your Grade 10 students leave, and the bridge course turns them into DP1 customers who then stay for two years.

With the numbers below, the business reaches **about ₹1,05,000 a month in profit after GST by December 2027**, on 13 groups and 5 tutors. Here is what that means in your pocket:
- **While you are a minor**, the profit is taxed as a parent's income. The family keeps about **₹67,000 a month**.
- **From your 18th birthday**, the profit is your own income. You keep about **₹1,00,000 a month in hand**, which is the take-home pay of a ₹12–13 LPA Mumbai job (about ₹90,000–97,000 a month).

So the plan fully replaces a job once you turn 18, and gets you about 70% of the way before that. It needs no company, no website and no family capital.

## Assumptions

- "Job replacing money" means matching the **take-home** pay of a ₹12 LPA salary. Under the new tax regime that salary pays almost no income tax (the s.87A rebate plus the ₹75,000 standard deduction), so its take-home is about ₹90,000–97,000 a month after PF.
- You started DP1 in mid-2026, sit finals in May 2028, and turn 18 sometime between October 2027 and October 2028. Your diploma matters more than the business.
- You score a 7 (or A*) in one of Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Economics or English. The worked example uses Maths. If you don't have a 7 in any of these, read "If tutoring isn't you" below.
- Prices are estimates for affluent Mumbai families, and Step 1 tests them. Experienced IB/IGCSE tutors in Mumbai charge ₹2,000–5,000 an hour one-to-one. Your group prices come to about ₹750–920 per student-hour.
- This is general information, not legal or tax advice. The family's CA confirms the tax and GST points.

## Step 0: Set up in one evening (week of 19 Oct 2026)

1. **WhatsApp Business** (free): one group per tutoring group, with the parents in it.
2. **Google Calendar and one Google Sheet** (free). The sheet has these columns: student, parent, product, grade, fee, paid date, sessions held. A cell at the top shows the **financial-year running total of fees**.
3. **Paid video plan** (Zoom Pro or Google Workspace, about ₹1,000–2,000 a month). Free plans cut group calls at 40–60 minutes, so they won't work. Teach online only, because Mumbai traffic would eat your hours.
4. **A separate savings account with UPI**, which a minor aged 10 or older can operate, with a parent as guardian or joint holder.
5. **Write down your grade baseline.** For each of your 6 DP subjects, record the latest grade your school has reported, such as your first DP1 unit-test or report grade, on the school's own scale. The stop rule below uses these numbers.

Don't add a logo, a website, a company or an app.

## Step 1: Test whether anyone will pay (19 Oct – 30 Nov 2026)

**The product:** a group of 3 Grade 9–10 students meeting online twice a week for 1 hour, on Mon and Thu, at **₹6,500 a month per student, paid in advance on the 1st**. The fee covers every scheduled session that month, which is 8 or 9 sessions, or fewer when the school is on holiday. Missed sessions are not refunded unless the family gives 24 hours' notice.

**Send this to 20 families** (your parents' friends, neighbours, and your school's MYP parents):

> "Hi Aunty/Uncle, I'm [Name], in DP1 at [School]. I scored a 7 in MYP5 Maths. I'm starting a small online group for Grade 9–10 Maths: 3 students, Mon and Thu 6–7 pm, ₹6,500/month, paid in advance. The first session is free with no obligation. Would [child] like to try it? We start Mon 9 Nov."

**The decision rule:**
- If 3 families pay by 30 Nov, go on to Step 2.
- If they don't, change one thing (the subject, the grade or the time slot) and message 20 new families.
- If you still have no paying families by 31 Jan 2027, switch to a different skill (see "If tutoring isn't you").

In the sheet, record each child's grade (9 or 10). The Grade 10s will leave in May.

## Step 2: Teach the groups yourself (Dec 2026 – Apr 2027)

Grow to **3 groups (9 students)**. That is 6 hours of teaching plus 1 hour of admin a week, which is the most you should take on in DP1. Demand peaks January to May, before eAssessment and IGCSE.

**Revenue:** 9 × ₹6,500 = **₹58,500 a month**. All of it is profit except about ₹1,500 of software. There is no GST at this size.

**The monthly routine:**
1. On the 25th, send a payment reminder.
2. On the 1st, collect the fees.
3. Teach the sessions.
4. Log each session in the sheet.
5. Ask every family that has stayed past month 2 for one referral.

**In March 2027**, message every Grade 10 family to offer the bridge course in Step 3. These are the students who would otherwise just leave.

## Step 3: Bridge course, then a second product (May – June 2027; check your school's exam calendar)

**"Bridge to DP Maths AA/AI":** 12 sessions of 2 hours each, in 2 batches of 8 students, at **₹15,000 per student**.
- **Who fills it:** your own leaving Grade 10s (about 5), plus about 11 new students from referrals and school parents.
- **Gross:** 16 × ₹15,000 = **₹2,40,000**.
- **Cost:** a helper tutor teaches one batch, 24 hrs × ₹1,000 = ₹24,000.
- **Profit:** about **₹2,16,000**.

On the last session of each batch, offer the **second product: a DP1 Maths group** of 3 students, twice a week, at **₹8,000 a month all-in**. Students on it stay through DP1 and DP2, so for two years, which means far less churn than Grade 9–10. **Assume half the bridge students sign up (8 students).** Then hire the helper tutor as your first DP tutor.

DP1 students are a year below you, so they are not in your own cohort. That is allowed, under the integrity rules below.

## Step 4: Ramp up and hand over the teaching (June – November 2027)

**Tutors.** Hire tutors only when you have a waitlist of 3 students, enough to fill a group. You will need 5 tutors by November, each running at most 3 groups.
- **Who to hire:** your school's IB alumni at Mumbai colleges or on gap years who scored a **7 at DP level** in the subject. They can teach both products.
- **How to check them:** watch one demo session and get one teacher reference.
- **Pay rates:** **₹1,000 an hour for Grade 9–10 groups and ₹1,200 an hour for DP groups**.
- **When to pay:** by UPI on the 5th, after families have paid on the 1st. Never pay a tutor from fees you haven't collected yet.

**Students.** You need about **27 new students** on top of the 4 Grade 9s you kept and the 8 bridge converts:
- **Outreach:** your Step 1 test converted about 15% (3 of 20), so plan on about **180 messages** over 6 months, roughly 8 a week.
- **Referrals:** one referral asked from every bridge family.
- **Tutors' networks:** each new tutor introduces the group to their own school's parent network.

**Your time:** about **5 hours a week from June to November 2027**, covering 2 hours of outreach, 2 hours of scheduling and fees, and 1 hour of quality drop-ins. You teach nothing yourself after June. From December this falls to **3 hours a week**.

## Step 5: GST, from the first week of December 2027

The running total crosses ₹15 lakh in December 2027 and ₹20 lakh in February 2028. Ask the CA to **register for GST in the first week of December 2027**. That is DP2 term 1, before your mocks, and the CA does the filing. Your only part is exporting the sheet on the 1st of every month.

- **Whose PAN to register under:** the CA decides. Use your own PAN, with a guardian acting for you, or the PAN of a parent who has **no existing GST-registered business**. Never add this business to a PAN that already has a GSTIN. GST turnover is counted per PAN, so you would owe 18% from the first rupee.
- **Prices stay the same:** ₹6,500 and ₹8,000 are quoted as **all-inclusive**. The 18% GST comes out of your margin, so families see no price rise.

**The steady state from December 2027 (13 groups: 6 Grade 9–10, 7 DP):**

| Line | Monthly |
|---|---|
| Fees: 18 × ₹6,500 + 21 × ₹8,000 | ₹2,85,000 |
| GST included in fees (18/118) | −₹43,475 |
| Gr 9–10 tutors: 6 groups × 9 hrs × ₹1,000 | −₹54,000 |
| DP tutors: 7 groups × 9 hrs × ₹1,200 | −₹75,600 |
| Software and no-show buffer | −₹7,000 |
| **Profit after GST, before income tax** | **≈ ₹1,05,000** |

**Income tax on that profit:**
- **While you are a minor:** the profit is clubbed with a parent's income (explained under Tax below) and taxed at their top rate, about 36%. The family keeps **about ₹67,000 a month**.
- **From your 18th birthday:** the profit is your own income, about ₹12.6 lakh a year. On the new-regime slabs with marginal relief, the tax is about ₹62,000 a year, leaving **about ₹1,00,000 a month in hand**. That equals a ₹12–13 LPA job.

**If you fall short:** each Grade 9–10 group adds about ₹7,500 a month after GST and tutor pay, and each DP group adds about ₹9,500. If you stop at 8 groups, turnover stays close to ₹20 lakh, so do the GST sum with the CA before registering.

## Step 6: Your finals, the May churn, and making it formal (2028)

- **March to May 2028:** take no new students. Your work drops to about 1 hour a week of fee collection.
- **May 2028:** about 9 Grade 10 students leave, so profit dips by about ₹22,000 a month. The 21 DP students stay, because they are now in DP2 and sit their exams in May 2029.
  - In March 2028, choose your best tutor as **lead tutor** at ₹1,500 an hour to run the 2028 bridge course.
  - The bridge course refills the DP1 groups by August 2028, just as Step 3 did in 2027.
- **After you turn 18:**
  - Register as a sole proprietor with an Udyam registration, and later an LLP if you want one.
  - Move the GST registration to your own PAN if it isn't there already.
  - Sign the tutor agreements yourself.
  - Family money can fund expansion from this point, but only for a business that has already proved itself.

## Rules you must not skip

**Contracts.** A contract with a minor is void in India (Indian Contract Act, s.11). So keep all fees prepaid, which means you never have to enforce payment. Anything that needs a signature, such as tutor agreements or a card subscription, is signed by a parent until you turn 18.

**Tax.** A minor's income is normally added to the income of the parent who earns more. This is the clubbing rule: s.64(1A) of the 1961 Act, carried into the Income-tax Act 2025. Income from the minor's **own skill** is not clubbed. Your own teaching in Steps 1–3 probably counts as skill income, but profit earned from other tutors' teaching probably doesn't.
- **Until you turn 18:** set aside 36% of the Step 4–5 profit.
- **The year you turn 18:** the CA splits that year's income at your birthday.

**GST.** Private tutoring is taxable at 18% and is not exempt as education. Follow Step 5. Never let the running total pass ₹20 lakh without a registration.

**IB academic integrity.** Never write, edit or "fix" anyone's IA, EE or TOK work. Teach concepts only. Never tutor anyone in your own DP cohort, and make every tutor follow the same rules. A malpractice finding could cost you your own diploma.

**CAS.** Paid work does not count toward CAS, so keep the two separate.

**Safeguarding.**
- Teach online and in groups only.
- Keep parents in every WhatsApp group.
- Have no private one-to-one chats with students.
- Make tutors follow the same rules.

## Stop rules

- **Grades:** if any school report shows a subject one level below its Step 0 baseline on the school's own scale, halve your business hours until the next report shows it back.
- **Demand:** if you don't have 3 paying students by 30 Nov 2026, change the offer. If you still have none by 31 Jan 2027, change the skill.
- **Hiring:** add a tutor only when 3 students are waiting. Never hire first and look for students afterwards.
- **Time:** if the business needs more than 5 hours a week in DP2, stop taking new students until it needs less.

## If tutoring isn't you

Keep the same skeleton:
- a skill you already have
- sold to people who already know you
- prepaid monthly
- quoted GST-inclusive
- scaled only by hiring once there is a waitlist

Change only the skill:

| Skill | First offer | Price | How it scales |
|---|---|---|---|
| Python / no-code / AI tools | Automate one task (invoices, WhatsApp replies, reports) for businesses your parents know | ₹25,000 setup + ₹5,000/month | 15–20 clients, then a college student builds |
| Video editing | Reels/Shorts for Mumbai doctors, CAs, restaurants | ₹20,000/month for 12 videos | 2–3 editors at ₹8,000–10,000 each; you keep the clients |
| Design / Canva | Monthly social posts for local shops and clinics | ₹12,000/month | Same as above |

## What to do with the $8 million

Almost nothing. Take no more than ₹25,000 from the family, and this plan needs less than that.

Avoid a café, a franchise or a D2C brand funded by the family:
- You can't legally sign the leases and supplier contracts it needs.
- It would take more time than DP allows.
- It would teach you less than earning your first ₹58,500 a month from nothing.

The family's real advantages are three things:
- its **network**, which is where your first 20 messages and 180 outreach messages go
- its **CA**, who handles the clubbing and GST
- a **safety net**, which is what lets you try this at all

Once the business is proven and you are 18, family capital can help it grow.
