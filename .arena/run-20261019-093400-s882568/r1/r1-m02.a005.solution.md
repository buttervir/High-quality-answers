# A Business Plan for a 16-Year-Old IB DP1 Student in Mumbai

## The answer in one paragraph

Start a **tutoring business for IGCSE and MYP students in Mumbai, run in your own name. Begin by tutoring yourself, then turn it into a small online agency of adult IB-graduate tutors.** It suits the rules a 16-year-old in India has to work under:
- It needs almost no capital, so the family money cannot be lost in it.
- Indian tax law clearly treats income from your own skill as yours. Agency income earned before 18 is less clear, and the plan handles that below.
- You can run it in 5–6 hours a week alongside DP1.
- Prepaid packages solve the problem that a minor can't enforce a contract.
- Mumbai has a large, well-paying IB/IGCSE market, and you already know it from the inside.

Realistic targets:
- **₹25,000–30,000 a month by the end of DP1**, tutoring yourself.
- **₹1 lakh+ a month in margin after GST, by about age 18–19**, once 7–10 adult tutors work through you.

₹1 lakh a month is "job-replacing" money in Mumbai (definition below). Every number is shown with its working or marked as an assumption, so you can check it.

---

## Assumptions (things the request does not say)

1. **"Job-replacing money" means ₹1,00,000 a month of profit after GST** (₹12 lakh a year). That is above a typical fresher graduate salary in Mumbai, which is roughly ₹4–8 lakh a year (*assumption: market estimate, not a cited figure*). If you change the target, the maths below scales in a straight line.
2. **The family will back the plan legally but won't treat the business as a place to put large sums.** Backing it legally means a parent signs as guardian. At about ₹88/USD (*assumption*), US$8M is roughly ₹70 crore. Money is not the constraint. Inexperience is.
3. **The student is reasonably strong academically**, with a predicted IB score of 36+ or a 7 in at least one subject. If not, see "If tutoring doesn't fit you" below.
4. **The aim is income the student earns, not income from the family's wealth.** A 4% return on ₹70 crore already replaces a job many times over, so that route answers a different question.
5. **Laws are as they stood in late 2025.** India's **Income-tax Act, 2025 replaces the 1961 Act from 1 April 2026** and renumbers its sections. The 1961 section numbers are quoted below because most guidance still uses them. A CA confirms them in step 0.4.

## The facts this plan rests on (check each one)

| # | Fact | Source | What it means for the plan |
|---|------|--------|------------------------|
| F1 | A minor (under 18) is not competent to contract. A contract with a minor is void. | Indian Contract Act 1872, s.11; *Mohori Bibee v Dharmodas Ghose* (1903) | You can't enforce a deal against a client, or against a tutor who is a minor. So **take payment in advance**, have a parent sign anything that needs signing, and **only engage tutors aged 18+**. |
| F2 | A minor cannot be a member or nominee of a One Person Company. A minor can only be "admitted to the benefits" of a partnership, not be a full partner. | Companies (Incorporation) Rules 2014, Rule 3; Indian Partnership Act 1932, s.30 | **No company or LLP for now.** Trade as yourself, as an informal sole proprietorship, until you turn 18. |
| F3 | A minor's income is added to the income of whichever parent has the higher income. The exception is income from **"manual work or any activity involving application of his skill, talent or specialised knowledge and experience."** | Income-tax Act 1961, s.64(1A) and its proviso | **Your own tutoring** is skill income, so it is taxed as yours. **Margin you earn on other tutors' hours before 18 may not count as skill income.** It could be added to a parent's income and taxed at the top rate. See "Tax while you are under 18". |
| F4 | Under the new tax regime (FY 2025-26), a resident individual pays **zero tax on taxable income up to ₹12 lakh**, because of the s.87A rebate. | Finance Act 2025 | Your own tutoring income, and all income earned after 18, up to the ₹1 lakh/month target, should carry **nil income tax**. File a return anyway. |
| F5 | A supplier of services must register for GST once **aggregate turnover passes ₹20 lakh** in a financial year in Maharashtra. Turnover means gross receipts, not margin. Private coaching that doesn't lead to a recognised qualification is **not exempt** and is charged at 18%. | CGST Act 2017, s.22; Notification 12/2017-CT(Rate) (education exemption covers only recognised qualifications) | At Month 18, turnover is about ₹17 lakh a year, which is below the limit but close to it. **In Phase 3 it goes above the limit, so GST is built into the Phase 3 maths.** |
| F6 | Since April 2025, RBI lets **minors over 10** open and **operate** savings and term deposit accounts themselves, within limits the bank sets. | RBI circular, 21 April 2025 | You can collect UPI payments into **your own** account. |
| F7 | Adolescents aged 14–18 may work in non-hazardous occupations. | Child and Adolescent Labour (Prohibition and Regulation) Act 1986, as amended 2016 | Tutoring and running a small agency are legal. |
| F8 | Processing a child's personal data needs **verifiable parental consent**. | Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and Rules 2025 | Get a parent's consent on the sign-up form and collect only the data you need. |
| F9 | Anyone who knows of, or suspects, a sexual offence against a child **must report it** to the police or the Special Juvenile Police Unit. Failing to report is itself an offence. | POCSO Act 2012, s.19 and s.21 | Your safeguarding rules need a written incident procedure (see "Safeguarding rules"). |
| F10 | The IB DP core includes **CAS** over 18 months, the Extended Essay (4,000 words) and the TOK essay (1,600 words). | IB DP programme documentation | Your time is the real limit. Free peer tutoring can count as CAS **Service**, but paid work cannot. Keep the two separate. |

## The plan

### Phase 0: Setup (weeks 1–2, about 6 hours in total)

| Step | Exact action | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| 0.1 | Get a **PAN** in your own name, with a parent as representative. Apply using Form 49A on the Protean or UTIITSL website. | About ₹107 |
| 0.2 | Open **your own savings account with UPI** (F6), used only for the business. | ₹0 |
| 0.3 | Sign a one-page **"Founder Agreement"** with the family. It covers:<br>• a parent acting as guardian signatory<br>• the seed fund<br>• the time cap<br>• the tax reserve<br>• the stop rules | ₹0 |
| 0.4 | Book **one meeting with a CA**. Ask these questions, in this order:<br>**(1)** Before I turn 18, will the agency margin on other tutors' hours be added to my parent's income under s.64(1A), or will it count as my own skill income? Ask for the answer in writing.<br>**(2)** What are the new 2025 Act section numbers and rates?<br>**(3)** How will my return be filed?<br>**(4)** When do I register for GST, and how do I invoice once registered? | About ₹3,000–5,000 (*assumption*) |
| 0.5 | Write the **policy page**, as a one-page PDF and as your website's FAQ. It covers:<br>• fees are prepaid in packages<br>• the 24-hour cancellation rule<br>• the safeguarding rules below<br>• data consent (F8)<br>• no refunds after the 2nd session of a package | ₹0 |
| 0.6 | Set up your tools:<br>• Gmail or Google Workspace<br>• WhatsApp Business<br>• a one-page site on Carrd or Google Sites<br>• Google Calendar booking slots<br>• Google Forms for sign-up and consent<br>• Google Meet, with recording turned on | ₹0–2,000 a year |

**Don't do these yet:**
- company or LLP
- trademark
- GST
- Udyam registration
- Shop & Establishment registration
- paid ads
- an app
- an office

None of these earns money before you have revenue, and F2 blocks some of them.

### Phase 1: Tutor yourself (DP1, months 1–6)

**What you sell:** IGCSE (Grades 9–10) or MYP tutoring in your 2–3 strongest subjects. Maths, sciences and English are the usual choices. You are one or two years ahead of these students, you know their syllabus, and you use the same mark schemes.

**Pricing** (*assumption: check it in week 1 by asking 5 parents and 3 existing tutors near you*):
- One trial session for ₹1,000.
- Then a **10-session package for ₹15,000** (₹1,500 an hour), paid in advance by UPI.
- Sessions are online, or at the student's home with a parent at home (see the safeguarding rules).

**Time cap: 5 hours a week of tutoring and 1 hour of admin.** This is non-negotiable. It allows about 20 paid hours a month once cancellations and holidays are taken out.

**Capacity: 5 students, each on one 1-hour session a week.** That is about 20 hours a month, so each package lasts about 2.5 months.

**Revenue by month 6:** 20 hours × ₹1,500 = **₹30,000 a month**. Expect about ₹25,000 in months with exams or holidays.

**Finding customers, in this order** (all free, starting with people you know):
1. **Week 1:** Message 30 families you know who have children in Grades 7–10 at IB or IGCSE schools. Send one message with your subjects, your grades as proof, the trial price and a booking link.
2. **Week 2:** Ask every trial family for **two referrals**. Give one free session for each referral that becomes a paying student.
3. **Month 2:** Post in the parent WhatsApp groups of 3–4 nearby housing societies. Do it only through a parent who is already a member.
4. **Month 3 onward:** Each month, publish one free resource with your contact details on it, such as a "Grade 10 Chemistry formula sheet" or an "MYP eAssessment checklist".

**Proof of results:**
- Record each student's test scores before and after, with permission.
- Have 3 written parent testimonials by month 4.

**Phase 1 is done when:**
- 5 students are on weekly sessions.
- At least 3 families are on a **waitlist**. They are the demand you hand to your first agency tutors.
- You have 3 or more testimonials.
- Your own IB predicted grades have **not dropped**.

### Phase 2: Become a small online agency (end of DP1 to DP2, months 7–18)

You can't increase your own hours in DP2, but you **can** add other people's hours.

**1. Recruit 3–5 tutors. All must be 18 or over** (F1: their agreement has to be enforceable).
- Look for IB graduates now at Mumbai colleges, with a final score of 38+ and a 7 in the subject they will teach.
- Find them through your school's alumni, college societies and LinkedIn.
- **Don't recruit DP2 students.** They are minors, so you couldn't enforce their agreement.

**2. Vet every tutor before their first session.** The steps are in the safeguarding rules below.

**3. Pay** tutors **₹1,000 an hour**, by UPI from your business account, after each session is done.

**4. Charge** clients **₹1,900 an hour**, sold as a 10-session package for ₹19,000. This price is justified by your testimonials.
- **Margin: ₹900 per tutor-hour.** (*Assumption: check it against what you see in Phase 1.*)

**5. Agency sessions are online only, on Google Meet, recorded with parent consent.** This lets you vet sessions without leaving home, covers all of Mumbai and keeps the safeguarding simple.

**6. Your job: 5 hours a week.** Stop most of your own tutoring.

| Task | Hours a month |
|---|---|
| Matching new students to tutors | 3 |
| Quality check: skim 15 minutes of one recording per tutor per month (5 × 15 min), plus the monthly parent feedback form | 2 |
| Package renewals and billing: about 8 packages a month at 15 minutes each, since billing is per package and not per session | 2 |
| Tutor payouts and updating the CRM | 2 |
| Marketing: one free resource a month plus referral messages | 4 |
| Recruiting replacement tutors (averaged over the year) | 2 |
| Buffer | 6 |
| **Total** | **about 21 (5 hours a week)** |

**7.** Get one parent to sign each **tutor agreement** as guardian of the business. It is one page and covers:
- the rate
- payment after each session
- the safeguarding rules, signed by the tutor
- not poaching your students for 12 months
- data handling (F8)

**8.** Use a **shared Google Sheet as your CRM**, with these columns: student, school, subject, tutor, package start, sessions left, paid (Y/N), latest score, last feedback score.

**Month 18 target:**
- 5 tutors × 15 hours a month = 75 tutor-hours.
- 75 × ₹900 = **₹67,500 a month in margin.**
- Revenue is 75 × ₹1,900 = ₹1,42,500 a month, about ₹17.1 lakh a year. That is below the ₹20 lakh GST limit but close to it.
- **From now on, add up your turnover every month for the financial year (April–March).** Apply for GST registration as soon as it is likely to pass ₹20 lakh. You must register within 30 days of becoming liable.

During the May exams of DP2, reduce your role to billing only. Write down your matching process beforehand so a senior tutor can cover it.

### Tax while you are under 18 (Phases 1–2)

- **Your own tutoring income** is skill income, so it is taxed as yours and is nil up to ₹12 lakh (F3, F4).
- **Agency margin earned before your 18th birthday** might be added to your higher-earning parent's income (F3). With US$8M in family assets, that parent is probably in the top bracket: 30% + surcharge (up to 25% under the new regime) + 4% cess, so up to about 39%.
- **Rule: until the CA confirms in writing that it is your skill income, move 39% of every month's agency margin into a separate "tax reserve" fixed deposit.**
  - At Month 18 that is about ₹26,000 of the ₹67,500.
  - If the CA confirms the income is yours, the reserve is released. Otherwise the parent pays the tax from it.
- Keep **separate columns** in your sheet for "own tutoring" and "agency margin", so the two can be taxed differently if necessary.
- The best approach is not to grow the agency fast before 18. Phases 1–2 build proof and a team. The job-replacing income comes in Phase 3, after 18, when all of it is clearly yours.

### Phase 3: Reach job-replacing income (age 18, after the IB)

**On your 18th birthday:**
1. Convert your bank account from minor to major status.
2. Sign tutor agreements yourself.
3. Register for Udyam (it's free) if you want MSME status.
4. Keep trading as a sole proprietorship under your own name.
5. Move to a Private Limited Company only if you take on a co-founder or an investor, or if your CA advises it because profit has grown large.

**GST is now required:** at Phase 3 volumes, turnover is above ₹20 lakh a year (F5). So you charge 18% GST and file returns through your CA. There are two ways to price:

| | **Safe case: GST absorbed** (client pays a flat ₹2,000 an hour) | **Upside case: GST added on top** (client pays ₹2,000 + 18% = ₹2,360) |
|---|---|---|
| Your revenue per hour before GST | ₹2,000 ÷ 1.18 = ₹1,695 | ₹2,000 |
| Tutor pay per hour | ₹1,000 | ₹1,000 |
| Margin per hour | ₹695 | ₹1,000 |
| Hours a month needed for ₹1 lakh | 145 (145 × ₹695 = ₹1,00,775) | 105 (105 × ₹1,000 = ₹1,05,000) |
| Tutors at 15 hours a month each | 10 | 7 |
| Turnover per year (ex-GST) | about ₹29.5 lakh | about ₹25.2 lakh |

**Plan for the safe case. Treat the upside case as a bonus.** The upside is most realistic for **DP-level tutoring**. That sells for roughly ₹2,500–4,000 an hour (*assumption*), so ₹2,360 including GST is still below the going rate. Your tutors are now graduates, so they can teach DP.

**Two ways to fill the hours:**
1. **Add DP students** as customers, which raises the price and moves you toward the upside case.
2. **Run holiday and exam crash courses for small groups online.** For example, 6 students × ₹1,000 an hour = ₹6,000 an hour, against a tutor cost of ₹1,500. The margin is about ₹3,580 an hour after GST, so a few group hours count as much as many one-to-one hours.

**If you study abroad:** the business can keep running online with Indian tutors. Before you leave, check your residency and tax position with your CA.

## Safeguarding rules (part of every tutor agreement and the policy page)

Your students are aged 11–16, and your business depends on parents trusting it.

**Vetting each tutor before their first session:**
1. **Age and ID:** see Aadhaar or a passport, plus a college ID. They must be 18 or over.
2. **Two references** you call yourself. At least one must be a teacher or professor.
3. **Police character verification certificate.** Ask for a certificate from the Maharashtra Police citizen portal, or a Police Clearance Certificate from Passport Seva (*assumption: confirm the current service name and how long it takes. Allow 2–4 weeks*).
4. **A 20-minute trial lesson with you,** recorded.
5. **Signed safeguarding rules.**

**Session rules, for students of every age:**
- **Agency tutors teach online only,** on the business's Google Meet account. Sessions are recorded with parent consent, and recordings are kept for 90 days, then deleted (F8).
- **Your own in-home sessions** need a parent or another adult at home, with the door open. If that can't be arranged, the session is held online.
- **No private contact:** tutors message only through a WhatsApp group with the parent in it. There is no social media contact and no meeting in person.
- Tutors never see a student's address or the parents' details beyond the parent's first name.

**Incident procedure:**
1. A complaint or concern comes in.
2. The **same day**, the tutor is suspended from all sessions and your guardian parent is told.
3. Within 24 hours, the affected family is contacted and the recording is preserved.
4. If the concern is about a sexual offence, you **must report it to Mumbai Police or the Special Juvenile Police Unit (POCSO s.19)**, and your guardian makes the report with you.
5. Write down each step with the date.

## Rules for using the family's money

Family money helps most by **taking pressure off you**, not by being spent.

1. **Seed fund: up to ₹50,000.** It covers the CA fee, the website, a laptop if you need one, and printing.
   - Treat it as a **gift**. A gift from a relative is tax-free to you (Income-tax Act 1961, s.56(2)(x)).
   - Keep gift money and business income in separate records, because interest on gifted money is added to your parent's income (F3).
2. **No more than ₹2 lakh in total** goes into the business before it has been profitable for 3 months in a row.
3. **What the family's wealth is really for:**
   - removing your need for the income, so you can charge properly and turn down bad clients
   - paying for CA and legal advice
   - introductions to families and schools
4. **Don't use family money to buy growth**, such as paid ads, an app or a leased centre. That is how a 16-year-old with money loses ₹20 lakh learning what a ₹0 test would have shown.

## When to stop or change course (decide these now)

- **After 6 weeks:** fewer than 2 paying students means the problem is your subject or your price, not demand. Switch to your strongest subject and drop the trial price to ₹500.
- **After 3 months:** fewer than 4 weekly students means you should test a different group, such as Grade 6–8 maths or SAT Maths.
- **After 6 months:** if families leave after one package, the problem is your teaching quality or your proof of results. Fix that before you hire anyone.
- **Any time:** if a tutor breaks a safeguarding rule, end their agreement. There is no second chance.

## If tutoring doesn't fit you

The same pattern works for any **skill-based service**:
- no capital needed
- prepaid
- sold first to people you know
- turned into an agency of adults later

This keeps it simple and legal (F1, F2), and it keeps your **own** work taxed as yours (F3). The same caveat about agency margin applies before 18.

Alternatives:
- **Video editing or design** for Mumbai small businesses and creators, at ₹5,000–15,000 a month per retainer client (*assumption*).
- **Small websites and automations** for local businesses.

Avoid inventory-based D2C products, apps that need fundraising, and trading or crypto. They need capital, contracts or legal standing that a minor doesn't have, and the income would mostly be added to your parents' income for tax (F3).
