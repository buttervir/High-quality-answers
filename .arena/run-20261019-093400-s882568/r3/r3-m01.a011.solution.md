# From DP1 to a job's take-home pay: one business, worked out in rupees

## The answer in one paragraph

Start a **small-group online tutoring business** in the one subject where you score a 7. It has two products:
- **Grade 9–10 (MYP 4–5 / IGCSE) groups**, which you teach yourself during DP1.
- **DP1–DP2 groups** for students one year below you. Paid alumni tutors teach these from June 2027.

A two-week **"Bridge to DP" course every May** connects the two products. It runs in the Mumbai summer break, after the March board exams. Each year it turns Grade 10 leavers into DP customers who then stay for two years.

**The target:** 39 students in 14 groups, with 5 tutors and a lead tutor, by November 2027. That gives **about ₹90,000 a month profit after GST and all costs**.
- **Until you turn 18**, the business is legally your parent's (a minor cannot run it; see Step 4). Its profit is taxed at the parent's rate of about 39%, so the family keeps about **₹55,000 a month**. You are paid the tutor rate for the hours you teach yourself.
- **After you turn 18**, the parent transfers the business to you. If your total income stays at or under ₹12 lakh, you pay no tax and keep **about ₹90,000 a month**. A ₹12 LPA Mumbai job takes home about ₹90,000–97,000 a month. If you have other income, see Step 6.
- **Downside case:** if outreach converts half as well as the first test, you reach about 26 students in 9 groups and profit is **about ₹60,000 a month**.

This replaces a job only if about 23 new students join every year, 12 of them through the bridge. The plan has an intake engine and a check every September for exactly that reason. It needs no company, no website and no family capital.

## Assumptions

- **"Job replacing money"** means matching the take-home pay of a ₹12 LPA salary. Under the new regime that salary pays almost no tax (₹75,000 standard deduction plus the s.87A rebate up to ₹12 lakh). After PF it takes home about ₹90,000–97,000 a month.
- **Your school calendar.** Like most Mumbai IB schools, your school starts its year in June and breaks for the summer in May. You started DP1 in June 2026, start DP2 in June 2027 and sit finals in May 2028. You turn 18 between October 2027 and October 2028. Your diploma matters more than the business. If your school runs August to June, move every "May" holiday task below to your actual break.
- **Your subject.** You score a 7 (or an A*) in one of Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Economics or English. The worked example uses Maths. If none of these applies, see "If tutoring isn't you".
- **Exam calendars.** Most Grade 10s you will teach sit their boards in **March**: Cambridge's India-only February/March IGCSE series, ICSE or CBSE. MYP eAssessment is in May and the IGCSE June series ends mid-June. The plan is built on March and handles the others separately.
- **Prices** are estimates for affluent Mumbai families, and Step 1 tests them. Experienced IB/IGCSE tutors in Mumbai charge ₹2,000–5,000 an hour one-to-one. Your group prices work out to about ₹750–920 per student-hour.
- **Churn:** about 3 seats in 42 are empty at any time, so groups average 2.8 students, not 3.
- This is general information, not legal or tax advice. The family's CA confirms every tax and GST point.

## Step 0: Set up in one evening (week of 19 Oct 2026)

1. **WhatsApp Business** (free). Make one group per tutoring group, with the parents in it.
2. **Google Calendar and one Google Sheet** (free).
   - Sheet columns: student, parent, product, grade, **exam series (March / May / June)**, fee, paid date, sessions held, lead source.
   - Three cells at the top: the count of enrolled students (it turns red at **48**; see the cap in "Rules"), the count of new students this academic year, and the GST running total (set up in Step 4).
3. **A paid video plan:** Zoom Pro or Google Workspace, about ₹1,000–2,000 a month. Free plans cut group calls off at 40–60 minutes. Teach online only, because Mumbai traffic would eat your hours.
4. **A minor's savings account with UPI**, with a parent as guardian. RBI rules let a minor aged 10 or over operate one. It is used only until 31 March 2027.
5. **Your grade baseline.** Write down your latest school-reported grade in each of your 6 DP subjects. The stop rules use these numbers.

Do not set up a logo, a website, a company or an app.

## Step 1: Test whether anyone will pay (19 Oct – 30 Nov 2026)

**The product:** a group of 3 Grade 9–10 students, online, twice a week for 1 hour (Mon and Thu). The fee is **₹6,500 a month per student, paid in advance on the 1st**. It covers every scheduled session that month, usually 8–9. A missed session is refunded only if the family gives 24 hours' notice.

**Send this message to 20 families** (your parents' friends, neighbours and your school's MYP parents):

> "Hi Aunty/Uncle, I'm [Name], in DP1 at [School]. I scored a 7 in MYP5 Maths. I'm starting a small online group for Grade 9–10 Maths: 3 students, Mon and Thu 6–7 pm, ₹6,500/month, paid in advance. The first session is free with no obligation. Would [child] like to try it? We start Mon 9 Nov."

**The decision rule on 30 Nov:**

| Paying families | What to do |
|---|---|
| 3 or more | Go to Step 2. |
| 1–2 | Run the group at that size. It is prepaid, so it costs you nothing but 2 hours a week. Keep the slot and fill its empty seats from your next 20 messages. To test a change, open a **second, new slot** that changes one thing (subject, grade or time). Never move families who have already paid. |
| 0 | Change one thing (subject, grade or time) and message 20 new families. |
| Still 0 by 31 Jan 2027 | Change the skill. |

For every child, record their grade and **which exam series they sit**. That decides when their family stops paying.

## Step 2: Teach the groups yourself (Dec 2026 – Mar 2027)

Grow to **3 groups of 9 students** (5 in Grade 10, 4 in Grade 9). That is 6 hours of teaching and 1 hour of admin a week, which is the most you should take on in DP1. Demand peaks from January to March, before the board exams.

**Revenue:** 9 × ₹6,500 = **₹58,500 a month**. All of it is profit except about ₹1,500 for software. There is no GST at this size. This is your own skill income (see Tax).

**The monthly routine:**
1. Send a reminder on the 25th.
2. Collect fees on the 1st.
3. Teach.
4. Log each session.
5. Ask every family that has stayed past month 2 for one referral.

**Grade 10 families pay through the month of their last exam.** March-series families stop after March. May or June-series families stay until then, and they get the June jump-start (Step 3) instead of the bridge.

**Build the lead list (Jan – Mar 2027).** In a second tab, list every household you can reach that has a child in Grade 9 or Grade 10 who takes your subject. Count ICSE and CBSE Grade 10s who plan to join an IB school for DP. Sources:
- your parents' contacts
- your school's parent groups
- building societies
- your future tutors' schools

**You need 200 households.** If you reach fewer than 120, plan for the downside case (9 groups, not 14).

**In January 2027,** offer the bridge to every Grade 10 family and to the lead list. Bookings close 31 March and payment is due by 15 April.

## Step 3: The May bridge, then the DP product (May – June 2027)

**"Bridge to DP Maths AA/AI"**
- **Dates:** **Mon 10 – Sat 22 May 2027**, Mon–Sat, 2 hours a day. That is after the March boards, in the Mumbai summer break, and before DP1 starts in June. Check the dates against your school calendar. If they fall in your term, the helper teaches both batches.
- **Format:** 12 sessions of 2 hours, in 2 batches of 8 students, at **₹15,000 per student**.
- **Who fills it:** about 5 of your own Grade 10 leavers and about 11 from referrals and the lead list.
- **Gross:** 16 × ₹15,000 = **₹2,40,000**, collected by 15 April.
- **Cost:** the helper tutor teaches one batch (24 hours × ₹1,000 = ₹24,000). You teach the other batch and are paid the same ₹24,000 as skill income.
- **Profit:** about **₹1,92,000** for the business.

**May/June-series students, and schools that start DP1 in August:** run the same content as a **"DP1 Maths jump-start"**. It is 6 Saturday sessions of 4 hours in the first 6 weeks of their DP1, at the same price, taught by a tutor.

**On the last session, offer the DP Maths group:**
- 3 students, twice a week, **₹8,000 a month all-in**.
- It starts the week the student's DP1 starts (June 2027 for most) and runs until their exams in May 2029.
- **Assume half the bridge students sign up (8 students).** Hire the helper as your first tutor from June.

These students are one year below you, so they are not in your own cohort. That is allowed under the integrity rules below.

**You stop teaching after the bridge.** From June 2027 (DP2) you only run the business.

## Step 4: Hand over and ramp up (Apr – Nov 2027, in your DP2)

**Legal form, from 1 April 2027.** A minor cannot sign the tutor agreements, the bank mandates or the GST registration. So the business becomes the **sole proprietorship of the parent the CA chooses**.
- **Open a current account** in that parent's name by 31 March. Collect every fee there from 1 April.
- **The profit is that parent's business income**, taxed at their rate, until the business is transferred to you (Step 6).
- **You are paid the tutor rate** (₹1,000 an hour) for every hour you teach: the April–May Grade 9 groups and your bridge batch, about ₹60,000. That is your own skill income, not clubbed with your parent's. File it in your own ITR.

**GST running total (set it up on 1 April).** GST registration becomes compulsory when the PAN's **aggregate turnover** for the financial year passes **₹20 lakh**. That turnover counts **all** of that parent's supplies, including exempt ones such as **residential rent**. So the top cell is:

> fees collected since 1 April **+ the parent's other GST-counted supplies this financial year** (the CA gives you this monthly figure once)

It turns **red at ₹17 lakh**. The CA chooses the parent who has **no existing GSTIN and the least other turnover** (for example, no let-out flats).

**Tutors.**
- **When to hire:** only when 3 students are waiting. You need 5 tutors by November, each running at most 3 groups.
- **Who to hire:** your school's IB alumni at Mumbai colleges or on gap years who scored a **7 at DP level** in the subject.
- **How to vet them:** one 30-minute demo, watched online, and one reference call.
- **Pay:** **₹1,000 an hour for Grade 9–10 groups and ₹1,200 an hour for DP groups**, by UPI on the 5th. Never pay a tutor from fees you haven't collected yet.

**Students.** You need **27 new students** on top of the 4 Grade 9s and the 8 bridge converts. That gives 17 Grade 9–10 students and 22 DP students.
- **Outreach:** message the lead list at about 8 messages a week from June to November (about 180 messages).
- **Referrals:** ask every bridge family and every paying family for one.
- **Tutors' networks:** each tutor introduces you to their own school's parents.
- **Trials:** a free trial is the student joining an existing paid group session, so it costs nobody extra time.

**Your time in DP2, task by task:**

| Task | Hours a week |
|---|---|
| 8 outreach messages and replies | 1.0 |
| Booking trials into existing sessions | 0.5 |
| Fees: broadcast on the 25th, UPI check and sheet on the 1st–3rd, tutor pay on the 5th | 0.5 |
| Hiring 4 tutors over 6 months (1 hour each) | 0.2 |
| One 30-minute quality drop-in | 0.5 |
| WhatsApp groups (tutors post their own schedules) | 0.2 |
| **Total** | **about 3** |

**Pause outreach** in the 2 weeks before school mocks and before each university deadline (for example, 1 November). The ramp just takes longer. If the business still needs more than 5 hours a week, the Time stop rule halts new intake, and you are in the downside case, which is already costed.

**Re-measure on 31 July 2027.** The first test converted 3 of 20 warm contacts, which is too small a sample to plan on. After the first 60 lead-list messages, divide paying students by messages sent:

| Conversion | What to plan for |
|---|---|
| 10% or more | Keep the 14-group target |
| 5–10% | Target 9–11 groups (₹60,000–75,000 a month). Don't stretch. |
| Under 5% | Stop cold outreach. Grow only through referrals and the bridge. Treat this as a ₹35,000–60,000 side income. |

## Step 5: GST

Private tutoring is taxable at 18%. It is not exempt as education.

- **When the red cell lights up,** tell the CA. Registration is due within 30 days of the collection that takes aggregate turnover past ₹20 lakh, and GST is owed from that date.
  - With **no other supplies** on the PAN, that is the **1 January 2028** collection.
  - With **₹6 lakh of residential rent**, it is the **1 November 2027** collection.
  - The red cell gives a month's warning in both cases. Your only job is to export the sheet on the 1st.
- **Prices don't change.** ₹6,500 and ₹8,000 stay all-inclusive, and the GST comes out of your margin.
- From April 2028, every financial year starts above the threshold, so the business stays registered.

**Steady state from November 2027** (39 students in 14 groups: 6 Grade 9–10 groups, 8 DP groups, 3 seats empty):

| Line | Monthly |
|---|---|
| Fees: 17 × ₹6,500 + 22 × ₹8,000 | ₹2,86,500 |
| GST included in fees (18/118) | −₹43,700 |
| Gr 9–10 tutors: 6 groups × 9 hrs × ₹1,000 | −₹54,000 |
| DP tutors: 8 groups × 9 hrs × ₹1,200 | −₹86,400 |
| Software and no-show buffer | −₹7,000 |
| Lead tutor coordination fee (from Jan 2028) | −₹5,000 |
| **Profit after GST, before income tax** | **≈ ₹90,000** |

**Each full group of 3 contributes** about ₹7,500 a month (Grade 9–10) or about ₹9,500 (DP) after GST and tutor pay.

**Over a year:** in April–May, March-series Grade 10s and the DP2 cohort leave before the new intake starts. That dip costs about ₹0.8 lakh. The May bridge (Step 6) earns about ₹2.1 lakh after GST and tutors. So a full year comes to roughly **₹11–12 lakh**.

**Two weaker cases:**

| Case | Students | Fees | GST | Tutors | Other costs | Profit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Downside:** outreach at about 7% | 26 in 9 groups (11 Gr 9–10 in 4 groups, 15 DP in 5 groups) | ₹1,91,500 | ₹29,200 | ₹90,000 | ₹12,000 | **≈ ₹60,000** |
| **DP-intake shortfall:** bridge yields only 8 DP1s a year | 16 DP students in 6 groups | ₹2,38,500 | ₹36,400 | ₹1,18,800 | ₹12,000 | **≈ ₹71,000** |

## Step 6: The yearly intake engine (from 2028, every year)

**Why it matters:** every year three groups of seats empty out. To hold 39 students you need **about 23 new students a year**, and **12 of them must be DP1s**:

| Seats to refill | New students | Where they come from |
|---|---|---|
| Grade 10s who leave (about half of 17) | 9 | Referrals from about 39 families (8–10 new Grade 9s), plus 2 hours a week of outreach from January to September |
| DP2 cohort who leave (about half of 22) | 11 | **The bridge at 24 seats** (3 batches of 8). About half convert, so 12 new DP1s. |
| Mid-year drop-outs | 3 | Both of the above |
| **Total** | **≈ 23** | |

**Bridge gate, 31 March every year.**

| Bridge bookings | What to do |
|---|---|
| 20 or more | Run 3 batches. |
| 16–19 | Run 2 batches. Add jump-start seats in June. |
| Under 16 | Expect DP to settle near 16 students and profit near ₹71,000. Do not hire for 22. |

The 2028 bridge grosses 24 × ₹15,000 = ₹3.6 lakh. After about ₹55,000 GST and about ₹94,000 tutor pay (lead tutor at ₹1,500 an hour plus two DP tutors), it leaves **about ₹2.1 lakh**.

**Lead tutor, from January 2028.** Pick your best tutor and pay them a **₹5,000 a month coordination fee** on top of their teaching. This cost is already in the steady-state table. They:
- run bridge bookings and the waitlist
- do new-tutor demos and trials
- teach the lead bridge batch at ₹1,500 an hour

This frees you for your finals. From March to May 2028 you take no new students and do only about 1 hour a week of fees. You keep the 2 hours a week of outreach from June to September every year, including from university.

**Intake check, 30 September every year:**

| Students enrolled | What to do |
|---|---|
| 36 or more | On target |
| 30–35 | Merge part-filled groups so every group has 3. Don't replace tutors who leave. |
| Under 30 | You are in the downside case. Hold at that size, stop hiring, and consider one more channel, such as a second subject with a tutor who already has students waiting. |

**The 2028–29 bump.** The first DP cohort (22 students) is about twice a normal year's intake. From June 2028 you run about 34 DP students: 22 in DP2 plus 12 new DP1s.
- **Cap Grade 9–10 at 16 that year so total enrolment stays at 50 or below.** See the cap in "Rules".
- Profit rises above target. **Treat 2028–29 as a bonus year and don't spend it as income.**
- In May 2029, the 22 DP2 students leave together and the business falls back to about 39 students, provided the 2029 bridge gate passed.

**At 18: transfer the business to yourself.** A GSTIN is tied to a PAN and cannot be moved. The CA does this in the month after your birthday:
1. You open a current account in your own name.
2. Your parent transfers the business to you as a going concern under a short written deed, at nil consideration. A gift from a parent is not taxable to you, and a going-concern transfer is exempt from GST.
3. You apply for your own GST registration effective from the transfer date. The transferee of a business must register from that date (CGST s.22(3)). Your parent then cancels their registration.
4. You sign new tutor agreements and get an Udyam registration. You can form an LLP later if you want one.
5. From the transfer date the profit is your own income. The CA splits that financial year at the transfer date.

**Your tax after the transfer:**
- **If your total income is ₹12 lakh or less,** the s.87A rebate means **no tax**. You keep about ₹90,000 a month.
- **If you have other income:** once you are 18, interest and dividends on investments in your name are no longer clubbed with your parent's. They add to your total. Example: ₹10.8 lakh of business profit plus ₹3 lakh of interest makes ₹13.8 lakh. The tax is about ₹91,000 a year, so you keep about **₹83,000 a month**. Either ask the family whether those investments should stay in your name, or accept the tax.
- **If total income lands just over ₹12 lakh,** marginal relief caps the tax at the amount above ₹12 lakh.
- **If you study abroad and become a non-resident,** the rebate stops applying. Tax on ₹10.8 lakh is then about ₹50,000 a year, and you keep about ₹86,000 a month.

Family money can now fund expansion, but only for a business that has already proved itself.

## Rules you must not skip

**Contracts.** A contract with a minor is void in India (Indian Contract Act, s.11). So:
- **Steps 1–3 (to March 2027):** keep every fee prepaid, so you never need to enforce payment.
- **From April 2027:** the parent's proprietorship signs everything (tutor agreements, the current account, the video plan) until the transfer.

**Tax.**
- **Clubbing.** A minor's income is normally added to the income of the parent who earns more. This is s.64(1A) of the 1961 Act, carried into the Income-tax Act 2025. Income from the minor's **own skill** is not clubbed.
- **Your own income:** about ₹2.3 lakh of teaching in FY 2026–27 and about ₹60,000 of tutor pay in FY 2027–28. **File your own ITR for both years**, signed by a guardian. The tax is nil, but filing shows the income is yours.
- **The business profit** from April 2027 is the parent's own business income. Set aside the parent's rate (about 39%: 30% plus a 25% surcharge plus 4% cess for income above ₹2 crore) until the transfer.

**GST.** Track turnover on the parent's PAN, including other supplies such as rent (Step 4). Never let it pass ₹20 lakh without the CA acting within 30 days.

**Enrolment cap: 50 students, never more.** The Ministry of Education's 2024 Guidelines for Regulation of Coaching Centres cover centres that coach more than 50 students. They require registration and graduate tutors, and they bar students under 16. How far they bind depends on Maharashtra's rules (ask the CA or a lawyer). Staying at 50 or below keeps you outside the definition. The sheet's red cell at 48 is your warning.

**IB academic integrity.**
- Never write, edit or "fix" anyone's IA, EE or TOK. Teach concepts only.
- Never tutor anyone in your own DP cohort.
- Make every tutor follow the same rules.

A malpractice finding could cost you your own diploma.

**CAS.** Paid work does not count toward CAS, so keep the two separate.

**Safeguarding.**
- Online and in groups only.
- Parents are in every WhatsApp group.
- No private one-to-one chats with students.
- Tutors follow the same rules.

## Stop rules

- **Grades:** if any school report shows a subject one level below its Step 0 baseline, halve your business hours until the next report shows it back.
- **Demand:** use the 30 November table in Step 1. If you have 0 payers by 31 Jan 2027, change the skill.
- **Conversion:** on 31 July 2027, resize the target with the table in Step 4.
- **Bridge:** every 31 March, use the bridge gate in Step 6.
- **Intake:** every 30 September, use the intake table in Step 6.
- **Hiring:** add a tutor only when 3 students are waiting.
- **Size:** never enrol more than 50 students.
- **Time:** if the business needs more than 5 hours a week in DP2, stop taking new students until it needs less.

## If tutoring isn't you

Keep the same skeleton:
- a skill you already have
- sold to people who already know you
- prepaid monthly and priced GST-inclusive
- in a parent's proprietorship until you turn 18, then transferred to you
- scaled only by hiring against a waitlist
- refilled by a yearly intake check

Change only the skill:

| Skill | First offer | Price | How it scales |
|---|---|---|---|
| Python / no-code / AI tools | Automate one task (invoices, WhatsApp replies, reports) for businesses your parents know | ₹25,000 setup + ₹5,000/month | 15–20 clients, then a college student builds |
| Video editing | Reels/Shorts for Mumbai doctors, CAs and restaurants | ₹20,000/month for 12 videos | 2–3 editors at ₹8,000–10,000 each; you keep the clients |
| Design / Canva | Monthly social posts for local shops and clinics | ₹12,000/month | Same as above |

## What to do with the $8 million

Almost nothing. Take no more than ₹25,000 from the family, because this plan needs less than that.

Avoid a café, a franchise or a family-funded D2C brand:
- You can't legally sign the leases and supplier contracts they need.
- It would take more time than DP allows.
- It would teach you less than earning your first ₹58,500 a month from nothing.

The family's real advantages are:
- **its network**, where your first 20 messages and your 200-household lead list come from
- **its CA**, who handles GST, the proprietorship, your ITR and the transfer at 18
- **a parent's name and signature** on the business until you are 18
- **a safety net**, which is what lets you try this at all

Once the business has passed two intake checks and is in your name, family capital can help it grow.
