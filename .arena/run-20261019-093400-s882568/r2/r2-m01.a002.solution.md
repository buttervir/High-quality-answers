# A 16-year-old DP1 student in Mumbai earning job-replacing money: the plan

## The short answer

Don't start by asking the family for a big cheque. Start a **skill-based service business with almost no capital**: small-group tutoring first, then a digital set-up service for local shops. Over DP1 and the summer, **turn the tutoring into a small agency**. Trained younger students teach the groups and you keep a margin. That is the only way a student capped at 5–8 hours a week can earn job-level money **every month**, not just in one good holiday month.

**The target ("job-replacing" line):** **₹50,000 founder net income in each of 3 consecutive months**, with at least ₹35,000 of it from recurring monthly fees. The plan aims to hit it in **August–October 2027** (the start of DP2) at about 13 hours a month. It is built so that income can continue while you work **zero hours** during your final exams (January–May 2028). Wait until you turn 18 to set up a company and take serious family money, and only after this test has passed.

Every part of this plan was chosen because it should still be safe if things go wrong. The obvious plan ("you're rich, so put ₹50 lakh into a brand") fails quietly and expensively. This one fails early, cheaply and visibly, and it stops itself through written tripwires.

---

## Assumptions (check these, because the plan changes if they're wrong)

1. **"Job-replacing money" means at least ₹50,000 a month, every month, mostly recurring.** That is about what a Mumbai fresher takes home. One big month followed by thin ones doesn't count, because a job pays every month. You have no job to replace yet, so treat it as proof that you can earn on your own, not as money to live on.
2. **The ~$8M (about ₹70 crore at ~₹88/$) belongs to your parents, not to you.** You can't legally commit it, and nothing below assumes you can.
3. **School calendar:** DP1 started around August 2026. **The summer break is June–July. DP2 term starts in August 2027.** DP1 end-of-year exams are around May 2027, mocks around January 2028, and finals in May 2028. That is the usual pattern for Mumbai IB schools. If your school runs differently, shift every date by the same amount. **The calendar near the end of this document is the single source of dates. If any step seems to disagree with it, the calendar wins.**
4. **You'll probably apply to university, possibly abroad.** This matters a lot (see Step 9).
5. **Your parents are supportive and willing to sign things.** If they aren't, stop at Step 1 and sort that out first, because nothing legal works without them.
6. **The laws cited are as I understand them in October 2026.** India's Income-tax Act, 2025 took effect on 1 April 2026 and renumbered many sections. Treat every tax and legal point as **a question to confirm with a Chartered Accountant (CA) before you act**.

## Why the obvious plan is wrong

**The obvious plan:** "With $8M behind you, take ₹25–50 lakh from the family and launch a D2C brand, café, clothing label or franchise. Use the money to move fast."

| Problem | What actually happens |
|---|---|
| **A minor's contract is void.** Under the Indian Contract Act, 1872 (s.11, *Mohori Bibee v Dharmodas Ghose*, 1903), an agreement with a minor is void, not just hard to enforce. | Leases, supplier deals, marketplace seller accounts and loans all have to be in a parent's name. It becomes your parents' business with you as the face of it. |
| **You can't be a company director or an LLP designated partner** until you're 18. | Any "company" set up now is legally run by your parents. |
| **Tax clubbing.** A minor's income is generally added to a parent's income (old Act s.64(1A), carried into the 2025 Act). Income from the minor's *own skill* is the exception. | Profit from a capital-funded business is taxed at your parent's slab, probably about 30%+. |
| **Capital hides bad ideas.** With unlimited runway, a business nobody wants can survive for 2 years on family money. | You learn to spend money, not to make it. |
| **IB workload.** The EE, TOK, 6 IAs, CAS and 6 subjects all land in DP1 and DP2. | Inventory, staff or premises take 20+ hours a week. Your predicted grades fall just when universities look at them. |
| **Reputation.** In Mumbai's tight social circles, "rich kid's failed café" follows you around. | The losses go beyond money. |

**What survives from the obvious plan:** the family wealth is a real advantage, just not as **capital**. It works as **a safety net** (you can take risks with your time) and as **access** (advice and introductions). Use it that way until 18.

### The three approaches I compared

- **A. Capital-first** (fund a product or brand with family money). Rejected for the reasons above.
- **B. Investment income** (the family puts ₹8–10 crore into a portfolio in your name, which yields "job-replacing" returns). Rejected as the main path. You build nothing, the income is clubbed with your parents' anyway, and it is their money dressed up as yours. **A small version is kept** as a learning portfolio (Step 7).
- **C. Skill-first micro-business that grows into a small agency.** **Chosen.** Services are paid for before delivery, so you never have to enforce a contract. Income from your own skill is the stated exception to clubbing. The hours can be capped. And once others deliver the service, it can earn without your time. That last point is what makes monthly job-level income possible during IB.

The rest of this document develops C.

## The plan, step by step

### STEP 1: Set up the legal, tax and family foundations (Weeks 1–3, about 6 hours in total)

1. **Get a PAN in your own name.** Use Form 49A (Protean/NSDL or UTIITSL) with a parent as representative. You need Aadhaar, a birth certificate or Class 10 marksheet, and a photo.
2. **Open a bank account in your own name**, separate from any account your parents run for you. Since RBI's April 2025 directions, banks may let minors aged 10+ operate savings accounts themselves within limits the bank sets. Ask for a **minor account you operate, with UPI and a debit card**, with your parent as guardian. **All business money goes in and out of this account and nowhere else.**
3. **Get a free UPI handle and an invoice template** (Google Docs or Zoho Invoice's free tier). Number every invoice (INV-001, INV-002, ...).
4. **Book one paid CA meeting (about ₹3,000–₹10,000) and ask exactly these questions:**
   - "Will my income from tutoring and digital services count as income from my own *skill, talent or specialised knowledge*, so it isn't clubbed with my parent's income? What's the section number in the new Act?"
   - "**Once other students teach my groups and I keep a margin, is that margin still skill income, or will it be clubbed?**" (It probably will be. That's fine, but know it in advance.)
   - "For digital-services quotes, a parent will be the named contracting party (see point 5). Does that make the income my parent's, and how should we document that the profit is mine?"
   - "Must I file an ITR in my own name, and from what income? Do I need GST registration?" (The service threshold in Maharashtra is generally ₹20 lakh of turnover, far above this plan. Confirm it.)
   - **If the CA says income will be clubbed**, the plan still works. The money just shows up on a parent's return. Record in writing that the post-tax profit belongs to you.
5. **Contracts, stated correctly.** Paying up front does **not** make a minor's deal legally valid. What it does is make the deal *self-executing*: the money arrives before the work, so nobody ever needs to sue. Use two rules:
   - **Tutoring** (small monthly amounts paid in advance by the student's parent): send a one-page terms sheet issued by you **with your parent named as guardian**.
   - **Digital-services kits and retainers** (₹2,500–₹18,000, sold to businesses): the written quote names **your parent as the contracting party, acting for your venture**. Your parent signs it, and payment goes to your business account.
   - **Refund reserve:** advance fees for work not yet delivered **stay in the account untouched** until delivered. Every refund this plan promises can then always be paid.
6. **Write and sign a one-page Family Venture Agreement** with your parents. This is the most important defensive step.

> **Family Venture Agreement: [Name], [Date]**
> 1. **Capital.** The family funds at most **₹50,000 in DP1** and **₹1,00,000 in total before [Name]'s 18th birthday**. Each release needs a written request naming what it buys.
> 2. **No guarantees.** No parent signs a loan, lease or supplier credit for the venture before [Name] turns 18. A parent signs client quotes only as described in the venture's contract rules.
> 3. **Milestones that unlock more:** ₹10,000 of revenue from strangers (not relatives or family friends) unlocks the next ₹25,000. Passing the job-replacing test (Step 5) unlocks a meeting about post-18 funding.
> 4. **Grade tripwire.**
>    - *Measure:* the school-reported grade in each subject on the **IB 1–7 scale**.
>    - *Baseline:* there are two stages.
>      - **(a) Provisional (Week 1):** each subject teacher, asked by email, gives the IB grade [Name] is "currently working at". If a teacher won't, use the average of the first DP unit tests, marked on the school's own DP grade boundaries. **Grade 10 board results (IGCSE/ICSE/MYP) are never converted and never used.**
>      - **(b) Final:** the first DP1 report on the 1–7 scale (usually December 2026) replaces the provisional baseline. From DP2, official predicted grades replace term grades.
>    - *Rule:* if any subject falls by 1 or more from baseline, venture hours are halved for 6 weeks. If two subjects fall, or the 6-subject total falls by 3 or more, the venture pauses until the next report.
>    - *While the baseline is provisional:* any written teacher concern about missed work or falling standards triggers the "halve hours" rule.
> 4a. **Deadline tripwire (checked weekly).** Any missed internal deadline for an IA, EE, TOK or CAS item pauses the venture until that item is submitted.
> 5. **Hour cap, enforced every week:**
>    - At most **8 hours a week** in DP1 term, **20 a week** in school holidays and **5 a week** in DP2 term (from August 2027).
>    - **Zero** in the 2 weeks before and during DP1 end-of-year exams and any mock exams. **Zero from 1 January 2028 until the last exam paper.**
>    - Hours are logged daily in a shared sheet. **Any week over the cap is taken off the next week. The cap is never raised.**
> 6. **Ownership.** All profit belongs to [Name]. Parents act as guardians and signatories only, and take no share.
> 7. **Review.** A 30-minute review on the first Sunday of every month covers revenue, net income, hours and grades.

Clauses 4 and 5 make **failure trigger itself**. Nobody needs an awkward conversation, because the rule decides.

### STEP 2: Choose the business by scoring, not by passion (Weeks 2–3)

Score each candidate from 0 to 3 on each test. Any candidate that scores **0 on test 1, 2 or 3 is out**, whatever its total.

1. **Safe for a minor?** It is paid before delivery, small enough that you could refund it, and you'd never need to enforce a contract.
2. **No meaningful inventory or premises?**
3. **Fits the hour cap**, and can survive a 5-month pause (January–May 2028)?
4. **People already pay for this in Mumbai?** Can you name 3 competitors and their prices?
5. **Uses a skill you already have**, or one you can learn to a sellable level in 30 days?
6. **Can others deliver it later** (helpers, templates), so income isn't tied to your hours?

| Business | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | Total | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Small-group tutoring for MYP / IGCSE / ICSE students in your strongest subject** | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 | **18** | Fastest route to cash. It can be handed to trained helpers, which is the agency step. Adults in Mumbai commonly charge ₹1,000–₹4,000 an hour for IB/IGCSE tutoring. |
| **Digital set-up for local small businesses** (Google Business Profile, WhatsApp Business catalogue, one-page site) | 3 | 3 | 2 | 3 | 2 | 2 | **15** | Higher price per job, plus recurring "Listing Care" retainers. You'll need about 30 days to learn it. |
| **Video editing or design for creators** | 3 | 3 | 2 | 3 | 2 | 2 | 14 | Good if you already edit. Deadlines clash with IB crunch periods. |
| **Coding or freelancing for overseas clients** | 2 | 3 | 2 | 2 | 1–3 | 1 | 11–13 | Most platforms need you to be **18+**. **Never lie about your age**: banned accounts freeze earnings. |
| D2C product brand / reselling | 0 | 0 | 1 | 2 | 1 | 3 | — | **Out** until 18. Needs adult contracts and inventory. |
| "Assignment help" / writing IAs or essays for others | — | — | — | — | — | — | — | **Out, permanently.** It's academic misconduct and puts your own diploma at risk. |

**Default if you can't decide:** tutoring first, to get cash quickly and later become the agency. Pilot digital set-up in February 2027 for retainers and a post-18 business line.

**Before tutoring, check two things:** (a) your school's policy on students tutoring other students, and (b) that you never tutor anyone whose work your own teachers mark. Write down that you checked.

### STEP 3: Get your first 3 paying customers (Weeks 3–8, early November to mid-December 2026)

**The offer:**
- Small-group [Maths / Physics / Chemistry / Economics] for **MYP 3–4 / Year 9–10** students.
- **Groups of 4–5, 90 minutes, once a week, ₹700 per student per session, paid monthly in advance** (₹2,800 per student a month).
- **Recruit mostly from Year 9–10 / MYP 3–4, not exam-year students.** They stay with you past May 2027. **At most one group can be exam-year students**, and when they finish, offer them the summer "DP1 head-start" course (Step 5).
- Run sessions online on Google Meet, or at one student's home. **Never at your home with strangers you haven't met.** Bring a parent the first time.

**Why these numbers:** 5 students × ₹700 = ₹3,500 per 90-minute session (about ₹2,330 an hour). Monthly payment in advance means you never chase money.

**Where customers come from, in this order:** (1) parents of younger students at your school and nearby IB/IGCSE schools, through your parents' and siblings'/cousins' WhatsApp groups; (2) your building and society WhatsApp groups; (3) a 1-page Google Doc with your results, syllabus coverage, timings, price and 2 references.

**Message to send (copy and adapt).** It quotes your **Grade 10 result**, because you have no DP grade yet:
> "Hi, I'm [Name], a DP1 student at [School]. I got an [A*] in IGCSE [Maths] and I'm now taking HL [Maths AA]. I'm starting small-group sessions for MYP 3–4 / Year 9–10 [Maths] students: up to 5 per group, 90 minutes weekly, online or in [area]. ₹2,800/month, paid in advance, and the first session is a free trial. Happy to share references."

**Success test at Week 8 (mid-December 2026):** **3 paying students from at least 2 families that aren't relatives or close friends of your parents.**
**If you miss it:** change *one* thing (subject, price, age group or channel) and give it 4 more weeks. **After two failed rounds, switch to the second-ranked business.**

### STEP 4: Grow to about ₹25,000 a month (January–March 2027)

- **Fill two groups of 4–5:** 2 × 5 × ₹2,800 = **₹28,000 a month** (₹22,400 if groups have 4).
- **Build reusable material once:** a topic-by-topic question bank, revision notes and a **written session plan for every lesson**. This does double duty, because it's what a helper will teach from in summer.
- **Pilot digital set-up in February 2027** with **one free pilot** for a local bakery, salon, clinic or tuition centre with a weak Google listing. Get a written testimonial and before-and-after numbers (calls, direction requests).
- **Turn it into a fixed "Shop Online Kit"** (about 12 hours including selling): GBP set-up and optimisation, 15 photos, a WhatsApp catalogue of up to 20 items, a one-page site and a 30-minute handover. **₹18,000: 50% before you start, 50% before go-live.** Build the site on your own staging account. Publish it and transfer the domain only after the final payment.
- **Ownership rule:** the **client creates and verifies their own Google Business Profile and WhatsApp Business number** and adds you as manager. You never hold their accounts.
- **Retainer: "Listing Care", ₹2,500 a month, billed one month at a time.** It covers posts, offers, photos and review replies, about 1 hour a month. Offer it to every kit client, **and sell it on its own** to any shop that already has a profile, after a free 15-minute audit. Plan on **half of paid kit clients converting**. Never count the free pilot.
- **Family capital only for things that save time** (a microphone, graphics tablet, domain or Canva Pro). Total DP1 cap: ₹50,000.

### STEP 5: From ₹42,000 a month in DP1 term to the ₹50,000-every-month line (April–November 2027)

**The honest arithmetic.** At 8 hours a week, a student teaching every hour personally can't reach ₹50,000 a month in term. The way through is not more hours. It's **leverage**: trained helpers teach, and you keep a margin.

**A DP1 term month (April 2027): cap 8 h/week, about 34 h/month**

| Item | Hours a month | Revenue a month |
|---|---|---|
| 2 groups × 5 students, teaching (2 × 1.5 h × 4.3 weeks) | 13 | ₹28,000 |
| Prep (material already built) + parent messages and invoices | 5 | — |
| 2 Listing Care retainers (from about 4 kit or standalone prospects at 50%) | 2 | ₹5,000 |
| 1 kit every 2 months, **at most 2.5 h a week**, so each kit spreads over about 5 weeks | 6 (averaged) | ₹9,000 (averaged) |
| Bookkeeping + monthly review | 1 | — |
| **Buffer** | 7 | — |
| **Total** | **34** | **≈ ₹42,000** |

Weekly check: about 3 h teaching + 1.2 h prep/admin + 0.5 h retainers + ≤2.5 h kit + 0.25 h books ≈ **7.5 h**, inside the 8-hour cap in every week, including kit weeks.

**DP1 end-of-year exams (around May 2027):** zero hours for the 2 weeks before and during exams. Groups get the missed sessions in June or a pro-rata refund. Print this rule on every invoice from now on. Expect May at about ₹20,000–25,000.

**Summer, June–July 2027 (cap 20 h/week): build the agency.** Plan for about 70 of the 86 available hours a month.

1. **Recruit helpers in early June.** You need **4 helpers plus 1 trained backup**.
   - **Who qualifies: only incoming-DP1 students** (who have just finished Grade 10 boards, are free in June–July, and whose own heavy exam period is May 2029), **or alumni of your school now at Mumbai colleges.**
   - **Who doesn't: students in your own year.** They face the same EE, mocks and finals you are pausing for.
   - **How to select:** strong Grade 10 result in the subject, a teacher's reference, and a 20-minute trial lesson that you watch.
   - **Paperwork:** the helper's parent signs a one-page note acknowledging the arrangement.
2. **Training gate ("proven helper"):** a helper co-teaches 2 sessions from your session plans, then teaches the group **alone for 4 weeks** while you sit in on one session. **Nobody takes over a group before passing this gate.**
3. **Hand your 2 existing groups to helpers 1 and 2 in June–July.** Recruit **2 more groups of 5** for helpers 3 and 4 through the same WhatsApp channels, now with testimonials, starting by early August.
4. **New price from August 2027, the new school year: ₹3,500 per student a month** (about ₹815 a session). Tell existing families in June.
5. **Split per group:** a full group of 5 brings in ₹17,500 a month. **The helper gets 35% (₹6,125, about ₹940 an hour for 6.5 hours of teaching). You keep 65% (₹11,375)** for recruiting students, the curriculum, quality control and collecting fees. Pay helpers by UPI from the business account on fixed dates.
6. **Run the "DP1 head-start" course in July:** 6 students who have just finished IGCSE/MYP 5, 12 sessions × 2 h over 4 weeks, **₹7,500 each, paid in full before Day 1** (₹45,000). Start selling it in April 2027, to your exam-year group first.
7. **Sell 2 kits a month in June and July** (₹36,000 a month). That adds about 4 paid kit clients, so at 50% conversion you reach **3 retainers by August**.
8. **New kits stop on 31 July 2027.** A kit doesn't fit DP2's 5-hour weeks. Any kit sold in July is delivered by 15 August.

July 2027 roughly: head-start ₹45,000 + 2 kits ₹36,000 + 2 helper-run groups (65% margin, with about 25% lost to holiday travel) ≈ ₹17,000 + retainers ≈ ₹5,000 = **about ₹1,00,000**. That's a strong month, **but it is mostly one-off, so it does not count toward the test below.**

**A DP2 term month (August–October 2027): cap 5 h/week, about 21 h/month. This is the job-replacing month.**

| Item | Hours a month | Founder net a month |
|---|---|---|
| 4 helper-run groups × 5 students × ₹3,500 = ₹70,000 billed, minus helpers' 35% (₹24,500) | — | ₹45,500 |
| Quality control: sit in on 1 session per group | 6 | — |
| Fee collection, helper pay, parent messages | 3 | — |
| 3 Listing Care retainers | 3 | ₹7,500 |
| Bookkeeping + monthly review | 1 | — |
| **Buffer** | 8 | — |
| **Total** | **21 (≈ 3 h/week used)** | **₹53,000, all recurring** |

**If groups average only 4 students**, net falls to about ₹43,900, which is below the line. The rule is to **fill every group to 5 (6 at most), or open a 5th helper group**, before August. 5 groups of 4 produce about ₹59,500 for 1.5 more hours a month. If you can't fill them, you haven't passed. Don't fake it.

**The pass test (the job-replacing line):** **3 consecutive months between June and November 2027 in which every month has:**
- (a) **founder net ≥ ₹50,000**, where founder net = money in, minus helper pay, minus spending;
- (b) **≥ ₹35,000 of it from recurring monthly fees** (tutoring and retainers, not kits or courses);
- (c) **≥ 70% from non-family customers**;
- (d) **the weekly hour cap never broken.**

The planned pass is **August, September and October 2027 at about ₹53,000 each**. One-off summer revenue can't carry the test.

**If you haven't passed by 30 November 2027:** don't chase it into the exam period. Record your best qualifying month and your recurring base. Step 8 then starts from that number with a smaller family investment.

**If a grade or deadline tripwire fires:** in DP1, cut your own teaching first and stop selling kits. Retainers go last. In DP2, cut your sit-ins to one every 2 months and stop recruiting new groups. **Never** recover hours by skipping helper training.

### STEP 6: DP2: run it without you, and pause safely (August 2027 – May 2028)

1. **August–October 2027:** at most 5 h/week, spent on quality control, collecting fees and paying helpers. You don't teach.
2. **Name one helper as "lead helper"** from 1 November and pay them ₹2,000 a month extra (out of your margin). They sit in on each other group once a month during January–May 2028 and are the first contact for parents.
3. **The wind-down, dated (November–December 2027):**

| Stream | Rule | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Kits | None sold since 31 July. All delivered and fully paid, or the undelivered part of the deposit refunded from the reserve | 15 Aug 2027 |
| Retainers | Each client chooses in writing: (a) pause until June 2028, (b) continue with a trained helper at the same price, or (c) get a handover pack (they already own their accounts) plus a referral. Nothing is billed for January or later unless they chose (b) | Choice by 30 Nov; last bill for December |
| Tutoring groups | A group continues **only if** its helper is proven *and* has confirmed in writing that they're available January–May 2028. Every other group ends on 15 Dec, at a natural break | Decide by 15 Nov; tell parents by 30 Nov |
| January–May 2028 fees | **Billed one month at a time, never per term.** Every invoice says: "If your helper becomes unavailable, a trained replacement starts within 2 weeks, or missed sessions are refunded pro rata." The **backup helper** is the replacement | Terms on the December invoice |
| The helpers' own exams | Helpers are incoming DP1, so their DP1 end-of-year exams fall around May 2028. Each group pauses for 2 weeks then, with the sessions made up in June or refunded pro rata. This goes in the calendar sent to parents in December | Calendar sent by 15 Dec |
| Admin January–May 2028 | One parent collects fees and pays helpers and the lead helper on fixed dates from the business account. You do zero hours | Set up by 31 Dec |

4. **1 January 2028 until your last exam: zero hours.** If all 4 groups hold, founder net runs at about **₹43,500 a month** (₹45,500 minus the lead-helper fee), plus any retainers on option (b), while you work zero hours. Plan as if it will shrink. **If it can't survive without you, let it shrink. Don't rescue it at the cost of your grades.** The restart in June 2028 doesn't depend on it, because the head-start course draws a new cohort every year and kits can be sold again.

### STEP 7: Run a learning portfolio alongside (from December 2026, about 1 hour a month)

- Ask your parents to put **₹2–5 lakh** into a **minor's mutual fund folio** (guardian-operated), split between a **Nifty 50 index fund and a flexi-cap fund**.
- **You make every decision** (allocation, SIP amount, rebalancing), with a one-paragraph written reason for each change.
- The gains are clubbed with your parent's income, which is fine. **The point is to learn what compounding and drawdowns feel like.**
- **Rule:** no single stocks, F&O, crypto or "tips" until 18. SEBI's own studies show that the large majority of individual F&O traders lose money.

### STEP 8: At 18, upgrade properly, and the path to ₹1 lakh a month (from your 18th birthday or June 2028, whichever is later)

**If the Step 5 test passed**, use the full plan below. **If it didn't**, incorporate only once you have a buyer for something, and cap family money at ₹5 lakh until you've passed the Step 5 test as an adult.

1. **Incorporate** (confirm the choice with a CA or Company Secretary).
   - **Private Limited Company** is the default if you'll study abroad or might bring in a partner or investor. It needs 2 directors, and a parent can be the second.
   - **One Person Company (OPC)** works if you'll stay sole owner. Since the 2021 amendment rules, any Indian citizen, resident or not, can form one. But it can't take outside equity without converting to a Pvt Ltd.
   - **Resident director.** Section 149(3) of the Companies Act, 2013 (as amended in 2017) requires at least one director who **stayed in India for at least 182 days during the financial year** (April–March). If you leave in August 2028, you may still meet 182 days for FY 2028–29, but probably not for later years. **Put a parent on the board from day one** so the company never depends on your travel.
2. **Move the business into the company:** the client list, curriculum, domain, and helper and retainer arrangements. **Clubbing ends at 18**, so income from here on is yours.
3. **The concrete path to ₹1,00,000 a month of recurring founder net (target: by December 2028):**

| Line | From | To | Founder net a month |
|---|---|---|---|
| Helper-run groups (₹3,500 × 5, 65% margin, ₹11,375 each) | 4 | **8** (recruit 4 incoming-DP1 or alumni helpers in June 2028) | **₹91,000** |
| Listing Care retainers (₹2,500, now sold directly by the company) | 3 | **6** (kits resume June 2028, plus standalone audits) | **₹15,000** |
| Lead helper / operations fee | ₹2,000 | ₹4,000 | −₹4,000 |
| **Recurring total** | | | **≈ ₹1,02,000** |
| One-offs: 2 head-start cohorts each summer (₹90,000) + kits | | | On top, seasonal |

   **Hours:** about 25 hours a week of full-time work in the June–July 2028 gap to recruit and train, then about 4–5 hours a week of management. **If you go abroad**, replace yourself with a **part-time operations manager** (an adult, for example an alum, at about ₹15,000 a month plus 5% of group revenue). Recurring founder net then falls to roughly ₹75,000–80,000, and **you stay an owner, not a worker** (see Step 9).
   **The adult version of the pass test:** 6 consecutive months, each ≥ ₹1,00,000 founder net (≥ ₹75,000 if abroad with a manager), ≥ 80% recurring.
4. **Family money as a documented investment, never a casual gift:** equity at an agreed valuation, or a written loan with interest. **Cap it at ₹25–50 lakh** (well under 1% of family net worth), released in tranches against milestones set in advance (for example "8 groups live", "₹1 lakh net for 3 months", "first B2B contract over ₹5 lakh").
5. **Products only now:** a recorded revision course, a template pack, or a scheduling and billing tool for tutors or shops. Build one only for something your paying customers keep asking for.
6. **Open adult accounts:** a current account in the company's name, Razorpay or Stripe, and freelance platforms if needed.

### STEP 9: Decide in advance what happens if you go abroad for university

**Student visas often restrict self-employment.** US F-1 students generally can't work for their own business without authorisation, and UK Student visa holders can't be self-employed. **Check the current rules for your target country in DP2.**

- **Safe pattern:** the business stays in India, owned through your company and **run day to day by the operations manager and lead helper**. You are owner only. Get the line between passive ownership and active work from an immigration lawyer for that country.
- **Unsafe pattern:** quietly running sales calls from your dorm. A visa violation can end your degree. **Decide this in writing before you fly.**
- Becoming a non-resident also changes your tax and FEMA position (NRO/NRE accounts). Book one CA meeting before you leave.

## Tripwires: the plan is built to stop itself

| Signal | Automatic response |
|---|---|
| Any subject (IB 1–7; DP2 predicted grades) down 1 or more from baseline | Venture hours halved for 6 weeks |
| Two subjects down, or the 6-subject total down 3 or more | Venture paused until the next report |
| Written teacher concern while the baseline is still provisional | Venture hours halved for 6 weeks |
| Any missed IA, EE, TOK or CAS internal deadline (checked weekly) | Venture paused until that item is submitted |
| Any week's log over the cap | The overage is taken off next week. Never raise the cap. |
| No paying non-family customer by Week 8 | Change one variable. After two failed rounds, switch business. |
| A helper candidate is in your own year (DP2 in 2027–28) | Don't recruit them for any group that runs past December 2027 |
| A helper hasn't passed the 4-week solo gate | They don't take over. You keep the group or it doesn't start. |
| A helper drops out, January–May 2028 | The backup starts within 2 weeks, or that month is refunded pro rata. Never take a fee for a term in advance. |
| Groups average fewer than 5 students in DP2 | Fill them or open a 5th group. Don't count the test as passed on 4-student groups. |
| Advance fees held for undelivered work aren't fully covered by the account balance | Stop all spending until the refund reserve is restored |
| A customer won't pay up front, or won't pay the final 50% before go-live | Don't do the work, or don't publish it |
| A kit or retainer quote has no parent named as the contracting party | Don't send it |
| You're tempted to hold a client's account or number as leverage | Don't. They own it. Your leverage is the unpublished site and the untransferred domain. |
| Any request to write or "help with" someone's graded work | Refuse, and end that relationship |
| A platform asks you to confirm you're 18+ | Don't sign up |
| More than 30% of revenue comes from family friends | Treat the business as unproven, whatever the total |
| You're relying on income after moving abroad | Get immigration advice. The manager and resident director are in place before you fly. |

## Things I've deliberately left out, and why

- **"Use the family's network to land big contracts now."** A big contract with a minor is void, and it teaches you nothing about selling. Use the network for advice and introductions after 18.
- **"Start a YouTube or Instagram channel for income."** Fine as a side project, but the income is unpredictable and usually takes years. It won't produce ₹50,000 every month on 5–8 hours a week.
- **"Hit ₹50k by teaching more hours yourself."** The arithmetic doesn't work inside the cap, and breaking the cap is exactly how student founders lose their predicted grades. Leverage through helpers is the only honest route.
- **"Drop to the IB courses certificate to focus on business."** This plan is designed so you don't have to choose. If the tripwires keep firing, the business shrinks, not your grades.
