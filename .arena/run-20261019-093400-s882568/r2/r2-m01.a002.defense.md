ATTACK 1: CONCEDE. The September figure (₹44,500) used the 8 h/week DP1 term budget with no helper split, while Step 6 capped DP2 at 5 h/week and gave helpers 40%. August was also called "summer" in one place and "term restarting" in another. Fixed: August is now DP2 term everywhere (cap 5 h/week), and the DP2 month is costed from scratch on helper margins. That works out to ₹53,000 founder net in about 13 hours. The worked pass example now uses only that budget.
ATTACK 2: CONCEDE. A 3-month average that relies on one summer spike is not job-like income. Fixed: the pass test now requires **every** month in 3 consecutive months to reach ₹50,000 founder net, with ≥₹35,000 of it from recurring monthly fees. The route there is concrete: a helper-run tutoring agency (4 groups at ₹3,500/student, 65% margin) plus 3 retainers. That income keeps coming in during the founder's zero-hour exam months. Step 8 now has a costed path to ₹1 lakh a month of recurring income after 18.
ATTACK 3: CONCEDE. Most students won't have a DP1 report by early November, and IGCSE/MYP grades don't convert to IB 1–7. Fixed: a provisional baseline comes from each subject teacher's written "working-at" IB grade in Week 1, or from the first DP unit tests marked on the school's DP boundaries. The Term 1 report replaces it when it arrives. A stricter rule applies while the baseline is provisional. The outreach script now quotes the Grade 10 board result instead of a DP grade that doesn't exist yet.
ATTACK 4: CONCEDE. Allowing same-year (DP2) helpers contradicted the reason for the founder's own Jan–May 2028 blackout. Fixed: helpers must be incoming-DP1 students or alumni at Mumbai colleges, never DP2. One trained backup helper is required. Fees for Jan–May 2028 are billed monthly only, with a written "replacement helper within 2 weeks or pro-rata refund" clause. The helpers' own May exam blackout is built into the group calendar.
ATTACK 5: CONCEDE. Three retainers by April–May would need 100% conversion, including the free pilot. Fixed: the model now assumes 50% conversion and doesn't count the pilot. That gives 2 retainers in DP1 term (₹5,000) and 3 by August after the summer kits. Retainers can also be sold on their own as "Listing Care" to shops that don't buy a kit.
ATTACK 6: CONCEDE. Paying up front makes the deal self-executing, not legally valid. A minor's agreement stays void under s.11. Fixed: the test is renamed "safe for a minor". A parent is the named contracting party on every kit or retainer quote, and that structure goes to the CA for a clubbing check. A refund reserve (the undelivered portion of advance fees stays in the account) backs every refund promise.
ATTACK 7: CONCEDE. Since the 2017 amendment, s.149(3) counts 182 days in the financial year, not the previous calendar year. Fixed in Step 8, with the consequence for an August departure spelled out. The hour cap is now enforced weekly: a weekly log, at most 2.5 h a week of kit work in term (so a kit spreads over about 5 weeks), and any overrun is taken off the next week.
