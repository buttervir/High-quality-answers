ATTACK 3: CONCEDE. The plan named no lawful route for the parent's retained profit to reach the student, and the obvious routes fail exactly as described: a gift to the company is taxable for it, shares bought by the parent belong to the parent, and income from a gift to a minor is clubbed. Fix: a new section says plainly that retained proprietorship profit is the parent's after-tax money. It reaches the student only as a parent-to-child gift, which is exempt for the student, best made after 18 so that clubbing ends, and never routed to the company. What moves to the student-owned company is the going business and its future profit. That happens by a valued transfer paid in deferred instalments out of the company's own profit, so no new capital is needed and no money flows back to the parent. The Memorandum is rewritten to match.
