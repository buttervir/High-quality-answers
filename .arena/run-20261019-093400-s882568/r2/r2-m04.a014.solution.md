# A Business Plan for a 16-Year-Old IB DP1 Student in Mumbai

This is for a 16-year-old in DP1 in Mumbai whose family is worth about US$8M (about ₹70 crore). The goal is to start a business that earns enough to replace a salary. The plan starts from your real situation:

- You have little free time.
- You are a minor under Indian law.
- Money is not your problem.
- Your most valuable asset is something most founders don't have: direct access to Mumbai's IB families.

---

## Assumptions

1. **Timeline.** You started DP1 in either **June 2026** (call this Calendar J) or **August 2026** (Calendar A). Either way, your final IB exams are in **May 2028**. Being 16 in October 2026 means you turn 18 somewhere between **October 2027 and October 2028**. The phases below say "your holiday window" wherever the start month matters. Section 8 gives the exact dates for both calendars. Pick yours on Day 1.
2. **"Job-replacing money"** is measured in two stages. Neither counts family money moved into your name.
   - **Milestone A, "the business can pay a salary":** the company makes **₹1,00,000 a month in profit before tax**, averaged over January–June 2028.
   - **Milestone B, "the money reaches you":** **₹1,00,000 a month lands in your bank account**, after company tax and tax on your dividends, from FY 2028-29 (April 2028 onward). That is about the monthly take-home pay of a good first job in Mumbai (₹12–15 LPA CTC). It needs about **₹1.75 lakh a month in company profit before tax** (Section 5 shows the arithmetic).
3. **The IB Diploma comes first.** A plan that costs you 5–7 points has failed, because that drop changes which universities you can get into, and that is worth more than any revenue in the first two years.
4. **Your parents will help.** They act as company directors and as your guardian for paperwork a minor can't sign (contracts, the bank account, the payment gateway). They also provide a capped amount of starting capital.
5. **You are a strong student**, predicted 38 or more, or very strong in two or three subjects. If you aren't, or if your school forbids tutoring, use **Path B (Section 7)**. It has its own numbers and checkpoints. The legal setup, the time plan and the money rules are the same.
6. **All prices quoted to parents are GST-inclusive.** All margins below are calculated *after* 18% GST and payment-gateway fees, so they still hold once the business registers for GST.

## 1. Ten questions to answer before starting

**Q1. What does a 16-year-old in DP1 actually have time for?**
They are in school all week, with IAs, the Extended Essay, TOK and CAS ahead of them. Realistically that leaves **6–8 hours a week** during term and **25–30 hours a week** in holidays. Any plan that needs 20 hours a week during term will fail by December.
→ *Effect on the plan: the business must run in small blocks of time. The admin (scheduling, invoicing, chasing payments) goes to a paid part-timer from the day the agency starts, and the timeline follows your school's own calendar.*

**Q2. Is capital the bottleneck?**
No. Your family could fund almost any small business, and that is a risk in itself. Founders with too much money hide a bad idea behind spending: a fancy brand, an office, ads. They never find out whether customers will pay.
→ *Effect: family money comes in small amounts, released only when you hit milestones (Section 6). Your first rupee of revenue must come before any real spending.*

**Q3. What do you have that a 30-year-old founder doesn't?**
Three things:
- You are inside the IB system right now.
- Your parents' network is affluent Mumbai families.
- You know exactly what an IB student struggles with, because you are one.

→ *Effect: build where these give you an edge. That points to education services for IB, IGCSE and MYP students in Mumbai, where parents routinely pay ₹1,500–₹5,000 an hour.*

**Q4. What does Indian law let a 16-year-old do?**
- **Contracts.** A minor cannot enter a binding contract (Indian Contract Act 1872, Section 11). Any contract a minor signs is void, not just voidable. So vendor and tutor contracts, the payment gateway and the business bank account must belong to an adult or to a company run by adults.
- **Companies.** A minor cannot be a company director or a partner in an LLP. A minor **can hold shares** in a private limited company through a guardian.
- **PAN and bank account.** A minor can have a PAN and a savings account. Under RBI's April 2025 rules, banks may let minors aged 10 and over open and run savings accounts on their own.
- **Tax clubbing.** A minor's income is normally added to the income of the parent who earns more (Income-tax Act 1961, Section 64(1A); the Income-tax Act 2025, in force from 1 April 2026, keeps the same rule under new section numbers). **The exception is income from the minor's own manual work or from their skill, talent or specialised knowledge.** Tutoring you deliver yourself is taxed as *your* income. Dividends on shares you hold before you turn 18 are clubbed with your parent's income. After 18 they are yours.
- **GST.** Private coaching is a taxable service at **18%**. The education exemption covers recognised institutions, not private tutoring. Registration is compulsory once a person's **aggregate turnover** in a financial year passes **₹20 lakh**. Aggregate turnover counts **every supply made under that PAN**, including rent, consulting fees and director's sitting fees, not just the main business. If tutoring income runs through a parent, then a parent who already has ₹20 lakh of rent or fees under their PAN would make the tutoring GST-liable from the first invoice.
- **Company tax.** A private company can opt for tax at **22% + 10% surcharge + 4% cess ≈ 25.17%** (Section 115BAA).

→ *Effect: you earn personally first. The agency runs inside a **new private limited company with its own PAN** (so your parents' rent and fees never count towards its GST threshold). You own **99% of it from the start**, before it is worth anything (Section 5). All prices are GST-inclusive from day one.*

**Q5. How much does a business have to earn to pay you ₹1 lakh a month?**
Selling your own time at ₹1,100 an hour, you would need about 90 hours a month. That is impossible during the DP. So the plan moves from **selling your own hours** to **earning a margin on other people's hours**. After GST and fees, that margin is about **₹490 an hour on MYP/IGCSE** and **₹980 an hour on DP** (Section 2). With about ₹65,000 a month in fixed costs:
- **Milestone A (₹1 lakh pre-tax profit)** needs about **230 tutor-hours a month**.
- **Milestone B (₹1.75 lakh pre-tax, which becomes ₹1 lakh in your hands)** needs about **340 hours a month at today's prices**, or about **265 hours after a 10% price rise**.

→ *Effect: the plan has three stages: solo tutor → tutoring agency → a business run by a manager.*

**Q6. Couldn't family money simply "replace the job"?**
Yes. ₹1.5 crore in debt funds at about 7% pays about ₹87,000 a month. But that is a gift, not a business, and investment income from money a parent gives a minor is clubbed with the parent's income anyway. It doesn't answer the question, so this plan doesn't use it. It is still worth saying: **your safety net already exists, so you can aim for learning and growth rather than quick cash.**

**Q7. What would get you into real trouble?**
- **IB academic misconduct.** Writing or "editing" someone else's IA, EE or TOK essay for money breaks IB rules and can get *your* Diploma withdrawn. The business never touches graded coursework.
- **Child safety.** Your customers are children. Every tutor is an adult with a police certificate who follows written safeguarding rules.
- **School policy.** Some schools ban students from tutoring classmates for money. Check before you start.

**Q8. What happens when you leave for university in August 2028?**
- A **US F-1 visa** restricts off-campus employment. A **UK Student visa** prohibits self-employment and business activity.
- **Holding shares and receiving dividends** (passive ownership) is generally allowed. **Running the business** (setting prices, hiring, managing staff) generally is not.

So from August 2028:
- You are a **shareholder only**. You read a monthly report and vote at the annual meeting.
- An **operations manager** has written authority over pricing and hiring within set limits.
- **Parent-directors** decide anything outside those limits.
- You do hands-on work only while physically in India during vacations, and only after an immigration adviser for your destination country has confirmed in writing that it is allowed.

→ *Effect: someone other than you must be the real decision-maker by June 2028. That is built into Phase 3.*

**Q9. Will this help or hurt university applications?**
A real business with customers, hiring and audited numbers makes a strong essay or activity entry. A business funded by your parents with no customers looks bad. Revenue gives you credibility.

**Q10. What does success look like at each checkpoint?**
- **31 March 2027:** 8+ paying students, about **₹26,000 a month in your own account** (solo, skill income taxed as yours).
- **31 August 2027:** company formed, 8–10 active tutors (12 on the books), about 150 billed hours a month, **₹70,000+ a month in company profit before tax**.
- **30 June 2028 (Milestone A):** **₹1,00,000+ a month in company profit before tax**, averaged over January–June 2028, from tutoring alone, with a manager running daily operations.
- **FY 2028-29 (Milestone B):** **₹1,00,000+ a month reaching your bank account after all tax**, paid as quarterly dividends.

## 2. The recommended business: an IB/IGCSE/MYP tutoring agency, "[YourName] Learning Pvt Ltd"

**Why this business, and not a D2C brand, an app or a café:**

| Test | Tutoring agency | D2C/e-commerce brand | App/SaaS |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue in your first month? | Yes | Rarely | No |
| Starting capital | Under ₹3 lakh, company included | ₹5–20 lakh | ₹5–30 lakh |
| Uses your IB knowledge and network | Strongly | Weakly | Weakly |
| Works in 6–8 hrs a week | Yes, with a paid scheduler | No (stock, logistics) | No |
| Path to ₹1 lakh/month in 18 months | Realistic | Unlikely | Very unlikely |
| Can run while you're abroad | Yes, with a manager | Partly | Partly |

**Who buys:** parents of MYP, IGCSE and DP students at Mumbai's international-curriculum schools. Examples are Dhirubhai Ambani International School (BKC), Ecole Mondiale (Juhu), Oberoi International (Goregaon/JVLR), Aditya Birla World Academy (Tardeo) and Jamnabai Narsee International (Juhu). Families studying online anywhere in India are a second market.

**What sells:** IB Maths AA/AI, Physics, Chemistry, Economics and English, and above all **"a tutor who just did this exact syllabus"**.

### The unit economics (one table, used everywhere in this plan)

| Level | Price to parent (GST-incl.) | Less 18% GST (÷1.18) | Less gateway ~2% | Tutor pay | **Margin per hour** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MYP / IGCSE | ₹1,800 | ₹1,525 | –₹36 | ₹1,000 (band ₹900–₹1,100) | **≈ ₹490** |
| DP1 / DP2 | ₹3,000 | ₹2,542 | –₹60 | ₹1,500 (band ₹1,400–₹1,800) | **≈ ₹980** (₹680 at the top of the pay band) |
| *After a 10% rise (June 2028)* | ₹1,980 / ₹3,300 | | | same bands | **≈ ₹645 / ≈ ₹1,240** |

Rules that keep the table true:
- **Pay bands never overlap price bands.** The highest pay (₹1,800, DP only, for experienced teachers) still leaves at least ₹680 an hour after GST. MYP/IGCSE tutors are never paid more than ₹1,100.
- **Before GST registration** the margin is higher, about ₹760 an hour on MYP and ₹1,440 on DP. The plan never relies on that extra. It is a cushion.
- **Monthly profit formula:** `Profit before tax = (490 × MYP/IGCSE hours + 980 × DP hours − fixed costs) × 0.95`. The ×0.95 covers the manager's 5% profit share and applies from January 2028 only. Check the formula monthly in your sheet.

## 3. The plan, phase by phase with exact steps

Your weekly business hours in each period come from **one place, Section 8**. Nothing below overrides it.

### Phase 0: Setup (2 weeks, before 15 November 2026). About 8 hours in total

1. **The family meeting (1 hour).** Agree, and write on one page:
   - the **two parent-directors** of the company formed in Phase 2, and which of them acts as your **guardian** for the shares and bank paperwork;
   - the capital rules (Section 6);
   - a **grade guardrail**: your predicted grades stay at or above a number you agree (for example 38), or business hours drop to 3 a week until they recover.
2. **Check school policy.** Email your DP coordinator: *"Is it permitted for a DP student to tutor younger students, from outside school or from our school, for a fee?"* Keep the reply.
3. **Pick your subjects.** Choose only the 2 subjects where your marks are strongest (7s, or high 6s). Tutor **MYP 4–5 and IGCSE students**, who are 1–2 years behind you. Don't tutor DP students yourself.
4. **Paperwork. All of it is free or cheap:**
   - a PAN in your own name, if you don't have one (Form 49A through NSDL/UTIITSL, about ₹107);
   - a **minor savings account with UPI** (for example an HDFC, ICICI or Kotak youth account), where your personal tutoring fees go;
   - an income register in Google Sheets (date, client, hours, amount, payment mode).
5. **Write your safeguarding rules now.** Sessions happen in the student's home with a parent there, in a public place, or online with recording and the parents' consent. Never behind a closed door with a child alone. No personal social-media contact with students. Parents are copied on every message.
6. **Set your price:** **₹1,100 an hour** for MYP/IGCSE (adult IB tutors charge ₹2,500–₹5,000). Sell a **pack of 8 hours, paid upfront**, at ₹8,800. Never charge by the single session. Your personal income stays far below ₹20 lakh, so you don't need GST.

### Phase 1: Solo tutor (15 Nov 2026 – 31 Mar 2027). 6 hours a week
**Goal: 8 paying students, about ₹26,000 a month, 10 written testimonials.**

| Week | Exact action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Make a 1-page PDF profile in Canva: name, school, predicted grades, subjects, the 8-hour pack, the safeguarding rules and your parent's phone number. |
| 1 | Your parents send it to 30 families they know with children aged 12–15 on IB or IGCSE, as personal messages, not a group blast. Script: *"My son/daughter is in DP1 and scoring 7s in Maths. They're tutoring a few MYP/IGCSE students. Would X like a free trial session?"* |
| 2 | Offer a **free 45-minute diagnostic session**. Aim to turn at least 1 in 3 into a paid 8-hour pack. |
| 3–4 | Make a free PDF: **"MYP 5 eAssessment Maths: 20 most-tested questions"**. Share it in parent WhatsApp groups, with permission. Collect phone numbers. |
| 5–8 | After every 4th session, send the parents a 3-line progress note. This is what drives referrals. |
| 9–16 | Ask every happy parent for **one referral and one written testimonial**. The reward is 1 free hour for each family referred. Keep a waiting list. It becomes Phase 2's first customer list. |

**Numbers:** 8 students × about 3 hrs a month × ₹1,100 ≈ **₹26,400 a month, all in your own account.** This is skill income, so it is taxed as yours and falls inside the basic exemption. The family CA files an ITR in your name.

### Phase 2: Become an agency (1 Apr 2027 – 31 Aug 2027). Hours from Section 8 (3 a week in exam weeks, 6 in term, 25 in your holiday window)
**Goal: 8–10 active tutors (12 on the books), about 150 billed hours a month, ₹70,000+ a month profit before tax, and a tutor bench that survives September.**

1. **Form the company (April 2027).** The CA does this. Your input is about 2 hours.
   - **Incorporate "[YourName] Learning Pvt Ltd"** through MCA's SPICe+ form: both parents as directors (DIN and digital signatures for each), authorised capital ₹1,00,000 (10,000 shares of ₹10). Cost about ₹15,000–₹25,000. The PAN, TAN and current-account application come bundled with SPICe+.
   - **Give the shares to you while they are worth nothing.** At incorporation the parents subscribe 5,000 shares each. **Within 30 days**, before the company has any customers, they **gift 9,900 shares (99%) to you**, held through your guardian, using a share-transfer deed (form SH-4, with stamp duty at 0.015%, a few rupees). One parent keeps 100 shares (1%), because a private company needs at least two members. A gift from a parent is not a taxable "transfer" for the parent (s.47(iii)). It is not taxable for you either, since gifts from relatives are exempt (s.56(2)(x)). And the shares are worth only their ₹10 face value, so **no capital gain arises now and no business transfer is ever needed later.** If the CA prefers, you can instead subscribe 99% directly at incorporation through your guardian. The result is the same.
   - **Within the first weeks:** a Maharashtra Shops & Establishments intimation (online, free or nominal); Maharashtra **Professional Tax enrolment (PTEC)**, which every company needs, at ₹2,500 a year; a **current account** and a **Razorpay or Cashfree** gateway in the company's name. Your solo students move to the company at their next pack renewal, after the parents have approved the message to them.
   - **Annual compliance** (statutory audit, ROC filings AOC-4 and MGT-7, company ITR, TDS returns): about ₹60,000–₹1,00,000 a year. This is in the ₹10,000 a month "compliance and accounting" cost below.
2. **GST, by rule.** The company has its **own PAN, so its aggregate turnover starts at zero** whatever your parents earn from rent or fees. Prices are already GST-inclusive. Billings reach about ₹3.4 lakh a month by August 2027, so the financial year's running total crosses **₹15 lakh around September 2027**. At that point the CA applies for registration, which is mandatory within 30 days of crossing ₹20 lakh. Expect registration by **October–November 2027**. Returns (GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B, monthly or quarterly under QRMP) are filed by the CA. Parents see no price change.
3. **Hire a part-time scheduler from day one of the agency (April 2027): ₹15,000 a month for about 12 hours a week.** A college student or a retired teacher works well. They handle tutor-student matching, the booking calendar, monthly invoices from the company, payment reminders and routine parent calls. **You keep only:** final say on tutor hires (watching demo lessons), a monthly quality call with each family, and a **30-minute weekly numbers review**. This is what makes 150 hours a month workable on 3–6 hours a week of your time.
4. **Recruit tutors, sized for September, not for summer.** Every tutor is **18 or over on their start date**, so their contract is valid and they can get a police certificate. **Rule: tutors leaving Mumbai within 6 months hold no more than one third of contracted hours.** Recruit in this order:
   1. **3–4 IB-trained teachers** (current or former) who want evening and weekend income. They take DP subjects at ₹1,500–₹1,800 an hour and are the stable core.
   2. **3–4 IB/IGCSE alumni at Mumbai colleges** (for example IIT Bombay, ICT, St. Xavier's, Jai Hind, HR, Mithibai, NMIMS). They take MYP/IGCSE at ₹900–₹1,100 an hour and stay in the city for 3–4 years.
   3. **2–3 recent DP graduates aged 18+ with 40+ points**, for holiday demand and the bootcamp. Those going to Indian universities can continue online after they leave.
   - **Screening:** transcripts, a 30-minute demo lesson that you watch, two references, a signed safeguarding code, and a **police clearance certificate for every tutor** (through the Maharashtra Police online portal).
   - **Contract:** an **independent contractor agreement between the company and the tutor**. The CA or a lawyer drafts it once, for ₹5,000–₹10,000. It contains:
     - a **non-solicit clause that applies during the engagement** (restraints after the engagement ends are likely void under Contract Act s.27, so the plan doesn't rely on one);
     - a **confidentiality clause** covering client names and contact details;
     - a rule that **all fees are paid to the company only**;
     - the **"no graded work" clause**.
   - **Loyalty:** a tutor gets **₹100 an hour extra on every hour rebooked by their students after the first pack**, paid from the margin. That gives them a reason to stay inside the company.
5. **Second recruiting round, 4 weeks before the new school year** (May for Calendar J, July for Calendar A). Replace every departing tutor before they leave. Target **12 tutors on the books for 150–200 hours**, which is a **25% bench** above booked hours. Pay departing graduates ₹2,000 for each referred junior who stays 3 months.
6. **Sell DP-level tutoring** at **₹3,000 an hour**, taught by teachers and 42+ scorers. MYP/IGCSE is ₹1,800. Both are sold as 8-hour packs, paid upfront.
7. **Holiday product: the "DP Head Start" bootcamp.** It runs for 2 weeks, 3 hours a day, for students who are **about to start DP1**. It covers Maths AA/AI foundations, how IAs work, and TOK in plain English. Mumbai schools start the DP in different months, so run **two batches**:
   - **Batch J (May 2027)**, for schools starting in June;
   - **Batch A (late June–July 2027)**, for schools starting in August.
   Each batch is 15 students × ₹18,000 = ₹2.7 lakh revenue (about ₹2.3 lakh after GST), taught by 2 tutors (two-adult rule), in a society clubhouse or co-working room (₹1,500–₹3,000 a day) or online. You design it during your own holiday window. The batch that falls in your term time is run by the tutors and the scheduler. **This is upside. No monthly target depends on it.**
8. **Systems:** Google Sheets for students, tutors, hours, invoices and the GST running total. WhatsApp Business on a number **owned by the company**, which the parent-directors can also see. Google Calendar for bookings. Invoices from the company.

**Phase 2 maths (August 2027), at post-GST margins to be safe:**
- Contribution: 90 MYP/IGCSE hours × ₹490 + 60 DP hours × ₹980 = ₹44,100 + ₹58,800 = **₹1,02,900**
- Fixed costs: scheduler ₹15,000 + compliance and accounting ₹10,000 + software, phone and small marketing ₹7,000 = ₹32,000
- **≈ ₹71,000 a month profit before tax.** Before GST registration the real figure is about ₹1.2 lakh.

This profit belongs to the company, which you own 99% of. It stays in the company as working capital until dividends start (Section 5).

### Phase 3: Make it run without you (Sep 2027 – Jun 2028). 4 hrs a week, dropping to 2 in exam season
**Goal: Milestone A (₹1,00,000+ a month profit before tax, January–June 2028 average, tutoring only), and a manager who can run the business with no input from you.**

DP2 is your hardest year: the EE and IAs are due, university applications go in from October 2027 to January 2028, then come mocks and finals. So:
1. **September 2027: upgrade the scheduler to operations coordinator** (or hire one) at ₹25,000 a month. Once anyone is on salary, the company registers as an employer for **Professional Tax (PTRC)**. The CA does it.
2. **January 2028: promote them to operations manager** at ₹40,000 a month plus **5% of monthly profit before the share is paid**. Give them a written **delegation of authority**:
   - they can hire tutors within the Section 2 pay bands, after the full screening checklist;
   - they can set prices within ±15% of the price table;
   - they can spend up to ₹25,000 a month on marketing;
   - anything beyond these limits goes to the parent-directors.

   From January your role is a **1-hour weekly review of the numbers**, dropping to monthly during May finals.
3. **June 2028: raise prices by 10%** for the new academic year, but only if there is a waiting list. This rise is the cheapest route to Milestone B.
4. **Keep the tutor bench at 25% above booked hours** through every intake.
5. **Never offer** "IA/EE writing help", "assignment help" or anything that produces graded work. The offer is teaching, past-paper practice and feedback on the student's own understanding, and it says so in writing.

**Milestone A target, January–June 2028 average, tutoring only (GST-registered):**
- Contribution: 150 MYP/IGCSE hours × ₹490 + 150 DP hours × ₹980 = ₹73,500 + ₹1,47,000 = **₹2,20,500**
- Fixed costs: manager ₹40,000 + compliance and accounting ₹10,000 + software and phone ₹5,000 + marketing ₹10,000 = **₹65,000**
- Manager's 5% share: ≈ ₹7,800
- **≈ ₹1,48,000 a month profit before tax.** That is 300 hours a month, from about 12 tutors × 25 hours, or about 38 students × 8 hours.
- **Break-even for Milestone A: about 230 hours a month** at a 50/50 mix. If January–June averages fewer, the target is missed. The fix is more DP hours (each is worth two MYP hours), not more spending.
- June is a seasonal low because exam classes end. That is why the test is a 6-month average.

**Upside, not counted in either milestone:**
- **Bootcamp 2028:** a May batch for Calendar J schools and a late June–July batch for Calendar A schools, run by the manager and 2 tutors each. About ₹5–8 lakh revenue across 2–3 batches. Your role is a 1-hour approval in April.
- **"IB Maths AA SL Paper 1 & 2 crash course":** 20 videos plus worked solutions that you write yourself (never IB's copyrighted material). Recorded in June–July 2028 after your exams and sold from August 2028 at ₹4,999 (GST-inclusive) through Graphy, Teachable or Gumroad, on an account owned by the company. If you record it as a paid contractor of the company, that fee is your skill income.

## 5. Ownership and the money that reaches you

### Who owns what, and when

| When | Structure | Whose income for tax |
|---|---|---|
| Nov 2026 – Mar 2027 | You as an individual, tutoring personally | **Yours** (skill income, not clubbed) |
| April 2027 onward | **[YourName] Learning Pvt Ltd.** Directors: both parents. Shareholders: **you 99%** (through your guardian until you turn 18), one parent 1%. Shares were gifted to you at face value in the company's first month, so **no later business transfer, slump-sale tax or GST re-registration is ever needed.** | The company pays ~25.17% tax on its profit. Your dividends are **clubbed with your parent's income until you turn 18**, and yours after that. |
| Your 18th birthday (Oct 2027 – Oct 2028) | Your guardian stops acting for you, and the shares are fully under your control. You become a **director only if** an immigration adviser confirms in writing that it fits your visa. | Dividends are yours |

**Why the company starts in April 2027, not later:** a proprietorship that becomes a company later causes three problems.
- **Tax:** the switch is only tax-neutral under s.47(xiv) if the proprietor keeps 50% or more of the votes for 5 years. Giving you 99% breaks that rule, so it becomes a taxable slump sale at fair market value.
- **GST:** the company needs a fresh GST registration (CGST s.22(3)). The old registration and its credits have to be wound down.
- **Who owns the income:** every rupee of profit in the meantime is your parent's.

Starting the company first avoids all three for about ₹60,000–₹1,00,000 a year of compliance, which tranche 2 (Section 6) covers.

### What actually lands in your account (Milestone B arithmetic)

Company tax ≈ 25.17%, so **₹1 of profit becomes ₹0.748 available for dividends**, and your 99% share is **₹0.741**.

| Your situation when the dividend is paid | Tax on your dividend | Pre-tax company profit needed for ₹1 lakh/month in hand |
|---|---|---|
| Under 18 | Clubbed with your parent at their slab, up to about 36% (dividend surcharge is capped at 15%). The family decides who bears it. | Family's choice |
| 18+, **resident** in India (for example a gap year, or university in India) | Slab rates. On about ₹15.5 lakh a year of dividends, new-regime tax is about ₹1.2 lakh, or about ₹10,000 a month | **≈ ₹1.5 lakh** |
| 18+, **non-resident** (abroad for most of the year, which is likely from FY 2028-29) | About **20.8% withheld in India** (s.115A rate plus cess, or a lower treaty rate if one applies). Also check whether your study country taxes it | **≈ ₹1.7 lakh** |

**So Milestone B is set at ₹1.75 lakh a month in pre-tax profit**, which works in the worst case. Check: ₹1,75,000 × 0.741 = ₹1,29,700 dividend; less 20.8% = **₹1,02,700 in your account each month.**

- **Payout rhythm:** the board (your parents) declares an **interim dividend each quarter** from after-tax profit. They always keep **2 months of fixed costs (about ₹1.3 lakh) as cash in the company**. The first dividend can be declared from the January–March 2028 quarter's profit.
- **How the hours work out:** after the June 2028 price rise (margins ≈ ₹645 / ₹1,240), ₹1.75 lakh needs about **265 hours a month**. At old prices it needs about **340**. The January–June 2028 run-rate of 300 hours at new prices gives about ₹2.07 lakh pre-tax, which is about **₹1.2 lakh in your hands as a non-resident**.

Also keep a **dated family memo**, signed now, recording that you founded and run the business and that the 99% shareholding is yours.

## 9. Risk register

| Risk | What you do |
|---|---|
| IB academic misconduct (helping with coursework) | A written "no graded work" clause in every tutor contract; tutors who break it are removed immediately |
| A child safety incident | All tutors 18+, with police certificates and a signed safeguarding code; a parent present or sessions recorded; two adults at every bootcamp |

| Tax surprises in the ownership structure | Shares gifted to you at face value in month one; no proprietorship-to-company transfer, so no slump sale and no GST re-registration |
