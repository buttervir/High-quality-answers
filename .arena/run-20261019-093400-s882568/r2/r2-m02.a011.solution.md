# From DP1 to a job's take-home pay: one business, worked out in rupees

## The answer in one paragraph

Start a **small-group online tutoring business** in the one subject where you score a 7. It has two products:
- **Grade 9–10 (MYP 4–5 / IGCSE)** groups, which you teach yourself during DP1.
- **DP1–DP2 groups** for students one year below you, which paid alumni tutors teach from August 2027.

A short summer "Bridge to DP" course connects the two. Every year your Grade 10 students finish their board exams, and the bridge course turns them into DP customers who then stay for two years.

**The target:** 39 students in 14 groups with 5 tutors by December 2027. That gives **about ₹95,000 a month profit after GST**.
- **While you are a minor**, that profit is taxed as a parent's income at about 39%. The family keeps about **₹58,000 a month**.
- **From your 18th birthday**, the profit is your own income, about ₹11.4 lakh a year. That is under the ₹12 lakh rebate limit, so you keep **about ₹95,000 a month in hand**. A ₹12 LPA Mumbai job takes home about ₹90,000–97,000 a month.
- **The downside case:** if outreach converts half as well as the first test, you reach about 26 students in 9 groups, and profit is **about ₹65,000 a month**.

This replaces a job only if it gets about 23 new students every year. The plan has a yearly intake engine and a check every September for that reason. It needs no company, no website and no family capital.

## Assumptions

- "Job replacing money" means matching the **take-home pay** of a ₹12 LPA salary. Under the new regime, that salary pays almost no tax (the ₹75,000 standard deduction plus the s.87A rebate up to ₹12 lakh). After PF it takes home about ₹90,000–97,000 a month.
- You started DP1 in mid-2026 and sit finals in May 2028. You turn 18 between October 2027 and October 2028. Your diploma matters more than the business.
- You score a 7 (or an A*) in one of Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Economics or English. The worked example uses Maths. If none of these applies, see "If tutoring isn't you".
- Prices are estimates for affluent Mumbai families, and Step 1 tests them. Experienced IB/IGCSE tutors in Mumbai charge ₹2,000–5,000 an hour one-to-one. Your group prices work out to about ₹750–920 per student-hour.
- **Churn:** at any time, about 3 seats in 42 are empty because students drop out or move. Groups therefore average 2.8 students, not 3.
- This is general information, not legal or tax advice. The family's CA confirms every tax and GST point.

## Step 0: Set up in one evening (week of 19 Oct 2026)

1. **WhatsApp Business** (free). Make one group per tutoring group, with the parents in it.
2. **Google Calendar and one Google Sheet** (free).
   - **Sheet columns:** student, parent, product, grade, fee, paid date, sessions held, *lead source*.
   - **Two cells at the top:** the financial-year running total of fees, which turns red at **₹17 lakh**, and the count of new students this academic year.
3. **A paid video plan**, either Zoom Pro or Google Workspace (about ₹1,000–2,000 a month). Free plans cut group calls off at 40–60 minutes. Teach online only, because Mumbai traffic would eat your hours.
4. **A minor's savings account with UPI**, with a parent as guardian. RBI rules let a minor aged 10 or over operate one. This account is for Steps 1–3 only (see Step 4).
5. **Your grade baseline.** Write down the latest school-reported grade in each of your 6 DP subjects. The stop rules use these numbers.

Do not set up a logo, a website, a company or an app.

## Step 1: Test whether anyone will pay (19 Oct – 30 Nov 2026)

**The product:** a group of 3 Grade 9–10 students, online, twice a week for 1 hour, on Mon and Thu. The fee is **₹6,500 a month per student, paid in advance on the 1st**. It covers every scheduled session that month, usually 8–9. Missed sessions are refunded only if the family gives 24 hours' notice.

**Send this message to 20 families** (your parents' friends, neighbours and your school's MYP parents):

> "Hi Aunty/Uncle, I'm [Name], in DP1 at [School]. I scored a 7 in MYP5 Maths. I'm starting a small online group for Grade 9–10 Maths: 3 students, Mon and Thu 6–7 pm, ₹6,500/month, paid in advance. The first session is free with no obligation. Would [child] like to try it? We start Mon 9 Nov."

**The decision rule:**
- If 3 families pay by 30 Nov, go on to Step 2.
- If not, change one thing (the subject, the grade or the time slot) and message 20 new families.
- If you still have no paying families by 31 Jan 2027, change the skill.

Record each child's grade, because the Grade 10s leave after April.

## Step 2: Teach the groups yourself (Dec 2026 – Apr 2027)

Grow to **3 groups (9 students: 5 in Grade 10, 4 in Grade 9)**. That is 6 hours of teaching and 1 hour of admin a week, which is the most you should take on in DP1. Demand peaks from January to April, before the board exams.

**Revenue:** 9 × ₹6,500 = **₹58,500 a month**. All of it is profit except about ₹1,500 for software. There is no GST at this size.

**The monthly routine:**
1. Send a reminder on the 25th.
2. Collect fees on the 1st.
3. Teach.
4. Log each session.
5. Ask every family that has stayed past month 2 for one referral.

**Build the lead list (Jan – May 2027).** In a second tab of the sheet, list every household you can reach that has a child in Grade 9, Grade 10 or entering DP1 who takes your subject. Sources:
- your parents' contacts
- your school's parent groups
- building societies
- your future tutors' schools

**You need 200 households.** If you reach fewer than 120, your target is 9 groups, not 14. Plan for the downside case.

**In March 2027,** offer the bridge course (Step 3) to every Grade 10 family. Booking closes 31 May and payment is due 15 June.

## Step 3: Bridge course, then the DP product (late June – July 2027)

**Timing.** IGCSE papers run until about mid-June, and MYP eAssessment runs in May. Nobody pays for a DP preview during board exams. Your own DP1 exams also fall in that period. So:
- **Grade 10 groups end after April.** Their families pay for April, which is the revision month, and stop.
- **The bridge runs in the 2 weeks after the last IGCSE paper** and before DP1 starts. The default dates are **21 Jun – 3 Jul 2027**, Mon–Sat, 2 hours a day. Check them against the IGCSE timetable and your school calendar.
- **If a target school starts DP1 in June,** run the course for its students as 6 Saturday sessions of 4 hours in the first 6 weeks of term instead, called a "DP1 Maths jump-start".
- **If the bridge dates are not in your own school holiday,** the helper tutor teaches both batches and costs ₹24,000 more.

**"Bridge to DP Maths AA/AI":**
- **Format:** 12 sessions of 2 hours, in 2 batches of 8 students, at **₹15,000 per student**.
- **Who fills it:** about 5 of your own Grade 10 leavers, plus about 11 students from referrals and the lead list.
- **Gross:** 16 × ₹15,000 = **₹2,40,000**, collected by 15 June.
- **Cost:** a helper tutor teaches one batch, 24 hours × ₹1,000 = ₹24,000. You teach the other batch in your holiday.
- **Profit:** about **₹2,16,000**.

On the last session, offer the **DP Maths group**:
- 3 students, twice a week, **₹8,000 a month all-in**.
- It runs from the first week of DP1 (the table assumes 1 Aug 2027) until the students' exams in May 2029.
- **Assume half the bridge students sign up (8 students).** Hire the helper as your first DP tutor.

These students are a year below you, so they are not in your own cohort. That is allowed under the integrity rules below.

## Step 4: Ramp up and hand over the teaching (Aug – Dec 2027)

**Money first, by 30 Jun 2027.** Open a **current account** in the name of the parent whose PAN the CA chooses for GST (Step 5), and collect all fees there from July. A minor's savings account has bank-set limits and is not meant for about ₹2.8 lakh a month of business receipts. At 18 you open your own (Step 6).

**Tutors.**
- **When to hire:** only when 3 students are waiting. You need 5 tutors by December, each running at most 3 groups.
- **Who to hire:** your school's IB alumni at Mumbai colleges or on gap years who scored a **7 at DP level** in the subject.
- **How to vet them:** one demo session and one teacher reference.
- **Pay:** **₹1,000 an hour for Grade 9–10 groups and ₹1,200 an hour for DP groups**, by UPI on the 5th. Never pay a tutor from fees you haven't collected yet.

**Students.** You need **27 new students** on top of the 4 Grade 9s you kept and the 8 bridge converts. That gives 17 Grade 9–10 students and 22 DP students.
- **Outreach:** message the lead list at about 8 messages a week from July to December (about 180 messages).
- **Referrals:** ask every bridge family and every paying family for one.
- **Tutors' networks:** each tutor introduces you to their own school's parents.

**Re-measure on 31 Jul 2027.** The first test converted 3 of 20 warm contacts, which is too small a sample to plan on. After the first 60 messages to the lead list, divide paying students by messages sent:

| Conversion | What to plan for |
|---|---|
| 10% or more | Keep the 14-group target |
| 5–10% | Target 9–11 groups (₹65,000–80,000 a month). Don't stretch. |
| Under 5% | Stop cold outreach. Grow only by referrals and the bridge, and treat the business as a ₹40,000–65,000 side income until you are 18. |

The hiring rule means you never carry tutors for students you don't have.

**Your time:**
- **June–July 2027 (holiday):** your bridge batch plus 2 Grade 9–10 groups.
- **August to December:** about 5 hours a week, covering outreach, scheduling, fees and one quality drop-in. You stop teaching in August.
- **After that:** 3 hours a week, all of it doable from a phone.

## Step 5: GST, registered in the last week of January 2028

For a service business in Maharashtra, GST registration becomes compulsory once the financial year's turnover crosses **₹20 lakh**, and you must apply within 30 days. Your prices are all-inclusive, so registering early only hands over 18/118 of your fees. Registering in December would have cost about ₹87,000.

- **When the red cell (₹17 lakh) lights up** in January 2028, ask the CA to register in the **last week of January**. The 1 Feb collection takes the total from ₹18.4 lakh past ₹20 lakh. The CA confirms the exact effective date and handles the filing. Your only job is to export the sheet on the 1st.
- **Whose PAN:** the CA decides, and it should be the parent whose name is on the current account. That parent must have **no existing GST-registered business**. GST turnover is counted per PAN, so adding this to a PAN that already has a GSTIN means 18% from the first rupee.
- **Prices don't change.** ₹6,500 and ₹8,000 stay all-inclusive, and the GST comes out of your margin.
- From April 2028 every financial year starts above the threshold, so you stay registered.

**The steady state from December 2027 (39 students in 14 groups: 6 Grade 9–10, 8 DP, 3 seats empty):**

| Line | Monthly |
|---|---|
| Fees: 17 × ₹6,500 + 22 × ₹8,000 | ₹2,86,500 |
| GST included in fees (18/118) | −₹43,700 |
| Gr 9–10 tutors: 6 groups × 9 hrs × ₹1,000 | −₹54,000 |
| DP tutors: 8 groups × 9 hrs × ₹1,200 | −₹86,400 |
| Software and no-show buffer | −₹7,000 |
| **Profit after GST, before income tax** | **≈ ₹95,000** |

**The downside case (outreach at about 7%):** 26 students in 9 groups (11 Grade 9–10 students in 4 groups, 15 DP students in 5 groups). Fees are ₹1,91,500, GST ₹29,200, tutors ₹90,000, software ₹7,000, so **profit is ≈ ₹65,000**.

**Income tax on the target profit:**
- **While you are a minor:** the profit is clubbed with the higher-earning parent's income (see Tax). For a family worth about ₹70 crore, assume that parent earns over ₹2 crore. Under the new regime that means **39%** (30% plus 25% surcharge plus 4% cess). Under the old regime above ₹5 crore it is 42.74%. The CA uses the parent's actual rate. At 39%, the family keeps **about ₹58,000 a month**.
- **From your 18th birthday:** the profit is your own income, about ₹11.4 lakh a year. That is under the ₹12 lakh s.87A rebate limit, so the tax is nil while you are an Indian resident and you keep **about ₹95,000 a month**. If you study abroad and become a non-resident, the rebate stops applying. The tax is then about ₹56,000 a year and you keep about ₹90,000 a month.

**Each group's contribution after GST and tutor pay:** about ₹7,500 a month for a Grade 9–10 group and about ₹9,500 for a DP group.

## Step 6: The yearly intake engine (from 2028, every year)

**Why this matters:** every May, two cohorts leave. The Grade 10s finish their boards, and from 2029 the DP2 students finish their DP. A job replacement has to refill them every year. To hold 39 students you need **about 23 new students every year**:

| Seats to refill each year | New students |
|---|---|
| Grade 10s who leave (about half of 17) | 9 |
| DP2 cohort who leave (about half of 22) | 11 |
| Mid-year drop-outs | 3 |
| **Total** | **≈ 23** |

**Where they come from:**
- **The bridge course:** about 16 attendees, of whom about 8 become new DP1 students.
- **Referrals:** one ask per family per year from about 39 families, which brings about 8–10 new Grade 9s.
- **Outreach:** 2 hours a week from June to September, done from your phone.

**Who runs it:**
- **In March 2028,** pick your best tutor as **lead tutor** at ₹1,500 an hour. They teach the 2028 bridge and run the waitlist.
- **You** keep the 2 hours a week of outreach every June to September, including from university.

**Intake check, 30 September every year.**

| Students enrolled | What to do |
|---|---|
| 36 or more | On target |
| 30–35 | Merge part-filled groups so every group has 3. Don't replace tutors who leave. |
| Under 30 | You are in the downside case. Hold at that size, stop hiring, and decide whether to add one more channel, such as a second subject with a tutor who already has students waiting. |

**The 2028–29 bump and the May 2029 drop.** The first DP cohort (22 students) is about twice a normal year's intake. So from August 2028 you run about 33 DP students: the 2027 cohort in DP2 plus about 11 new DP1s. That needs 11 DP groups and pushes profit above the target. **Treat 2028–29 as a bonus year and don't spend it as income.** In May 2029, the 22 DP2 students leave together and the business falls back to the baseline of about 39 students, provided the 2029 intake check passed.

**Your own DP2 and finals (2027–28):**
- Take no new students from March to May 2028. Your work drops to about 1 hour a week of fee collection.
- The time stop rule below also applies.

**After you turn 18:**
- Register as a sole proprietor with an Udyam registration, and later an LLP if you want one.
- Open a current account in your own name.
- With the CA, move the GST registration to your own PAN.
- Sign the tutor agreements yourself.
- Family money can now fund expansion, but only for a business that has already proved itself.

## Rules you must not skip

**Contracts.** A contract with a minor is void in India (Indian Contract Act, s.11). So:
- Keep every fee prepaid, so you never need to enforce payment.
- A parent signs anything that needs a signature until you are 18: tutor agreements, the current account and the video subscription.

**Tax.** A minor's income is normally added to the income of the parent who earns more. This is the clubbing rule: s.64(1A) of the 1961 Act, carried into the Income-tax Act 2025. Income from the minor's **own skill** is not clubbed.
- **Your own teaching** (Steps 1–3) brings in about ₹2.3 lakh in FY 2026–27 and ₹2.7 lakh in FY 2027–28. **File your own ITR for both years**, signed by a guardian, showing this as skill income. It is under the rebate limit, so the tax is nil, but filing documents that it is yours and not clubbed. Set aside nothing for it.
- **Profit from other tutors' teaching** (from August 2027, plus the helper-taught bridge batch) is clubbed. **Set aside 39%,** or the parent's actual rate if the CA gives you one, until your 18th birthday.
- **The year you turn 18:** the CA splits that year's income at your birthday.

**GST.** Private tutoring is taxable at 18% and is not exempt as education. Register when the red cell lights up (Step 5), and never let the running total pass ₹20 lakh unregistered.

**IB academic integrity.**
- Never write, edit or "fix" anyone's IA, EE or TOK. Teach concepts only.
- Never tutor anyone in your own DP cohort.
- Make every tutor follow the same rules.

A malpractice finding could cost you your own diploma.

**CAS.** Paid work does not count toward CAS, so keep the two separate.

**Safeguarding.**
- Online and in groups only.
- Parents are in every WhatsApp group.
- No private one-to-one chats with students.
- Tutors follow the same rules.

## Stop rules

- **Grades:** if any school report shows a subject one level below its Step 0 baseline, halve your business hours until the next report shows it back.
- **Demand:** if you don't have 3 paying students by 30 Nov 2026, change the offer. If you have none by 31 Jan 2027, change the skill.
- **Conversion:** on 31 Jul 2027, resize the target using the conversion table in Step 4.
- **Intake:** every 30 September, use the intake table in Step 6.
- **Hiring:** add a tutor only when 3 students are waiting.
- **Time:** if the business needs more than 5 hours a week in DP2, stop taking new students until it needs less.

## If tutoring isn't you

Keep the same skeleton:
- a skill you already have
- sold to people who already know you
- prepaid monthly
- priced GST-inclusive
- scaled only by hiring against a waitlist
- refilled by a yearly intake check

Change only the skill:

| Skill | First offer | Price | How it scales |
|---|---|---|---|
| Python / no-code / AI tools | Automate one task (invoices, WhatsApp replies, reports) for businesses your parents know | ₹25,000 setup + ₹5,000/month | 15–20 clients, then a college student builds |
| Video editing | Reels/Shorts for Mumbai doctors, CAs, restaurants | ₹20,000/month for 12 videos | 2–3 editors at ₹8,000–10,000 each; you keep the clients |
| Design / Canva | Monthly social posts for local shops and clinics | ₹12,000/month | Same as above |

## What to do with the $8 million

Almost nothing. Take no more than ₹25,000 from the family. This plan needs less than that.

Avoid a café, a franchise or a family-funded D2C brand:
- You can't legally sign the leases and supplier contracts they need.
- It would take more time than DP allows.
- It would teach you less than earning your first ₹58,500 a month from nothing.

The family's real advantages are:
- **its network**, which is where your first 20 messages and your 200-household lead list come from
- **its CA**, who handles clubbing, GST and your own ITR
- **a parent's signature** on the current account and the agreements until you are 18
- **a safety net**, which is what lets you try this at all

Once the business has passed two intake checks and you are 18, family capital can help it grow.
