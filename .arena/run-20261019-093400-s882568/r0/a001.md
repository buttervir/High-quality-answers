# From IB DP1 to Job-Replacing Income: The Exact Plan (Mumbai, Age 16)

## The answer in one paragraph

Start a **tutoring business for younger international-curriculum students (Grades 6–10: MYP, IGCSE, ICSE), taught by you, this week**. Run it from home and online, with the money going into your own bank account. Over the next 12–18 months, turn it into a **small tutoring agency** where IB graduates and university students do most of the teaching and you keep 30–40% of every session. That is the quickest route from zero to about **₹1,00,000 a month**, the salary of a decent entry-level job in Mumbai. You can do it in **6–8 hours a week** without hurting your IB grades, and it needs almost none of the family's money. If teaching isn't for you, Plan B (a services business for the family's business network, Section 7) uses the same steps.

---

## Assumptions

- **"Job-replacing money" = ₹1,00,000/month in profit**, which is a good fresher salary in Mumbai (₹6–12 LPA). Stage targets: ₹25k/month by Month 3, ₹60k by Month 9, ₹1L+ by Month 15–18.
- **IB comes first.** DP grades decide university admission, and university is worth far more than anything this business earns before 18. The plan caps your hours and has a hard stop if grades fall.
- **Your parents agree and will help with paperwork.** A 16-year-old can't legally sign most contracts in India, so a parent is needed for a few setup steps.
- **The family's ~$8M is a safety net, not starting capital.** Total budget: **₹25,000 or less**. Section 8 explains why.
- You are at least average in one school subject (Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Economics, English or Biology). If not, use Plan B.

## 1. How this plan was chosen (three experts, one decision)

| Expert | Their best idea | Where they pushed back |
|---|---|---|
| **Mumbai startup operator** | "Sell to people you can reach this week, sell a service so you need no inventory, and don't build an app." | Wanted an agency for local businesses (web, WhatsApp, AI automation) because projects pay more. |
| **Chartered Accountant / lawyer** | "Pick income from your own skill. Under Indian tax law, a minor's income from their own skill or talent is *not* added to the parents' income, and it can sit in your own account under your own PAN." | Warned that once other people do the work, the income may be added to a parent's income, so the setup needs to change at that point. |
| **IB coordinator / admissions counsellor** | "Choose a business that strengthens your IB work and your university story. Tutoring sharpens your own subject knowledge, and 'built a tutoring company with 10 tutors' is a strong application line." | Firm limits: no work in exam months, never write anyone's IA or EE, and a paid business **does not count as CAS**. |

**How the disagreements were settled:**
- *Tutoring or agency?* **Tutoring first.** Your buyers (parents of younger students) are already in your school community, payment comes the same day, and it fits around IB. The agency becomes **Plan B**, or a second business after IB.
- *Solo or hire?* **Solo for 3 months, then hire.** You learn what works and build a reputation before you sell other people's time.
- *Register a company now?* **No.** Register when the numbers justify it (Section 6). Registering early costs money and time and earns you nothing.

## 2. The numbers (so you know the target is real)

**Typical Mumbai market rates, 2026** (check in Week 1 by asking 5 parents and searching local tutor listings):
- Professional IB/IGCSE tutors: ₹2,500–₹5,000+ per hour
- University-student tutors: ₹1,000–₹2,000 per hour
- **You (a strong DP1 student teaching Grades 6–10): ₹1,000–₹1,500 per hour to start**

**Path to ₹1L/month:**

| Stage | When | Model | Monthly profit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Solo | Months 1–3 | You teach 5 hrs/week, 1:1 at ₹1,200 | about ₹26,000 |
| 2. Groups | Months 3–6 | Two groups of 4 students, 2 hrs/week each, ₹700/student/hr, plus some 1:1 | about ₹45,000–55,000 |
| 3. Agency | Months 6–18 | 8–12 tutors doing about 100 billed hours/month at ₹2,500; tutor gets ₹1,500, you keep ₹1,000 | **₹1,00,000+** |

You spend 4–6 hours a week on Stage 3, mostly matching students, checking quality and talking to parents. That's why it works during DP2.

## 3. Setup: legal, money and tax (Days 1–7, one weekend with a parent)

1. **PAN card for you** (as a minor, applied through a parent): online through Protean (NSDL) or UTIITSL, about ₹107, arrives in 1–3 weeks. You need it for a bank account and tax returns.
2. **Your own savings account.** Most big banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Kotak) let children aged 10 and over **run their own minor account** with UPI and a debit card. Take your Aadhaar, PAN or the PAN acknowledgement, a parent's KYC and photos. **All business money goes into this account and nowhere else.** That keeps it clearly your income.
3. **UPI QR code** for that account. Print it and put it in your WhatsApp Business profile. For now you need no payment gateway. Razorpay or Stripe need business KYC you don't have yet.
4. **WhatsApp Business** (free) on your phone: business name, catalogue showing your 3 packages, auto-reply, and labels (Lead / Trial / Paying / Paused).
5. **Tools, all free:** Google Workspace personal account, Google Forms for intake, Google Sheets for the student list, hours and money, Cal.com or Google Calendar booking page, and Google Meet or Zoom for online sessions.
6. **Tax, in plain terms:**
   - Money you earn from your **own skill** (teaching) is your income, not added to your parents' income. That's the exception for a minor's income from "manual work or any activity involving skill, talent or specialised knowledge" (Section 64(1A) of the Income-tax Act 1961, carried into the new Income-tax Act 2025 that applies from 1 April 2026).
   - Under the new tax regime, a resident individual pays **no tax up to ₹12 lakh** of income a year because of the rebate. Year 1 will almost certainly be tax-free. **File an income tax return anyway** (a CA charges about ₹1,500–3,000). It creates a clean record of your income.
   - **GST:** service providers only need to register above **₹20 lakh a year** in turnover. You won't reach that in this plan before 18.
7. **Contracts:** a minor's contracts generally can't be enforced. So **collect payment in advance** (monthly, before sessions) and never give credit. Your parent is the named contact on the parent agreement in Section 4.

**Cost so far: about ₹500.**

## 4. Days 1–30: launch, step by step

### Week 1: offer and first leads
- **Day 1 (2 hours):** Pick **one subject and one grade band** where you're strongest, e.g. "MYP 4–5 / IGCSE Maths". Being specific beats being general.
- **Day 2:** Write 3 packages:
  - **Trial session:** ₹500 for 60 minutes. It lowers the risk for parents and turns into paying students.
  - **Monthly 1:1:** 8 sessions for ₹9,600 (₹1,200/hr), paid in advance.
  - **Exam sprint:** 6 sessions in the 3 weeks before school exams for ₹7,500.
- **Day 3:** Build a 5-question Google Form: student grade, school, subject, weak topics, parent phone number.
- **Day 4:** Make a list of **40 names**: younger siblings of your friends, your parents' friends with children aged 11–15, cousins, building-society families, and juniors in your school's younger grades. Your parents' network matters most here. Families in your circle tend to pay well for tutoring.
- **Day 5–7:** Send the message below to all 40. Ask your parents to forward it in their WhatsApp groups (school parent groups, building society, community, clubs).

> *Hi Aunty/Uncle, I'm [Name], in IB DP1 at [School]. I'm starting small-batch tutoring for Grade 7–10 Maths (MYP/IGCSE/ICSE), online or at home in [area]. First session is ₹500, and if it doesn't help, it's free. I've got 6 slots this month. Could [child] try one this week? Here's a 1-minute form: [link]*

**Week 1 target:** 5 trial sessions booked.

### Week 2: trials that convert
- Every trial follows the same plan: **10 min** quick diagnostic (5 questions), **35 min** fix the single weakest topic, **10 min** set homework, **5 min** call with the parent: "Here's what I found, here's the 4-week plan."
- Close on the call: *"I can keep Tuesday and Friday 6 pm for [child]. The monthly plan is ₹9,600, paid in advance on UPI. Shall I lock the slot?"*
- Send the parent a **one-page agreement** (Google Doc): fees paid in advance, 24-hour cancellation rule, no refunds after a session has happened, sessions online or with a parent at home, and **"I don't write coursework, IAs or homework for students."**

**Week 2 target:** 3 paying students. That's about ₹28,800/month at 6 hrs/week, which is close to your ceiling.

### Week 3–4: systemise from day one
- After each session, send a **2-line WhatsApp progress note** to the parent. This is what keeps parents paying and gets you referrals.
- Ask every paying parent: *"If you know one family who'd benefit, I'll give you one free session."*
- Track everything in your Sheet: student, hours, paid (Y/N), referral source.
- Start a **question bank** in Google Drive (topic → worksheets → answers). It becomes your training material when you hire tutors.

**End of Month 1:** 3–5 students, ₹20–30k in your account, about 5–6 hrs/week.

## 5. Months 2–18: from your own hours to a business

### Months 2–3: groups (more money for the same hours)
- When you get a 5th inquiry you can't fit in, start a **batch**: 4 students at the same level, ₹700/student/hr (₹2,800/hr to you). Parents see it as cheaper, and you earn more than double.
- Give it a simple brand: a name, a logo from Canva, and an Instagram page showing student results (only with the parent's permission and first names).

### Months 3–6: first 2 tutors
- **Who to hire:** IB alumni from your school now at university, and strong DP2 students in other subjects. Pay them ₹1,200–1,500/hr. Charge ₹2,000–2,500/hr.
- **Recruitment message:** post it in your school's alumni WhatsApp and LinkedIn: "Paid tutoring, ₹1,500/hr, flexible, online, MYP/IGCSE students."
- **Quality control:** every tutor uses your question bank and session format. You sit in on their first 2 sessions, and the parent progress note is required after every session.
- **Payment flow:** the parent pays **you**, and you pay the tutor within 7 days of month-end. Always keep the client relationship yourself.
- **Tutor agreement:** a simple Google Doc covering rate, no direct poaching of students for 12 months, safeguarding rules, and no coursework writing.

### Months 6–18: agency (about ₹1L/month)
- Grow to 8–12 tutors across Maths, Sciences, Economics and English. Add **IB DP subjects only when taught by alumni with a 6–7 in that subject**, never by you for your own classmates.
- Add a **booking page** (Cal.com plus a Google Form) and a **parent FAQ page** (free Google Sites or Carrd).
- Your weekly 4–6 hours: new parent calls, matching students to tutors, checking 2 progress notes per tutor, payroll at month-end.
- **The ₹1L/month formula:** about 100 billed tutor-hours × ₹1,000 margin. Track two numbers weekly: **billed hours** and **parent renewal rate** (target over 80%).

## 6. When the setup must change (the CA's triggers)

| Trigger | Action |
|---|---|
| You hire your first tutor | Pay tutors by bank or UPI with a note, and keep a record. Ask a CA (about ₹2–5k) whether agency income is still clearly your own skill income or should be **added to a parent's income**, and whether to run it as a parent's sole proprietorship for now. |
| Turnover nears ₹15 lakh a year | Get GST advice before you reach ₹20 lakh. Open a current account in the proprietor's name. |
| You turn 18 | Move the business into your own name: a sole proprietorship (Udyam registration is free and online), or an LLP or Private Limited company if you're raising money or hiring staff. Minors can't be company directors, so wait until 18 rather than rushing it. |
| Revenue over ₹50 lakh a year, or big expansion | Private Limited company, proper contracts, and payment gateway KYC. |

## 7. Plan B (if tutoring isn't your thing): a local business services agency

Use the same steps with a different offer. **"I set up WhatsApp Business catalogues, Google Business Profiles and simple websites for local shops, clinics and family businesses."**
- **Price:** ₹15,000–₹40,000 per setup, plus ₹3,000–₹8,000/month for maintenance and posting.
- **Leads:** your parents' business contacts, which a family with this net worth almost always has (suppliers, clinics, CAs, restaurants, showrooms).
- **Week 1:** build 1 free sample for a family friend's business and screenshot the before and after. **Week 2–3:** pitch 20 contacts. **Target:** 3 clients by Month 2. **₹1L/month** = about 10 retainer clients at ₹6k plus 1 new setup a month.
- **Tools:** Canva, Google Sites or Wix, WhatsApp Business, Google Business Profile, and ChatGPT or Claude for drafting copy.

Same rules apply: advance payment, your own bank account, a parent signs the agreements, and IB comes first.

## 8. Why not use the family's money? (Answer this before someone asks)

- **Money doesn't fix the hard part.** The hard part is finding a paying customer. Funding a café, D2C brand or franchise at 16 means paying rent and buying stock before you know anyone wants it, while you're in school 8 hours a day. Most fail. You'd learn less than you would from 3 paying students.
- **Rule:** you can ask the family for **one-time capital only after reaching ₹50k/month profit for 3 months in a row**, and only for something that clearly grows that business (e.g. a ₹1–2 lakh budget for tutor recruitment and ads). Write it down as a family agreement.
- **The real advantage of your family's wealth is the network** (who your parents know) and **the safety net** (you can take risks without needing the income). Use both and leave the capital alone.

## 9. Stress-tested: what breaks and how it's fixed

**1. "My DP grades are falling."**
*What breaks:* the business takes over study time, predicted grades drop, and university options shrink.
*Fix:* hard limits. **Maximum 6 hrs/week of your own teaching. Zero new students from 4 weeks before any mock or final exam.** Pause the business in exam periods (DP1 end-of-year exams, DP2 mocks, and May of DP2) and let tutors cover. **Kill switch:** if any subject's predicted grade drops by 1 or more points, cut to existing students only until it recovers. Your EE and IA deadlines go in the calendar first, business slots second.

**2. "A parent asks you to write their child's project, IA or homework" (or offers ₹20,000 for it).**
*What breaks:* academic misconduct. It risks the student's grades, your school standing, and potentially **your own IB diploma** if it involves IB work.
*Fix:* the "no coursework writing" clause is in every parent and tutor agreement. Say no every time: *"I teach the method, the student does the work."* Never tutor your own DP classmates on their IAs.

**3. "We can't find students, the market's full of tutoring apps."**
*What breaks:* the 40-name list produces 0 trials.
*Fix:* (a) Cut the trial price to free for the first 3 students in exchange for a written testimonial. (b) Switch to a narrower niche: "IGCSE Maths exam sprint" in the 6 weeks before exams sells itself. (c) Ask your school's MYP coordinator if you can run a free 1-hour revision session for Grade 9–10. Free sessions bring paid inquiries. (d) If still nothing after 4 weeks, switch to Plan B. Total loss: about ₹500 and 4 weekends.

**4. "Safety: going into strangers' homes, or a parent complains."**
*What breaks:* personal safety for you, and child-safeguarding risk for students who are younger than you.
*Fix:* **Online by default.** In-person only at homes of families you know, in a shared room, with an adult in the house. Never alone with a student behind closed doors. Tutors follow the same rule in their agreement. Complaints are handled within 24 hours with a free make-up session, and a parent reviews any serious complaint with you.

**5. "Legal or payment problems: a parent doesn't pay, the bank asks questions, or a tutor poaches students."**
*What breaks:* a minor can't easily enforce a contract, and large unexplained payments into a minor's account can be flagged.
*Fix:* **Advance payment only**, so there's nothing to chase. UPI notes on every payment ("Tuition – [student] – Oct"). A monthly income summary in your Sheet. File an ITR every year with a CA. For poaching, the tutor agreement has a 12-month no-poaching clause, but your real protection is **owning the parent relationship** (they message you, not the tutor).

**Extra check: "Will this count for CAS?"**
No. Paid work doesn't count as CAS. But you can run a **free weekly tutoring session for an NGO or a municipal school** (for example through a Mumbai education NGO near you). That counts as a CAS *Service* experience, and it trains you as a teacher too.
