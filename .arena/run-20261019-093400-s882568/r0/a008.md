# A Business Plan for a 16-Year-Old DP1 Student in Mumbai

## 1. The answer in one paragraph

Start an **IB-specialist tutoring and exam-bootcamp business for Mumbai's international-school students** (IGCSE / MYP / early DP). Start by tutoring yourself, then grow it into a small agency of vetted tutors (IB alumni aged 18 and over), with paid group bootcamps timed to exam season. Run it legally through a **sole proprietorship in one parent's name**, because a minor cannot sign enforceable contracts in India. Your own tutoring income goes into **your own PAN and bank account**. Cap family capital at **₹5 lakh (about US$5,700, under 0.1% of net worth) until you turn 18**, and release it in tranches only when the business hits written targets. The target is **₹1 lakh/month profit (job-replacement Level 1) by about mid-to-late 2027**, without your IB grades dropping. At 18, move the business into a private limited company that you control.

The rest of this document gives the constraints behind this choice, why it beats the alternatives, and the week-by-week plan with numbers, gates, legal steps and kill criteria.

---

## 2. Assumptions (where the request is ambiguous)

1. **"Job replacing money"** means recurring **net profit** (after costs, before personal tax) of:
   - **Level 1: ₹1,00,000/month** (₹12 lakh/year). That is above typical entry-level graduate pay in India.
   - **Level 2: ₹3,00,000/month** (₹36 lakh/year). That is closer to a well-paid Mumbai professional.

   Level 1 is the target while you are at school. Level 2 is a post-school target.
2. The student started DP1 in mid-2026, so IB final exams are **May 2028**, results come in early July 2028, and university applications run from mid-2027 to early 2028.
3. The student turns 18 sometime **between late 2027 and late 2028**, depending on birthday.
4. The family will support the plan with a parent acting as legal operator and guarantor. They are **not** asking the student to manage family wealth.
5. Exchange rate used: **₹88 ≈ US$1**. Prices quoted for Mumbai tutoring are **estimates you must verify in Week 2** (Section 7, Phase 1).
6. This is not legal or tax advice. Every legal or tax step below is marked for confirmation with a Chartered Accountant (CA). Budget for one: about ₹10–20k/year at this scale.

## 3. The hard constraints (they drive everything)

| # | Constraint | Exact implication |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Time.** DP1 involves 6 subjects, plus the Extended Essay (4,000 words), TOK essay and exhibition, Internal Assessments in each subject, and CAS. | Real spare capacity during term is about **6–8 hours/week**, near zero in school exam weeks and IA deadline weeks, and lower again in DP2. **The business must run with you working 4–8 hrs/week, mostly evenings and weekends.** This is the tightest constraint. |
| C2 | **Legal capacity.** Under Indian law a minor's contract is void (Indian Contract Act 1872, s.11; *Mohori Bibee v Dharmodas Ghose*, 1903). Minors cannot in practice be company directors or full LLP partners. | You cannot be the legal counterparty for leases, supplier contracts, payment-gateway merchant accounts or employment. **An adult (a parent) must hold the business legally until you are 18.** |
| C3 | **Banking.** Since RBI's April 2025 directions, minors aged 10 and over may open and operate their own savings account independently, within bank limits. A minor can also hold a PAN (applied for through a guardian). | You **can** receive your own earnings into your own account under your own PAN. |
| C4 | **Income-tax clubbing.** A minor's income is added to the parent's income, **except** income from manual work or from "skill, talent, specialised knowledge and experience". This was s.64(1A) of the 1961 Act, and the Income-tax Act 2025 (in force from 1 April 2026) carries the rule over. Confirm the new section number with your CA. | Fees **you personally earn by tutoring** count as skill income: they stay yours and are taxed on your own return. Under the new regime the first ₹4 lakh is nil-rate and the s.87A-type rebate covers income up to ₹12 lakh, so your own tutoring income is likely effectively untaxed at this scale. **Profit from other tutors' work** is business income and belongs in the parent's proprietorship. |
| C5 | **GST.** The registration threshold for services in Maharashtra is **₹20 lakh aggregate turnover per PAN per financial year**. Private coaching is taxable at **18%** (only recognised schools and courses are exempt). Aggregate turnover counts **everything under that PAN**. | Run the business under the PAN of the parent with **no other business turnover**, or the parent's existing turnover will push you over the threshold on day one. Watch the ₹20 lakh line (Section 9). |
| C6 | **Capital is not a constraint.** The family has ~US$8M (~₹70 crore). | The risk is the opposite: **overspending**. Money cannot buy what you lack, which is time, experience and customer evidence. Capital is released only against evidence. |
| C7 | **IB academic integrity.** | You must never write, edit substantially, or "polish" anyone's IA, EE or TOK work. You must never sell IB past papers or markschemes, which are IBO copyright. Breaking either rule could cost **your own diploma**, not just the client's. |
| C8 | **Child safety and data.** Your customers are minors. The Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and Rules (2025, phasing in through 2027) require verifiable parental consent to process children's data. POCSO law applies to anyone working with children. | The parent is always the paying, consenting customer. You need a written safeguarding code, and online sessions must be recorded or held in a common area. Collect only the data you need. |
| C9 | **University plans.** If you study in the US (F-1) or UK (Student visa), you generally **cannot actively work** for your business there. Passive ownership is usually fine; doing the work is not. | Before you leave India (about Aug–Sept 2028), the business must run **without you**: a paid manager, documented systems, and you in an owner/board role. Confirm with an immigration adviser for your specific country. |
| C10 | **Location: Mumbai.** | Mumbai has a dense cluster of IB and Cambridge schools (Bandra–Juhu–Andheri, Powai, Lower Parel–Worli, BKC, Malabar Hill) and a very large paid-tuition culture. Commute times make **online-first delivery** the only way to fit C1. |

**Design rule from C1 and C2:** choose a business that (a) produces cash with under 8 hours/week of your time, (b) needs no contracts signed by you, (c) uses an advantage only a current IB student has, and (d) can be handed to a manager by mid-2028.

## 4. Why this business and not the others

I tested three genuinely different approaches against the constraints above.

| Option | What it is | Fails on | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. D2C product brand** (e.g. a snack or apparel brand on Shopify, Amazon, Blinkit) | Use family money for inventory, branding and ads | **C1**: inventory, suppliers, returns and marketplace ops need daytime hours. **C2**: supplier and marketplace contracts. **C6**: burns capital before any evidence. Typical payback is 2–4 years. | Rejected for now. Good after age 18. |
| **B. Buy an existing small online business** (Flippa / Acquire.com, ₹20–80 lakh) | Use capital to buy cash flow | **C2**: you cannot own or sign it yourself. FEMA/ODI rules complicate buying foreign entities. **C1**: an acquired business still needs an operator. You learn little; your parents effectively make the investment. | Rejected. That is the parents' investment, not your business. |
| **C. IB-specialist tutoring and bootcamp agency** | Use your current IB knowledge and school network | Fits C1 (evenings and weekends, online), C2 (parent's proprietorship), C4 (your tutoring is skill income), C6 (under ₹5 lakh), C7/C8 (manageable with written rules), C9 (an agency can be run by a manager). | **Chosen.** |

Other ideas, ruled out quickly:
- **F&O or crypto trading.** SEBI's studies found around **9 in 10 individual F&O traders lose money**, and it builds no business.
- **An app or "startup idea".** It needs engineering time you do not have, and has no revenue for 12+ months.
- **A franchise.** It needs contracts and capital, gives low learning, and the franchisor holds the upside.

Why C is the strongest on the task's own terms:
1. It produces **cash within about 3 weeks**, not years.
2. Its realistic ceiling clears Level 1: Mumbai has **thousands of IB/IGCSE families** and tutoring spend of ₹50k–₹5 lakh per child per year is common.
3. The skills it builds (sales, hiring, pricing, unit economics, managing people) carry over to any later business.
4. **It strengthens your IB and university application.** It can count toward CAS, especially the CAS project if you involve other students. It also gives you a clear story for essays and interviews.

## 5. The business, exactly

**Working name:** choose something neutral, such as "[YourName] Academic Studio" or "Bridge to IB". **Do not** use your school's name or the "IB" logo in branding: "International Baccalaureate" and "IB" are IBO trademarks. Describe the service in words ("tutoring for IB Diploma students"), but do not imply IBO endorsement.

**Customers:** parents of students at Mumbai IB, IGCSE and international schools, in three segments:
1. **Grade 9–10 (IGCSE/MYP).** They need subject tutoring and **"DP readiness"** before entering DP1.
2. **DP1 students one year behind you,** and **DP2 students** in subjects where you are strong. Only take DP2 tutoring if you are clearly ahead in that subject. Otherwise use an alumni tutor.
3. **Exam season:** IGCSE (May–June), MYP eAssessment (May), IB mocks (Jan–Feb) and IB finals (May).

**Products and pricing.** These are starting prices; validate them in Phase 1.

| Product | Delivered by | Price to parent | Paid to tutor | Gross margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1:1 tutoring, you personally (subjects where you score 6–7) | You | ₹1,200–1,500/hr | n/a (your skill income) | ~100% |
| 1:1 tutoring, alumni tutor (IB graduate, 18+, scored 40+ or a 7 in the subject) | Tutor | ₹1,800/hr | ₹1,200/hr | ₹600/hr (33%) |
| Small-group exam bootcamp (6–8 students, 8 sessions × 2 hrs) | Tutor (you assist) | ₹14,000/student | ₹2,000/hr × 16 hrs = ₹32,000/cohort | 8 × 14,000 − 32,000 = **₹80,000/cohort** |
| "DP Readiness" summer course (Grade 10 → DP1: subject choice, IA/EE/TOK orientation, study systems) | You and a tutor | ₹18,000/student | ₹40,000/cohort | 10 × 18,000 − 40,000 = **₹1,40,000/cohort** |

**Price check before launch:** experienced IB tutors in Mumbai commonly charge well above ₹2,000/hr, and college-student tutors less. In Phase 1 you will collect **at least 10 real quotes** from parents (what they currently pay, and to whom) and adjust prices so you sit **15–30% below established adult tutors**.

**What you will never sell:** IA, EE or TOK writing or editing; "predicted grade" promises; IB past papers. Your own handwritten notes and study guides are fine. Explaining markscheme criteria in your own words is fine.

## 6. Legal, banking and tax setup (Phase 0, weeks 1–2)

| Step | Who | Exact action | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Student and guardian | Apply for a **PAN for the student** (Form 49A via NSDL/Protean or UTIITSL, with a parent as representative assessee) if you do not have one | ~₹107 |
| 2 | Student | Open a **minor's savings account in your own name**, operated by you (RBI 2025 allows independent operation from age 10, subject to bank limits). Link a UPI ID. | ₹0 |
| 3 | Parent with **no other business turnover** (C5) | Register a **sole proprietorship**: get **Udyam registration** (udyamregistration.gov.in, free, Aadhaar-based), then open a **current account** in the trade name | ₹0 to minimum-balance requirement |
| 4 | Parent | Maharashtra Shops & Establishments: an establishment with under 10 workers files an **online intimation** only (no full licence). Confirm on the MahaKamgar portal. | ₹0 |
| 5 | Parent | **Razorpay** (or Cashfree) account in the proprietorship's name for payment links and subscriptions. Standard domestic fee is about **2% per transaction**. | ~2% of revenue |
| 6 | Parent and student | Sign a 1-page **Family Founder Memorandum** (template in Section 11) recording that you are the founder, the profit split, and the transfer of the business to your company at 18 | ₹0 |
| 7 | Parent | Engage a **CA**: bookkeeping in Zoho Books (free tier) or Google Sheets, ITR for the parent (business income) and for the student (skill income), and a GST threshold watch | ₹10–20k/yr |
| 8 | Optional, Phase 2 | **Trademark** application, Class 41 (education), in the parent's name, assigned later. E-filing fee is ₹4,500 for individuals and MSMEs. | ₹4,500 |
| 9 | Student | Read your **school's policy** on paid tutoring of other students at the same school. Some schools restrict it. If restricted, target other schools only. | ₹0 |

**Money flow (keep it exactly like this):**
- Parents of **your own** tutees pay **you** via UPI into **your** account. This is skill income on your PAN, and you file your own ITR (signed by a guardian while you are a minor).
- Parents of **alumni-tutor and bootcamp** customers pay the **proprietorship**. The proprietorship pays tutors (by bank transfer, with a tutor invoice) and keeps the margin. The parent's ITR shows this business income.
- Each month the proprietorship pays you a **documented "founder management fee"** for the work you do, at a modest rate that your CA agrees is defensible. Alternatively, profit is left to accumulate and is transferred to your company at 18. A gift from a parent to a child is tax-exempt as a gift from a "relative", but your CA must confirm how clubbing interacts with it.

## 7. The plan, week by week

Time budget rule: **you spend at most 8 hrs/week during term, 2 hrs/week during school exam weeks and IA deadline weeks, and 15 hrs/week in holidays.** Track it in a sheet. If you exceed it two weeks in a row, cut scope, not sleep.

### Phase 0: Set up (26 Oct – 8 Nov 2026, 2 weeks, about 6 hrs/week)
1. Complete Section 6 steps 1–7.
2. Build a **Google Site or Carrd page** (Carrd is about US$19/year) with: who you are, subjects, prices, a parent consent and booking form (Google Form), and safeguarding rules.
3. Get a **WhatsApp Business** number. If you want a parent's number on file, use a dual-SIM line in the parent's name.
4. Write your **Safeguarding & Integrity Code** (1 page). It sets: online sessions only, in the student's common area or recorded with parental consent; no private chat with students (all messages go in a group that includes the parent); no IA, EE or TOK writing; a POCSO-aware reporting line to the parent operator.
5. Pick **your 2 strongest subjects**, ideally ones where you are predicted a 7 and ones IGCSE/MYP students struggle with, e.g. Mathematics, Physics or Economics.
6. **Budget released: Tranche 0 = ₹25,000** (tools, domain, CA retainer, printing).

### Phase 1: Validate (9 Nov 2026 – 31 Jan 2027, 12 weeks, about 6–8 hrs/week; Diwali break used for setup)
Weekly rhythm: **2 sessions × 1.5 hrs of tutoring per weekday evening** at most, plus **1 hr of sales/admin on Saturday** and a **30-minute Sunday review**.

1. **Weeks 1–2: 20 parent conversations.** Work through your parents' network, relatives and family friends with Grade 8–11 children at international schools. Ask exactly these 5 questions:
   1. Does your child have a tutor now? For which subject?
   2. How much do you pay per hour, and how often?
   3. What frustrates you about the current tutor?
   4. Would you try a 1-hour session with me at ₹[price] if the first one is free if you are not happy?
   5. Who else should I speak to?

   Record the answers in a sheet. **Output:** a real price range and at least 5 trial bookings.
2. **Weeks 2–6: tutor 4–6 students personally,** 4–6 hrs/week. After every session, send the parent a **3-line WhatsApp report** (covered / weakness / next step). This report is your retention engine.
3. **Weeks 4–8: recruit 3 alumni tutors.** Look among IB graduates aged 18+ who are at Mumbai colleges or on gap years: school alumni groups, your seniors from 1–2 years ago, LinkedIn. Screen with:
   - IB transcript (40+, or a 7 in the subject)
   - a 20-minute demo lesson to you
   - 2 references
   - ID check
   - signing the Safeguarding & Integrity Code and a **tutor agreement with the proprietorship** (adult to adult, so it is enforceable).
4. **Weeks 6–12: hand overflow demand to alumni tutors** through the proprietorship (₹1,800 to the parent, ₹1,200 to the tutor).
5. **Plan the first bootcamp** for IB mock season or IGCSE revision. Sell it in January, run it in February–March.

**Gate 1 (31 Jan 2027).** All must be true to release Tranche 1 (₹1,00,000):
- At least **8 paying students** in total (yours plus tutors')
- At least **70% of students who started in Nov–Dec still active in January**
- At least **₹60,000 cumulative revenue** across both money flows
- At least **2 alumni tutors** with paying students
- **Your own IB predicted grades unchanged or higher** compared with your September 2026 baseline. *This condition is non-negotiable.*

If Gate 1 fails: run **one** 6-week fix cycle on the one metric that failed (e.g. price, segment or subject). If it fails again, see the kill criteria in Section 10.

### Phase 2: Exam-season scale (1 Feb – 31 May 2027, about 6–8 hrs/week)
1. Grow to **6 alumni tutors** covering Maths AA/AI, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Economics and English.
2. Run **3–4 bootcamps** (e.g. "IGCSE Physics Final Sprint" and "IB Maths Paper 1 & 2 Mock Rescue"), each 6–8 students, online on Google Meet, on weekend mornings.
3. Marketing, ₹10,000/month at most:
   - **Instagram/Meta ads** targeted at parents aged 35–55 within 5 km of Bandra, Juhu, Powai, Worli or Lower Parel, with an interest in international schools
   - **Referral offer:** one free hour for each referred family that books 4+ hours
   - **Parent WhatsApp community groups.** Get permission from the group admin; never spam.
4. Your own role shifts. You tutor **at most 3 hrs/week**. The remaining time goes on sales calls, tutor quality checks (sit in on 1 session per tutor per month), and finance.
5. **CAS:** if you involve classmates (for example, as a team that writes free study guides for a low-income school as an outreach arm), document it as a **CAS project**. Confirm with your CAS coordinator that a paid business element is acceptable. Usually the service or creative component must be the substance.

**Gate 2 (31 May 2027)** releases Tranche 2 (₹1,50,000) for a part-time operations coordinator. All must be true:
- **Revenue run-rate at least ₹1.5 lakh/month** (average of March–May)
- **Operating profit at least ₹40,000/month** (proprietorship margin plus your own tutoring, minus all costs)
- **No safeguarding or integrity complaint** upheld
- **Predicted grades held**

### Phase 3: Summer machine (June – July 2027, up to 15 hrs/week, and the EE still comes first)
1. Run **2–3 "DP Readiness" cohorts** for students entering DP1 in June/August 2027. This is your highest-margin product and you are the most credible seller, having just done it.
2. **Hire a part-time operations coordinator**: an 18+ college student, 15 hrs/week, at **₹20,000–25,000/month**, contracted by the proprietorship. They handle scheduling, payment follow-ups, parent reports and tutor timesheets. Everything is documented as a process in a shared Google Doc ("the playbook").
3. Do the **Extended Essay draft and university shortlisting** this summer as well. Block the mornings for IB and the afternoons for business.

### Phase 4: DP2, defend not grow (Aug 2027 – May 2028, 4–5 hrs/week, and 0–1 hr/week from 15 Feb 2028 to the end of finals)
1. **No new products.** Keep tutors, bootcamps and the coordinator running.
2. Your weekly job: **one 90-minute block** for reviewing numbers, one tutor sit-in, and one sales call. The coordinator does everything else.
3. **From 15 Feb 2028 to your last IB paper:** move to monthly reviews only. Pre-sell the summer 2028 DP Readiness cohorts in January so they run without you.
4. **University applications** (Common App / UCAS deadlines, Oct 2027 – Jan 2028): the business is your strongest activity. Report real numbers (students served, tutors managed, revenue band), verified by the CA statement.

### Phase 5: Post-exams and age 18 (May 2028 onward)
1. **On turning 18:**
   - Incorporate a **private limited company** with yourself as director and majority shareholder (SPICe+ on the MCA portal; professional fees plus government fees are typically ₹10–25k; annual compliance including a mandatory audit is about ₹40–80k).
   - Transfer the business assets (brand, domain, customer list, tutor contracts) from the proprietorship to the company, structured by your CA.
   - Your parent can stay as second director or adviser.
2. **May–August 2028 (if going abroad):** hire or promote a **General Manager** on a salary plus profit share (e.g. ₹40–60k/month plus 10% of operating profit). Your role becomes board-level only, to stay compliant with C9.
3. **If you stay in India** (e.g. Ashoka, FLAME, an IIT, or a gap year), the paths to Level 2 (₹3 lakh/month) are:
   - **2nd city online** (Bengaluru, Delhi NCR and Pune IB schools)
   - **Content products**: recorded courses sold at ₹3–8k each, with near-zero marginal cost
   - **A university-admissions arm.** Only do this with experienced adult counsellors; you are not credible alone at 18.

## 8. The numbers: will this actually replace a job?

**Base-case model at Gate 2 (May 2027), monthly:**

| Line | Calculation | ₹/month |
|---|---|---|
| Alumni-tutor hours | 6 tutors × avg 7 hrs/week × 4.33 = 182 hrs | |
| Tutor revenue | 182 × ₹1,800 | 3,27,600 |
| Tutor pay | 182 × ₹1,200 | (2,18,400) |
| Bootcamp margin | 1 cohort/month avg × ₹80,000 | 80,000 |
| Payment fees | 2% × (3,27,600 + 1,12,000 bootcamp revenue) | (8,792) |
| Ads and marketing | | (10,000) |
| Tools (Workspace, Carrd, Zoho, misc.) | | (3,000) |
| CA (₹15k/yr ÷ 12) | | (1,250) |
| **Proprietorship operating profit** | | **≈ 1,66,158** |
| Your own tutoring | 3 hrs/week × 4.33 × ₹1,400 | 18,186 |
| **Total profit attributable to you** | | **≈ ₹1.84 lakh** |

Two adjustments:
- **Conservative case.** Assume tutors average only 3.5 hrs/week and there is one bootcamp every 2 months. Then profit is about **₹55k + ₹40k + ₹18k − ₹22k costs ≈ ₹91k/month**, roughly Level 1.
- **Coordinator cost** (from Phase 3): subtract ₹25,000.

So **Level 1 (₹1 lakh/month) is reachable by mid-to-late 2027 in the base case. In the conservative case it arrives in 2028.** These are targets with stated assumptions, not forecasts. Gate data will tell you within 3 months which case you are in.

**GST trigger (C5):** the base case annualises to about ₹4.4 lakh × 12 ≈ **₹53 lakh turnover**, well above ₹20 lakh. When the trailing 12-month proprietorship revenue reaches **₹15 lakh**, you have about 2–3 months before the threshold. At that point choose with your CA:
- **(a) Register for GST** and price GST-inclusive. At the same ₹1,800 the price becomes ₹1,525 + GST, and your margin per tutor hour falls from ₹600 to about ₹325. Raise prices to ₹2,000 inclusive to compensate.
- **(b) Move to a marketplace model.** Tutors (each individually under ₹20 lakh, so exempt) bill parents directly for tuition. The proprietorship bills only a **matching and management fee**, so its turnover is just that fee. This needs genuinely independent tutor-parent contracts and careful drafting. **Do not do it without your CA.**

Personal income tax (C4): your own tutoring income of ~₹2–3 lakh/year is likely nil-tax under the new regime, but **file a return anyway** for a clean record. The proprietorship profit is taxed in your parent's hands at their slab rate. Discuss with the family who bears that cost; the Founder Memorandum should say.

## 9. Family capital: exact rules

| Tranche | When | Amount | Allowed uses |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Phase 0 | ₹25,000 | Domain, site, CA retainer, printing, a decent headset and webcam |
| 1 | Gate 1 passed | ₹1,00,000 | Ads, bootcamp marketing, trademark, tutor onboarding |
| 2 | Gate 2 passed | ₹1,50,000 | Coordinator salary buffer (6 months), course-content production |
| Reserve | Board approval only | ₹1,25,000 | Emergencies, refunds |
| **Total cap until age 18** | | **₹4,00,000–5,00,000** | |

Rules:
1. **No spending on**: an office lease, a custom app, paid PR, a car or "business" gadgets beyond the basics, or influencer fees above ₹10k.
2. **Monthly family board meeting** (45 minutes, first Sunday of each month). You present one sheet: revenue, profit, students, retention, tutor quality score, hours you spent, and current predicted grades. Your parent and ideally one outside adult who runs a business attend.
3. **Repay tranches out of profit before taking any personal draw above the founder fee.** This teaches capital discipline, which is the single most valuable lesson an $8M family can give you.

## 10. Kill, pivot and pause criteria (pre-committed)

- **Pause everything for 4 weeks** if any subject's predicted grade drops by 1 or more, or your total predicted score drops by 2 or more. Restart only once it has recovered.
- **Pivot** if Gate 1 fails twice. Pivot options, in order:
  1. A different segment (e.g. only Grade 9–10 "DP readiness")
  2. A different channel (partner with an existing tutoring centre as their IB specialist, paid per head)
  3. A different product (written study guides and courses only, with no live hours)
- **Stop the business** immediately and permanently if any safeguarding incident is substantiated, or if any tutor is found writing assessed IB work. Refund affected families and inform them in writing.
- **Do not chase Level 2 before May 2028.** Growth beyond Gate 2 during DP2 is not a goal.
