# From DP1 to ₹1 lakh a month: one business, worked out in rupees

## The answer in one paragraph

Start a **small-group tutoring business for younger students (MYP 4–5 / IGCSE / Grade 9–10)** in the one subject where you score a 7. Teach the first groups yourself in DP1, use a summer "bridge to DP" course to fill a waitlist, then hand the teaching to 4 paid tutors so the business runs on about 3 hours a week of your time during DP2. With the numbers below, that reaches **about ₹1,10,000 a month in profit by roughly October 2027**. That is close to a ₹12–13 lakh a year salary, which is a good starting package for a Mumbai graduate. It needs **no company, no website, no investment and no family capital**, and nothing in it needs you to be 18.

## Assumptions

- "Job replacing money" means **₹1,00,000 a month in profit before tax**, comparable to a ₹12 LPA salary (CTC). If you mean more, the same plan scales by adding tutors.
- You started DP1 in mid-2026 and sit your final exams in May 2028. Your IB diploma matters more than any business, because it decides your university options.
- You have, or can show, top marks (a 7 or A*) in at least one subject: Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Economics or English. If you don't, see "If tutoring isn't you" below.
- Prices are estimates for affluent Mumbai families. Step 1 checks them against real parents before you rely on them.
- This is general information, not legal or tax advice. The family's CA should confirm the tax points.

## The plan, worked through with real numbers

### Step 0: Set up in one evening (week of 19 Oct 2026)

Use only these tools:
1. **WhatsApp Business** (free). Make one group per tutoring group, with the parents in it.
2. **Google Calendar and Google Sheets** (free). One sheet holds everything: student, parent, fee, paid date, sessions held.
3. **A paid video plan** such as Zoom Pro or Google Workspace, at about ₹1,000–2,000 a month. Free plans cut group calls at 40–60 minutes. Teach **online**, because Mumbai traffic would otherwise use up your hours.
4. **A separate bank account with UPI.** A minor aged 10 or over can operate a savings account in India. Set it up with a parent as joint holder or guardian.

Don't add a logo, a website, a company or an app.

### Step 1: Test whether anyone will pay (19 Oct – 30 Nov 2026)

**The product:** a group of 3 students, two 1-hour online sessions a week, at **₹6,500 a month per student**, paid in advance for 8 sessions. Missed sessions are not refunded unless the family gives 24 hours' notice. That works out to about ₹800 per student-hour, well under the ₹2,000–5,000 an hour that experienced IB/IGCSE tutors in Mumbai charge one-to-one.

**Send this message to 20 families.** Choose your parents' friends, neighbours and your school's MYP families who have children in Grade 9–10:

> "Hi Aunty/Uncle, I'm [Name], in DP1 at [School]. I scored a 7 in MYP5 Maths [or your subject]. I'm starting a small online group for Grade 9–10 Maths: 3 students, Mon and Thu 6–7 pm, ₹6,500/month, paid in advance. The first session is free with no obligation. Would [child] like to try it? We start Mon 9 Nov."

**Your decision rule:** if 3 families pay by 30 Nov, carry on. If not, change one thing (the subject, the grade or the time slot) and send to 20 new families. If that also fails by 31 Jan 2027, move to a different skill (see "If tutoring isn't you").

### Step 2: Teach the groups yourself (Dec 2026 – Apr 2027)

Grow to **3 groups (9 students)**. That is 6 hours of teaching a week plus 1 hour of admin, which is the most you should take on in DP1. Demand peaks January to May, before MYP eAssessment and IGCSE exams.

Revenue: 9 × ₹6,500 = **₹58,500 a month**, all of it profit except the software.

Run each month the same way:
1. On the 25th, send a payment reminder.
2. On the 1st, collect the fees.
3. Teach.
4. Log each session in the sheet.
5. Ask every family that stays past month 2 for one referral.

### Step 3: Run a summer bridge course (May–June 2027, in your break; check your school calendar)

Sell **"Bridge to DP Maths AA/AI"** (or your subject) to students who are about to start DP1. Make it 12 sessions of 2 hours, in 2 batches of 8 students, at **₹15,000 per student**.

- Gross: 16 × ₹15,000 = **₹2,40,000**.
- Cost: one helper tutor teaches one batch, at ₹1,000/hour × 24 hours = ₹24,000.
- Profit: about **₹2,16,000**.

This step has two jobs. It is your trial run at managing a tutor, and its 16 students are where your DP2-year groups will come from.

### Step 4: Hand over the teaching (June–Oct 2027, then all of DP2)

1. **Hire 4 tutors.** Look for your school's IB alumni who now study at Mumbai colleges, or who are on a gap year, and who scored a 7 in the subject. Check each one this way: you watch one demo session, and you get one reference from a teacher.
2. **Pay them ₹1,000 an hour**, by UPI on the 5th, after families have paid on the 1st. Never pay a tutor out of money you haven't collected yet.
3. **Hand over your own 3 groups.** Then form new groups from the bridge-course students and from referrals.
4. **Your job becomes 3 hours a week:** scheduling, collecting fees, dropping into one session a week to check quality, and talking to parents.

**The target state is 10 groups (30 students), each tutor running 2–3 groups:**

| Line | Monthly |
|---|---|
| Fees: 30 × ₹6,500 | ₹1,95,000 |
| Tutors: 10 groups × 8 hrs × ₹1,000 | −₹80,000 |
| Software and no-show buffer | −₹5,000 |
| **Profit** | **₹1,10,000** |

**Check what happens if you fall short:** with only 5 groups, profit is about ₹55,000 a month. Every extra group adds ₹11,500.

### Step 5: Exams, turning 18, and making it formal (Mar 2028 onwards)

- **March to May 2028:** take no new students. The tutors run the existing groups, and you spend about 1 hour a week on fee collection.
- **After you turn 18:** register the business properly, as a sole proprietorship with an Udyam registration, and later an LLP if the family wants one. Sign the tutor agreements in your own name. From that point the family's money can fund an expansion if the business has proven itself.

## Rules you must not skip

**Contracts.** In India a contract with a minor is void (Indian Contract Act, s.11). So keep all fees prepaid, which means you never have to enforce anything. Anything that needs a signature, such as a tutor agreement or a card subscription, is signed by a parent.

**Tax.** A minor's income is normally added to a parent's income (the clubbing rule: s.64(1A) of the 1961 Act, carried into the Income-tax Act 2025). There is one exception: income from the minor's **own skill or talent** is not clubbed. Your own teaching in Steps 2–3 probably counts as skill income, but the profit from other tutors in Step 4 probably doesn't. For a family like yours, that means Step 4 profit could be taxed at your parent's top rate. Plan to set aside about 35% until the CA tells you otherwise.

**GST.** Private tutoring is not GST-exempt. You must register once turnover crosses **₹20 lakh in a financial year** (April–March), and after that you charge 18%. In this plan, FY 2027–28 turnover comes to about ₹16 lakh. If the running total in your sheet reaches **₹15 lakh**, ask the CA to register (in a parent's name while you are a minor) before you cross ₹20 lakh.

**IB academic integrity.** Never write, edit or "fix" anyone's IA, EE or TOK essay. Teach concepts only. Don't tutor anyone in your own IB cohort. A malpractice finding could cost you your own diploma.

**CAS.** Paid work does not count towards CAS, so keep the two separate.

**Safeguarding.** You will be teaching other people's children:
- Teach online, in groups only.
- Keep parents in every WhatsApp group.
- Don't have private one-to-one chats with students.
- Make all tutors follow the same rules.

## Stop rules

- **Grades:** if any predicted grade drops 1 point below where it was in October 2026, halve your hours until it recovers. In DP2 you only manage the business, so this rule should rarely trigger.
- **Demand:** if you don't have 3 paying students by 30 Nov 2026, change the offer. If you still have none by 31 Jan 2027, change the skill.
- **Hiring:** add a tutor only when you have a waitlist of at least 3 students, enough for a full group. Never hire first and look for students afterwards.

## If tutoring isn't you

Keep the same skeleton: a skill you already have, sold to people who already know you, prepaid every month, scaled by adding people only once there is a waitlist. Change only the skill:

| Skill you have | First offer | Price | How it scales |
|---|---|---|---|
| Python / no-code / AI tools | Automate one task (invoices, WhatsApp replies, reports) for businesses your parents know | ₹25,000 setup + ₹5,000/month upkeep | Repeat for 15–20 clients, then hire a college student to build |
| Video editing | Edit Reels/Shorts for Mumbai doctors, CAs, restaurants and creators | ₹20,000/month for 12 videos | Hire 2–3 editors at ₹8,000–10,000 each, keep the client relationship |
| Design / Canva | Monthly social media posts for local shops and clinics | ₹12,000/month | Same as above |

## What to do with the $8 million

Almost nothing. Don't take more than ₹25,000 from the family, and the plan above needs less than that. A café, a franchise or a D2C brand funded with family money would mean leases and supplier contracts you can't legally sign. It would also take more of your time than DP allows, and it would teach you less than earning your first ₹58,500 a month from nothing. The family's real advantages are its **network** (your first 20 messages), its **CA**, and a **safety net** that lets you try this at all. Once a business has earned money and you are 18, family capital can help it grow.
