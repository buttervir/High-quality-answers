# From IB DP1 to Job-Replacing Income: An Exact Plan for a 16-Year-Old in Mumbai

## Assumptions

- **Today is 19 October 2026.** The student started DP1 in mid-2026, so IB final exams fall in **May 2028**. The student turns 18 around mid-2028 and 21 around mid-2031. If the birthday is earlier or later, move the legal steps marked "at 18" to match.
- **"Job-replacing money"** means owner profit you could live on instead of a salary. I use two targets:
  - **Target 1:** ₹1,00,000 a month net (₹12 lakh a year, roughly a solid graduate starting salary in Mumbai), held for 6 months in a row. **Due by 31 May 2028**, the end of IB.
  - **Target 2:** ₹2,50,000 a month net (₹30 lakh a year, roughly a top-tier fresher package), with the student working 15 hours a week or less. **Due by 30 June 2031**, around graduation.
- **Currency:** ₹88 = US$1. That puts the family's US$8M at about ₹70 crore.
- **School comes first.** The IB Diploma decides university options, and it can't be redone cheaply. The plan caps business time at about 6–8 hours a week in DP1 and 3–4 hours a week in DP2 from January 2028.
- **Indian law and tax** below are stated as I understand them as of late 2026. Items marked **[CA]** or **[Lawyer]** must be confirmed by a chartered accountant or lawyer before you act. The exact questions are listed in Section 7.

## PASS 1: The answer in one paragraph

Don't spend family money to "buy" a business. At 16 your real assets are what you know, the network you live in, and a safety net that lets you take risks, not the ₹70 crore. Start a **skill-based academic coaching business** that you run yourself: tutoring and exam coaching for MYP, IGCSE and IB students in Mumbai and online. It needs less than ₹15,000, it fits around school, and its income can legally count as your own. Then turn it into an **agency**: recruit high-scoring IB graduates as tutors, charge parents ₹1,800–3,500 an hour, pay tutors 60–67%, and keep the rest. Validate it in November–December 2026. Reach 6–8 students of your own by February 2027. Hire your first tutors in April–June 2027. Hire a part-time coordinator before DP2 gets heavy. Freeze growth from January to May 2028 to protect your IB score. Incorporate a private limited company once you turn 18. By May 2028 the base case is ₹75,000–1,00,000 a month of profit. By 2031, with online reach across India and to Gulf IB families plus a hired general manager, it is ₹2.5 lakh a month or more for 15 hours a week. Every stage has a numerical **kill/pivot test**, and there is a pre-built **fallback business** (Track B) if the first test fails.

The rest of this document works backwards from that end state, then lays out the exact forward steps, then goes deep on the parts most likely to break.

## PASS 3 (part 3): Deep dives on the parts most likely to break

### 6.1 Legal structure while you're under 18 (the most commonly misunderstood part)

- **Contracts.** Under Section 11 of the Indian Contract Act 1872, a minor can't contract. The Privy Council in *Mohori Bibee v Dharmodas Ghose* (1903) held a minor's agreement void. Consequences:
  - You can't be sued on your agreements, but **you can't enforce them either**.
  - Tutors and suppliers dealing with you have no legal recourse, which makes serious counterparties wary.
- **What you can lawfully do in your own name:** provide your own services and get paid into your own bank account, with a PAN. A small tutoring practice run on prepayment needs no enforceable contract, because the client pays before the session.
- **What needs an adult:** engaging other tutors, the agency layer. Until you turn 18, your **parent holds that layer as their own sole proprietorship** under your brand, and signs tutor letters. You run it day to day. That income is legally your parent's (Section 6.2), so it isn't "your" money until it moves to your company at 18.
- **Company, partnership, LLP:**
  - A minor can hold fully paid shares through a guardian.
  - In practice a minor can't be appointed director, because directors must be able to contract.
  - Under Section 30 of the Indian Partnership Act 1932, a minor can only be "admitted to the benefits" of a partnership, not be a full partner.
  - An LLP partner must likewise be able to contract.
  - So: **no company before 18. Incorporate in the month you turn 18.**
- **Udyam (MSME) registration and Maharashtra Shops & Establishments:** don't worry about either before the company exists. Ask the CA which applies once you have a few staff. **[CA]**

### 6.2 Tax (exact mechanics)

- **The clubbing rule.** A minor's income is normally added to the income of the parent who earns more. The Income-tax Act 1961 did this in Section 64(1A). The rule carries over into the Income-tax Act 2025, in force from 1 April 2026. **The exception:** income from manual work or from "any activity involving application of [the minor's] skill, talent or specialised knowledge and experience" is taxed as the minor's own income. **Your own tutoring and Track B design work fall within that exception.** That's a major reason this plan favours skill-based work. **[CA: confirm the exact section number and wording in the 2025 Act.]**
- **Your own income:**
  - Filed as your own return under your PAN. For a minor, the guardian typically verifies it. **[CA]**
  - Under the new regime (rates as set by Budget 2025 for FY2025-26), the basic exemption is ₹4 lakh, and the rebate means no tax for a resident individual on income up to ₹12 lakh. Phase 1 income of about ₹4 lakh a year is almost certainly tax-free. **[CA: confirm rates for the current tax year.]**
  - Business income can be filed under presumptive taxation (taxed on deemed profit) or with regular books. **[CA]**
- **Agency income before you turn 18** is your parent's income, taxed in their hands. Don't move it to you informally. If your parent later gifts you money, the gift is tax-free as a gift from a relative, but income earned on it is clubbed back to the parent until you turn 18.
- **GST:**
  - Private tutoring is generally a taxable service. Only education leading to recognised qualifications is exempt.
  - Registration becomes mandatory once total turnover crosses **₹20 lakh** for services in Maharashtra.
  - You'll stay below that until about 2028–29.
  - **Foreign online clients:** export of services is zero-rated, but there are conditions (payment in convertible foreign exchange, an LUT filing once registered). **[CA]**
- **Records from day one:** every receipt and payment in the ledger (Section 6.4), every payment through the bank, never cash. This makes the switch to the company at 18 painless.

### 6.5 IB interaction: CAS, Business Management IA, workload

- **CAS:** paid work generally does **not** count as a CAS experience. Don't try to log the paid tutoring. You *can* run a **free** weekly tutoring programme for an NGO or a municipal school as your CAS Service project (collaborative and sustained, which suits a CAS project). It builds your teaching skill and makes a genuine story. **[Confirm with your CAS coordinator.]**
- **Business Management IA:** the IA is a research project on a real organisation. Using your own business is risky: you'd struggle to show objectivity, and your teacher may not allow it. Ask first, and if in doubt, use another organisation.
- **Extended Essay:** finish it in the summer of 2027, before Phase 3 starts.
- **Hard caps:** 8 hours a week in DP1 terms, 15 hours a week in the summer break (after the EE), 6 hours a week from September to December 2027, 4 hours a week from January to May 2028.

### 6.6 University and visas (the hole most plans miss)

If you go abroad for university, **your visa may restrict you from working on your business while you're there**:
- **UK Student visa:** the conditions prohibit self-employment and business activity.
- **US F-1:** unauthorised off-campus work is prohibited. Many immigration lawyers treat actively running a business, even an Indian one, while physically in the US as a risk. Passively owning shares is generally fine.
- Other countries vary.

**Therefore:**
- (a) Hire the GM **before you leave**, by July or August 2028 if you're flying out that year.
- (b) Restructure your role to shareholder only (board meetings during vacations in India) while on a student visa.
- (c) Get one immigration-lawyer consultation for your destination country before departure. **[Lawyer]**

If you study in India, none of this applies. You can work up to 15–20 hours a week and hit Target 2 sooner.

### 6.7 Risk register with specific responses

| Risk | Trigger | Response |
|---|---|---|
| IB grades slip | Predicted total 3+ below baseline | Halve hours immediately; freeze hiring |
| School forbids paid tutoring | Reply to the step-3 email | Serve other schools and online only, or switch to Track B |
| Safeguarding incident | Any complaint | Suspend the tutor that day, inform the parents, document everything, consult a lawyer. This is why the parent-present rule, group chats and tutors aged 18+ exist. |
| Academic misconduct request ("just write my IA") | Any such request | Refuse in writing, end the engagement, keep the record |
| Tutor poaches clients | A client leaves with a tutor | Accept it. Compete on matching quality, progress reports and continuity, and keep the client relationship (parent WhatsApp) with the company, not the tutor. |
| Non-payment | Any | Prepaid packs only, so this can't happen |
| Burnout | You skip the Sunday review twice | Cut sessions to 4 hours; that week's priority is sleep |
